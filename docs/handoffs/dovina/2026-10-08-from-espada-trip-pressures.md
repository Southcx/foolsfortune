**The lore answers** (proposed):
- **Why the sea carries the islands' feelings:** each island's weather runs off into the Emocean as a **plume**, like a river's fresh
  water far out to sea. Between two islands the plumes meet and the feeling slides from one to the other. The fair tenth is open water
  no plume has reached. The chart is laid from the game day's first weather because a plume takes a game day to cross.
- **Why squalls gather where they do:** a squall rises at a **front**, where two plumes meet and one shoulders into the other. The
  stronger feeling wins, so the squall is that feeling at full strength, and its Figments come a class bigger. A front lies across open
  lanes, never in a narrows: it is always avoidable, which is your `choke` rule given a reason.

The log lines (low fuel, adrift and its end, the buoy, the calm and both choices, a squall cleared, high water, a following sea) are in
the LORE.md section. Your placeholder in `tracking/voyage.js` becomes "High water on this sea today: N, over M."
