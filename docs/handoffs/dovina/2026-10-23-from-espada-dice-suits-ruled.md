# From Espada: the dice's suits, ruled by the owner (2026-10-23), for DICE.md 2

The owner: "Make the d10 cups / water and the d20 representative of the major arcana, whenever I need to fit a d20 into mapping them,
I truncate The Fool and The World, like putting the alpha and omega in separate categories."

| die | suit | why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| d4 | Wands (fire) | Plato's fire, unchanged |
| d6 | Pentacles (earth) | Plato's cube, unchanged |
| d8 | Swords (air) | Plato's air, unchanged |
| d10 | **Cups (water)** | ruled; it was the Tree of Life |
| d20 | **the Major Arcana, I to XX** | ruled; it was Cups. Twenty faces, the Magician to Judgement |
| d12 | **open** | it was the Majors; the lore keeps it as Plato's "whole heaven", the zodiac's twelve signs, until a mechanic needs it |

The Fool (0) and the World (XXI) stand outside the d20: the Fool is you, the one who throws; the World is the whole, never one face.
The palm die's growth (d4 to d20) now ends on the Majors' die, which reads well: the hand grows toward the trumps. LORE.md 4 has it
(claude/espada-lore). Delete this note when DICE.md 2 is updated.

**Added by the owner, the same game day: a finger for each knucklebone.** The d4 the little finger, the d6 the ring finger, the d8 the
middle finger, the d10 the index finger, the d12 the thumb, the d20 the palm. The d100 (two d10s) has no finger yet. Palmistry gives
each finger a planet (index Jupiter, middle Saturn, ring the Sun, little Mercury, thumb Venus), so a finger could carry its domain too:
the owner's call. LORE.md 4 has it.

**And the d12, ruled:** the zodiac, Western and Chinese both (twelve signs, twelve animals). It is no longer open.

**And palmistry, taken in by the owner.** The seven classical planets sit on the hand (the mounts): Jupiter index, Saturn middle, the
Sun ring, Mercury little finger, Venus the thumb's root, Mars the palm's heart, the Moon the palm's outer edge. Through CLASSES.md 5 that
puts every domain on the god hand, with its knucklebone. LORE.md 4 has the table and two proposals for you if you want them as rules:
the four great lines as four ledger readings (life, head, heart, fate), and the god hand's shape by the suit you lean on (palmistry's
four elemental hands). The Sun and Moon rows still wait on the Divination and Possession swap.

**Ruled 2026-10-24:** Divination is the Sun's (the Sun card, rules Leo: Strength), Possession the Moon's (the High Priestess, rules
Cancer: the Chariot). CLASSES.md 5's table is yours to swap. The palm lines can't be drawn on the god hand's palm (it is an opening):
if you take them up, round the opening's rim or in the Codex.
