- The sea chart's rule is that an uncertain icon is blurred and faded, and only the silhouette class is crisp. A feeling's colour filled
  into that shape would read as certain, and it would carry two facts in one channel.
- So:
  - The class is the crisp ink silhouette (as planned).
  - The feeling is a halo round it: a soft glow in the feeling's canon colour (`weather.js` COLOR), textured with the feeling's own
    motif from the weather's look (grief's long streaks, dread's smoke, mirth's thrown facets, wonder's diamond motes, desire's sirocco
    sand).
  - The candidate icons stay neutral ink.
- The motif is what makes it read for colour-blind eyes. A fair waypoint has no halo.
