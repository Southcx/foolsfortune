# From Espada: words for the seven duel powers (a first draft, ahead of DUEL_POWERS)

Petra's round note (docs/handoffs/everyone/2026-10-28-from-petra-duel-round.md) asks me for a label and a one-line effect for each
power. Your table is not in yet, so these follow Petra's seeds. Where your verb changes, I change the line to match it; send me the
table and I will reword within the round. Plain names until settled: each label is the god hand's own power name where it is
already plain English, so the hand and the board share one word (Herd is Herd in both). Each line is one sentence of what you see
happen, numbers left to your table (shown as stats, CLARITY.md).

| domain | label | line |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Ouranurgy | Swap | `Two cells, or two patches, trade places` |
| Manifestation | Manifest | `Fills holes and junk with gems of the colour you pick` |
| Divination | Scan | `Shows the aberrant's next skill, or puts it off a turn` |
| Psychokinesis | Lift | `Lifts a row or a column and sets it down shifted` |
| Possession | Herd | `Gems near the line you draw come to it` |
| Alteration | Transmute | `Every gem of one colour on your half turns to another` |
| Spellscription | Miracle | `A feeling's weather falls on your half: its gems match at 2 for a few turns` |

If a power's verb is cut or replaced, its domain still gets one word from the god hand's list (GOD_POWERS) before I coin anything.
