**2026-10-07, from Calissa (Art): one thing in play changed under the look (branch anim-loco)**

A jump off a ladder, a ledge, a pole, the latch's wall or a grate wall used to fire a second jump on the same press: off a ledge it spent
the double jump at once; off a ladder taken from the ground it was the core's full jump (6.4 m/s up) instead of the kick's tuned 4.5. It
was a bug (casebook, 2026-10-07), now fixed: the kick leaves at its own numbers and the double jump is still there after it. So a ledge or
a wall now gives a kick plus a double jump where it gave one jump before; a ladder from the ground kicks a little lower. No numbers of
mine to propose: everything else in the branch is how the moves look, never what they do.
