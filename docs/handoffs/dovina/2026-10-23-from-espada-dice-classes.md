## 1. The name: keep "Pneuka stones"

The merge you record as ruled ("conceptually merge the Pneuka stones with the stones") dissolves the homonym: one thing, two names
that agree. Nothing needs qualifying. Plain names still hold on screen: the **label** is the genre's word, **dice** ("your dice", a
d20); the **lore name** is the Pneuka stones (CLARITY's label and lore split).

The triple the owner asked me to judge: **stone** is the gemstone (a set's mineral, the lapidary's virtues), the die (stones were cast
before bones were), and **stoneware**, the clay fired hard (the folk's own tier: Pip is stoneware). Pneuka is *pneuma*, breath, so
they are breath's stones in the breath's jar. That beats "knucklebones" (also a triple, but it throws away the merge and the minerals).
Keep **knucklebones** as lore, not a name: astragali were the first dice, and the palm die sits in the god hand's knuckles. That gives
the Divination line its root (astragalomancy) without spending the name.

- Plato's *Timaeus* gives exactly these: tetrahedron fire, cube earth, octahedron air, icosahedron water. The suits follow the

- **The palm die**'s growth (d4, d6, d8, d12, d20) climbs fire, earth, air, the heavens, water, ending on the roundest solid, the one

## 3. The planets: all seven rulerships are right; swap two domains

Every zodiac class sits under its classical ruler. One swap reads much stronger:

- **Divination → the Sun** (the Sun card). Helios *panoptes*, the all-seeing, who saw what was hidden (Persephone taken); Apollo, whose
  oracle at Delphi was the oracle. Divination is seeing, and the Sun sees everything. It rules Leo: Strength.
- **Possession → the Moon** (the High Priestess). *Lunatic* and *moonstruck*: in the old belief, the moon takes a mind. The High
  Priestess's veil falls over another's will. It rules Cancer, **the Chariot**: a driver holding the reins of beasts that are not
  his own, which is possession as tarot draws it. That is three meanings in one card, the owner's favourite kind.

The rest stand. Ouranurgy under Jupiter is the sky-father, and Ouranos is Jupiter's grandfather (the root reads true even though the
Uranus planet was not classical). Alteration under Saturn, the World's own planet, is time and change.
