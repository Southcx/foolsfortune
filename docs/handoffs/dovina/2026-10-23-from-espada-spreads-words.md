## 1. The positions: two clash with the glossary

All of tarot's names read right in the game's voice (Past, Present, Future, Hidden influences, Obstacles, Others, Advice, Outcome,
Foundation, Recent past, Near future, Self, Environment, Hopes and fears) except two:

- **"Crossing" → "Challenge."** "The crossing" is the rail's stage in the glossary. Tarot books call this position the Challenge as
  often as the Crossing ("what crosses you"), so it is still tarot's word. It is laid across the Present just the same.
- **"Crown": keep it, qualified.** "The crown" is the Great Slip Jelly's urn. Say "the Crown position" in docs and code
  (`crownPosition`); on the board the label alone is fine, since it sits in a spread. It goes in the homonym table when built.

## 2. The suits by kind: one move, one addition

The playing-card suits come from these, a footnote worth keeping: hearts from Cups, spades from Swords, clubs from Wands, diamonds
from Pentacles. Your table holds up.

- **shell → Wands** (from Swords). By your own rule, Wands is "what is fired", and a shell is a round fired from the psygun.
- **tool** (a kind in `items.js`, missing from the table): suit by the tool's nature:
  - psygun: Wands (fire);
  - Crucibelle: Wands (struck);
  - Sondelass: Swords (the blade);
  - Veritome: Swords (the eye that sees through);
  - Soul Brush: Cups (liquid Lachryma);
  - Lockheart: Cups (a heart; a coffin holds);
  - Dreamvane: Pentacles (the pick and the dowse: earth).
- Everything else stands: crude in Wands (it burns, and it carries no feeling, so not Cups), heart in Cups (hearts are Cups by
  descent), whistle in Swords (air), rutter in Swords (a plan), map in Pentacles (ground charted).

## 4. The wild card's line

**Why a planet lights every suit:** "planet" is Greek *planētēs*, the wanderer. The ancients named the planets for how they move
through every sign of the sky in turn. So a planet's card belongs to no one suit: it visits them all, and lights each a little less
brightly than a card that lives there (your 0.75).

For the board's tip, plain: **"A planet: it wanders through every sign, so it lights every suit, a little less brightly."**
