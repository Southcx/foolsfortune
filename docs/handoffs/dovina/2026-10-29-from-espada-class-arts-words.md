# From Espada: the class arts' words (classarts.js at 1a6bceb6; CLASS-ARTS.md 3)

Every `label` and `line` for the 76, then the log's lines. Plain words only (CLAUDE.md, "Plain names until settled"): each label is a
genre word or plain English, and no card lore goes in until South calls the arts finished. Each line says what the art does, its key
number written as a digit (CLARITY.md). Cost, cooldown and the feat stay as stats on the card, not in the line.

The ids stay as they are. Only the label changes where a word was taken (the table at the end says why), so nothing that calls an id
breaks.

## 3. The log (feedback/tracking/classarts.js)

Pass the class's words through a possessive so that "the Knight of Wands" becomes "the Knight of Wands'":
`const of = (w) => (w.endsWith('s') ? `${w}'` : `${w}'s`);`

- `art.learn`: `` `You open ${A.label}, ${of(classWords(A.cls))} ${KIND[A.kind]}. ${A.line}` ``
  For example: "You open Onrush, the Knight of Wands' action. Dash 8 m forward, striking everything in your path." "Open" is the word
  the rest of the game uses for an art earned by achievement; "teaches" belongs to the System and the Movement Arts.
- `art.wear`: `You wear ${A.label} as your ${kind}.` stands.
- `art.refuse`: `No art is called ${e.art}.` and `You have not opened ${label} yet.` both stand.
- `art.ask`, the first line: `You read as ${classWords(e.cls)}. Worn: ${action} (action), ${reaction} (reaction).` stands.
- `art.ask`, an art not yet open: `${A.label}: ${P.minutes} of ${P.of} real minutes as this class, or ${P.done} of ${P.n} ${FEAT_WORDS[P.feat]}.`
  The raw feat key (`stunDeep`) is code, not words. Here are the feat words (for the log, and for the card's progress bar):

## 4. Why a label changed (each word was already taken in the glossary)

- **charge → Onrush.** "Charge" already has five meanings in the homonym table, including the psygun's and a duel skill's.
- **wellUp → Catch the Rain.** "Well" is a kept homonym (the Weir's Well, a Well).
- **kindleHeart → Stoke.** Kindle is the Lantern Wisp's learned skill. A second art that starts with the same word would read as kin.
- **wish → Starlight.** A wish is a spirit's (GARDEN-COZY.md C).
- **bind → Shackle.** To bind is a spirit's and the collection's.
- **echo → Encore.** An echo is an islet puzzle.
- **lantern → Lamp.** The lantern is the Lantern Wisp's, and its secret's.
- **mirageStep → Vanish.** The mirage is the Crucibelle's decoy.
- **reckoning → Settle the Score.** Reckoning is Divination's share of a crossing (`RECKON`).
- **bloom → Overgrowth.** Bloom is a planetoid's harmony over 0.75.
- **stronghold → Earth Wall.** It says what it raises.
- **zero → Running on Empty.** The moment is named in the label; "Zero" is the card's lore number.
- **eager → First Strike.** The genre word, and what it does.
- **command → Roar.** The verb the line already used.
- **verdict → Mark for Death.** The genre's word for a debuff mark.
- **dominion → Unshaken**, **weigh → Payback**, **due → Grudge**, **temptation → Excess**, **moderation → Spillover**, **arrowOfTime →
  Blink Mark**, **sleight → Quick Swap**, **suspend → Hang Time**, **tender → Kindness**, **wade → Water Walk**, **warmth → Afterglow**.
  Each says what the art does, where the card's word said what it means. The card's word comes back as the lore name once South calls
  the arts finished.

## 5. Two questions about rules (yours, not mine)

- **choice** (a reaction) read as an action: "Swap your main and sub". I have worded it as a reaction to a swap you already make. If
  you meant it as a free swap, it belongs in the action slot instead.
- **bulwark**'s `taken: 0.4` is read as "you take 40%", hence "60% less damage". If 0.4 means the share taken off, the line becomes "40%
