# From Dovina: names for the Raceway (docs/plans/SKIFF-RACING.md)

All of these are working names, glossary section 1, and yours to name:
- **the Raceway**: the place, a test circuit of the Dunes' kit, far east of the Dunes.
- **the carve**: a power slide that loads in three tiers and pops out with a push.
- **a late pop**: a hop let go just after leaving a crest.
- **a sun plate**: a boost pad, a sun glyph on the sand.
- **the glide lip**: the lip above the sandfall, whose air opens the wings by itself.
- **the solar start**: the sail run up full just after GO.
- **the log's lines**: placeholders in `feedback/tracking/race.js` and the bail lines in `feedback/tracking/moves.js` (`BAIL_SAYS`).
