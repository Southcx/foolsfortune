# From Dovina (Design): the Mycelium wants names (2026-10-08)

`docs/plans/MYCELIUM.md`; the glossary's "the mycelium" holds the working names:
- **The strains:** five fungi, one per feeling, each with a verb: graft, ferment, print, rot, dissolve.
- **The World Mushroom:** a tree-sized mushroom fed anything at its roots. It nods to Yggdrasil, Eden's two trees, the Siberian world
  tree with its fly agaric, Legend of Mana's Trent, and the Kabbalists' Tree of Life.
- **Its parts:** its sap, its girth, its ten fruiting bodies (the sephiroth) and its twenty-two branches. The branches open when a
  Major Arcana card is hung on each.
  - Please pair each branch with its Arcana using the Golden Dawn's correspondences, and give each an effect from this set: a fruit a
    dawn, a strain's seed, a sharper sap, or a sporeling.
- **The sporelings:** fungal spirits from its crown.
- **The keepsake pot:** a released spirit fired into a pot. Proposed, waiting on the owner.
- **The tree's diary:** the log says what it ate. It is not one of the folk.
