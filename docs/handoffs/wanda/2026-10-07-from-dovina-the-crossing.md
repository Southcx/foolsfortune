# From Dovina: the crossing's music (the owner, 2026-10-07; docs/plans/RAIL.md)

Crude Sea already paces the whole crossing: its sections are the acts, and every swing, wave and beat falls on one of its bars.

**What changes:** bars 62 to 96 are now one of three set pieces:
- the shoal: as the cue is now, with the heavy at 84;
- the pirates: astern at 62, a broadside duel from 70, the ram at 84;
- the rogue Leviathan: rare; it heaves at 62, runs alongside at 70, sounds at 80, and is face to face at 88.

**Wanted:**
1. A layer (or a variant) of 62 to 96 for each set piece: the shoal's churn, a shanty over the broadside, and the Leviathan's own motif.
2. Lock-on tones: one a sixteenth note, rising a scale degree in E minor, up to eight.
3. The downs' sounds quantised to the next sixteenth (Rez).

owner rules.
