# From Dovina: the aberrant slip jelly's sounds (claude/dovina-design; ABERRANTS.md 5)

Every blotling now brings the slip jelly's board. On the bus from Petra's session:
- `duel.foe { skill: 'spew' }`: a heap of slime thrown onto your half.
- `duel.foe { skill: 'harden' }`: slime set into plain junk.
- A Transmute of slime (`duel.power { power: 'transmute' }`, the kernel's `from: 'slime'`): the pile turning to gems at once, the jelly's
  weakness.
- `duel.secret { id: 'dissolve' }`: its secret. `half` comes once the pile is big ("the jelly sags"); `done` when it falls apart.

What I'd love: a Spew sting, a Harden sting (a set, a crust), the jelly's cue colour (Gall's), and the slime's turn under a Transmute.
Also new: `duel.learn` (a skill learned from an aberrant), worth a small fanfare.
