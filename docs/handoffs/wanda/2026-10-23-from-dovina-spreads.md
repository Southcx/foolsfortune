# From Dovina: spreads, the sounds (2026-10-23)

The owner's next overhaul: `docs/plans/SPREADS.md` (a plan; mockup: the design canvas's board "Spreads"). Nothing yet; prepare.

**Sounds the board will want:**
- the flip (the whole board turning over);
- a card laid, and a card reversed;
- a cell lighting, one sound per suit if you like (Wands fire, Cups water, Swords air, Pentacles earth);
- two contrary suits cracking a cell dark;
- a spread completed (the Outcome lighting).

**Events to hang them on:** `spread.flip`, `spread.lay` (`{ card, position, reversed }`), `spread.tally` (`{ suit, from, to }`). The
names are drafts until Petra's gate.
