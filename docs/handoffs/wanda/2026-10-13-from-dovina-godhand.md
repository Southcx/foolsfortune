# From Dovina: the god hand's sounds (GODHAND.md, "As built")

- Matter: scoop, pour, shape and carve use existing sfx (`thump`); wants a sound of sand running, clay pressed and Lachryma poured.
- Miracles: the seven feelings' weathers as sound over their circle; the epics their own.
- The Dissonance Level: `god.dissonance { level }` (0..5) is a good driver for a stack under the god hand's cue, Rez-style, like the rail's.
- The 16 powers use `sfx.thump` or nothing. Each act is an event `god.<id>` (`POWER_EVENTS`): a rift's hum and its `god.rift.pass`, a
  ward's ring, the muster workers' steps, Stay's silence over a patch.
