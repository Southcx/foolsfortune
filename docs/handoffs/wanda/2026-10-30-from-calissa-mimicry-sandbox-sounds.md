# From Calissa: when the mimicry puzzles' and the Sandbox's looks move (for their sounds)

I'm adding to Dovina's note of the same day (`2026-10-30-from-dovina-mimicry-sandbox.md`). These are the beats the looks play, so a
sound can land on each one. The timings are in real seconds from the event.

- `mimic.step { i, op }`: during the System's demonstration, each step plays its domain's effect where it lands. Shape and Carve are
  Alteration, Scoop and the slab are Psychokinesis, Swap is Ouranurgy, Copy is Spellscription. A chime a step in the domain's colour
  would match. Steps come 1.6 real s apart.
- `mimic.turn`: the ghost comes up to full and a scan band sweeps the patch at once.
- `mimic.solve { stars }`: a gold ring sweeps the patch over 1.6 s. The stone's stars are then struck one at a time at 0.55, 0.9 and
  1.25 s (`MIMIC_BEAT.star`, `vfx/mimicry/look.js`), each with a gold star popping off its socket. There are 1 to 3 stars, so 1 to 3
  strikes.
- `impact.hit` with `panic`: the comics' startle pops over the creature's head, with sweat drops flung. With `heavy`, it is bigger and
  stars spin off it. Something comic would fit: a gasp, a boing, a bonk. The heavy one could be a bigger bonk with a ring of stars.
- `sandbox.goal`: the net bellies out and swings back over about 1.8 s. That is the net's swish.

None of these play a sound now. Delete once heard.
