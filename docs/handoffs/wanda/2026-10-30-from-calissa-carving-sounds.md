# From Calissa: carving a slab and scanning a code, their beats for your sounds (2026-10-30)

Dovina's plan asks you for the carving's sounds by verb and stratum, a crack, the flawless ring and the scan's chirp
(docs/plans/SEEDS-SLABS-SCANS.md 4). Here is what each moment looks like and when, so the sound lands on the picture. No audio code of mine.

## Carving a slab: `slab.use { verb, broke, cracked, stratum }`

`stratum` is the stone struck: 1 the clay on top (soft, damp), 2 the sand (packed), 3 the sandstone (hard, ringing), 0 the fossil itself.
`broke` is how many cells cleared; `cracked` how many fossil cells it cracked.
- **brush** (held, a use each 0.35 real s): dust swept off in a slow fan, a few grains. Soft, never sharp.
- **pick** (each 0.5 s): the impact at 0, chips thrown hard for 0.5 s, a gold glint off the pick.
- **blast** (each 0.8 s): a burst at 0, a gold ring blown flat across the face over 0.4 s, chips and dust for 1 s.
- **slice** (each 1.5 s): a bright line drawn along the whole layer at once (about 0.3 s), flakes peeling off it.
- **hoover** (each 0.6 s): the rubble drawn in to the cell over 0.4 s and gone.
- **toll** (each 1.2 s, three a slab): three gold rings run out through the stone at 0, 0.45 and 0.9 s, and the fossil's outline rings
  through the stone with them for 1.5 s. The Crucibelle's bell, three strikes.
- **silhouette** (each 1 s): the plate pressed to the slab's face, a flash of the film over 0.5 s (a shutter, or a plate laid down).
- **a crack** (`cracked` > 0): a star in the film at the fossil at once, dark crystal shards. Glass under strain.

## The carving's end: `slab.carve { flawless, score }`

- 0 to 0.9 s: the fossil lifts off its bed, glowing brighter (a rising crystal hum).
- 0.95 s: the stone left falls away in chips and dust.
- 1.3 s: the spirit wakes out of the fossil in a burst of its feeling (the flawless ring here when `flawless`, with a ring of white and
  gold stars round it: the shiny's).
- 1.3 to 3.3 s: the spirit rises and goes off to the Grove.
A flawless dig's spirit then glints in the Grove every 3.5 to 7 s (two white and gold stars, no event: a faint twinkle if you like).

## Scanning a code: `scan.read { what, kind, cls, sealed, again, own, how }`

- 0 to 0.5 s: the code's bars drawn in from the left.
- 0.35 to 1.15 s: a red laser line sweeps across them (the scan's chirp).
- 1.15 to 1.75 s: the bars drop and gather into what it held (a block of stone, a cut stone, cubes).
- 1.85 s: the words come up, and on a sealed slab (`sealed`) SEALED is stamped in vermilion, landing hard (a hanko's thud).
- `again`: cubes; `own` (a made track's code): about 2.2 s on, the code's card opens.
