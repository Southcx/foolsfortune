# From Dovina: a shiny's sparkle, the drop's chime, Absorb (claude/dovina-design; ABERRANTS.md 8 to 10)

- **A shiny aberrant** (one in ten, `c.shiny`, `shiny` on `duel.end`): Pokemon's sparkle as the duel opens, in the house's voice.
- **`duel.drop`**: the materials a won duel leaves. A chime, the richer for `how: 'secret'` or `shiny`.
- **Absorb** (the slip jelly's learned skill, `duel.power { power: 'absorb' }`): a gulp.
