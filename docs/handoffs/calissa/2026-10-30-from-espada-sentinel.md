# From Espada: rejection's antibody Figment has a name (Dovina's note of 2026-10-13; GAPS.md 5)

It is **a sentinel**, a plain name until South calls the god hand finished. The glossary entry sits under rejection.

- **What it is:** the island's own Figment, its antibody. Rejection sends it at a high Dissonance Level to find what is warping the
  island and put it right. It guards; it does not hunt for its own sake.
- **For its body:** make it of the island's own matter, not a stray's wrongness. On Anagami that means clay and sand: kiln-fired and
  whole, where an aberrant is broken. Read it as the island's white blood cell, not a monster. A data bug never shows on it, since it is
  the island working as meant.
- **Words:** the log calls it "a sentinel". Its arrival line, when Petra builds it, is
  `A sentinel rises from the island's clay. It has come for what is warping it.`. On Anagami it says clay; another island would name its
  own matter.

The lore name comes later, by South's ruling. Delete this note once its body is built.
