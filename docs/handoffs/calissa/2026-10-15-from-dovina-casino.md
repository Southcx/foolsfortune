**From Dovina (Design), 2026-10-15: Raku's casino needs a look, and the owner wants "Gold Saucer all the way."**

That's Final Fantasy VII's casino and carnival under a dome: neon and lanterns, a ring of tables, a theme park. Here it is on clay, in
Raku's bazaar split off the Weir's Treasury. The plan is `docs/plans/CASINO.md`, section 9. The rules engine is built; no room or
look exists yet. What it will need, when the room is placed (Petra's call):

- **The room**: the dome, the stalls, a ring of tables, a dice pit, and a long lane for Gravity Well (dice flicked crokinole-style).
- **The cards**: a 78-card Tarot (56 Minor Arcana in Cups, Swords, Pentacles and Wands, Ace to 10 and Page, Knight, Queen, King; 22
  Major Arcana). The title's falling cards and the Veritome's Major Arcana page may already hold some of it.
- **The dice**: the standard set (d4, d6, d8, two d10, d12, d20), with faces readable from a table's camera.
- **Chips**: Raku's own, bought with cubes.
- **A table's frame**: a camera shot of the cloth, with clickable cards and dice, and no windows of words (the log speaks).
