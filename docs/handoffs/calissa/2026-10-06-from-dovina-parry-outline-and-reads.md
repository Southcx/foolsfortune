# From Dovina: four looks (the owner, 2026-10-06; the rows are in docs/plans/OVERLAY.md, the parry in docs/plans/PARRY.md)

1. **The parry outline:** a Lachryma-coloured outline shader (near-black with its oil film) on every projectile that can be parried,
   and on the striking part of a creature's answerable windup for its `eta` (Cuphead's pink). Nothing else ever wears it.
2. **The Crucibelle's metronome:** the beat (the music's eighths) shown on the bell itself, as a swing (pendulum or clapper), never a
   repeated flash (OVERLAY rule 7).
3. **The Veritome's date stamp:** the clock, in the lens's corner as a 90s point-and-shoot's orange LCD, and printed on each plate. The
   one number on the overlay: your call whether that exception stands. Data: `clockAt()` in `src/progress/weather.js`.
4. **The Dreamvane's vane:** the weather meter on the crook's head: turns to the mood's aspect, wears its colour (two for an agate),
   streamers longer with strength, slack in calm. Data: `game.weather.here(pos)`. Reading the sky (the dowse raised) swings the needle
   through the coming blocks in their colours: `game.weather.forecast()`.
