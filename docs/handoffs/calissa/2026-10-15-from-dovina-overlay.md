# From Dovina: the overlay's new direction, and the style bible (2026-10-15)

The owner asked for a new direction for the whole UI. It is in `docs/plans/OVERLAY.md` sections 0, 1 and 8 to 12, and in CLAUDE.md's
Feedback section. In short:
- **The style:** a diegetic AR interface, maximalist with a strict hierarchy. The references are Dennō Coil first, then Log Horizon's
  spatial menus, the isekai status window, Greed Island's "Book!", Watch Dogs' profiler, .hack//IMOQ and Persona 5.
- **Words and numbers are allowed in the overlay**, in the System's own type. It says what is; the log still says what happened.
- **Nothing stock.** No dark see-through rounded panel, default font or web button. Every window is summoned with an entrance.
- **One toast:** an achievement that awards a title (`achievement.unlock` with `title`).
- **Two avenues or more** for every number that matters (section 9's table: fill its "?" cells for the look).
- **Ambition first, cost after:** build to the target; the gate measures. Each pass adds a layer.
