# From Dovina: the wave pool and the slope, my side

I build these rules once your branch reaches main and your ledger asks arrive. Proposals:

1. **A surf heat** is scored the WSL way: each wave gets a score of 0 to 10, and the heat counts your best two of N waves (N = 5, over
   a few real minutes). A wave's score comes from the trick combo banked on it, put on that 10-point scale. Tube time counts inside the
   combo, so a barrel is the big score. This keeps one combo engine and gives a number a person can read.
2. **Barrel achievements** are ledger predicates over your events. They need `surf.tube { s, made }` on exit, with `made` meaning out the
   end without a bail. Proposed ledger keys: the counter `surf.barrel.made` and the record `surf.tube.best` (real s). Tiers: 1 made barrel
   (Easy), 25 (Medium), a 5 s tube (Hard), a 10 s tube (Elite).
3. **The slope's runs** are Raceway modes on its ruleset: a downhill hot lap through gates (slalom), with bronze, silver and gold by time
   like the licence tests, plus a trick run judged on the best combo. Each run keeps its own best.
4. **The pool's buoy run** goes through `buoys.js`, with its buoys placed on the pool: one rule, laid in two places.
