# Mechs: the look (from Dovina, 2026-11-02)

South asked for giant robots the Pneuka Jar pilots: "Neon Genesis Evangelion mixed with armored core, mixed with the giant robot
sections from Ratchet and clank ... don't hold back on the design, loop in Calissa to get building". The plan is
`docs/plans/MECHS.md`; the rules are `src/progress/mechs/`; the hangar and a pilotable placeholder (boxes) are on claude/dovina-design
under `src/world/hangar/`, all placeholders for you.

**South's ruling with it:** what rides is the Pneuka Jar in a cradle or a saddle; the Courier rides only the Solar Skiff. That is yours
for the slip jelly mount too: a saddle on its crown that fits the Jar's foot ring (Petra swaps the rider).

**What to draw** (MECHS.md 8.2), your order:
1. **The clay frame** at about 14 m: a haniwa warrior (the island's own idiom), glazed armour plates over a clay body, the cradle in the
   chest with a **screw thread** and its gold collar (the seal turned home as a thread, two turns: South's "screw into"). The Jar seen
   through the chest's port while sealed.
2. **A look per part**, so a frame reads as its assembly (AC's): heads, cores (kiln core fired darker, light core bisque-thin), arms,
   the four leg types (biped, reverse joint, tetrapod, treads), boosters, generators, and the seven tools at giant scale (the big gun,
   the great blade, the great brush, the great bell, the great flail; the great vane and lens on the back).
3. **The straw giant**: Pip's biggest try at Strawman, 16 m, stitched on a scaffold, its barrier a quilted sheet; staggered, it sags.
4. **The barrier**: a giant's as a shimmer of hexes (Evangelion's AT Field, in the film's colours), and yours pressing into it, the two
   fields meeting and tearing open (the neutralising).
5. **The readout** while sealed (a plug-suit HUD in the Clay dress): sync as a percent, the battery with its seconds left, CABLE / OFF
   CABLE, the six regions' cracks, the giant's barrier and stagger. The placeholder is `src/world/hangar/readout.js` (classes `mk-*`).
6. **The hangar and the proving ground**: the gantry and its clamps, the launch's lift and shaft (Evangelion's launch), the yard at the
   mech's scale (Giant Clank's toy city: clay buildings at the mech's knee), the cable sockets.
7. **The assembly page** (classes `mk-*`): Armored Core's garage in the Clay dress, the slot row and the parts as choice cards.
