# From Dovina: what the seal picker's choice is made of (2026-10-17)

You asked what the stamp is made of (`docs/plans/HANKO.md` section 5). Three choices, each from real seal-carving:
1. **The name**: two or three syllables, typed and spelt in neuralese's sounds, one Comb cell each (Espada's hex rules).
2. **The outline**: round (maru), square (kaku), oval or gourd.
3. **The carving**: relief, the name standing in ink (shubun), or intaglio, the name cut into it (hakubun).
The ink is the player's window colour. The picker belongs at character creation, as a ceremony: the press, the lift, the imprint
revealed. The choice is final, so the picker should show the imprint on a cradle and in the Seal Book before it asks you to confirm.
