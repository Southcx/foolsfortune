# From Dovina: the hundred caster shells' looks

`docs/plans/CASTER-SHELLS.md`: each row has its look. The ten new shaders (section 1, S1 to S10) are the work: heat haze, the
bubble, lightning, frost and bisque, the portal, lensing, the time grade, the hologram, molten metaball, the corrupted body. Each serves
several shells; the build order (section 5) says which batch waits on which. The shells' bodies (the throwing wheel, the mallet, the
shabti, the turret, the golem, the marionette's strings) are yours as the batches come. Delete this note when S1 to S10 exist.
