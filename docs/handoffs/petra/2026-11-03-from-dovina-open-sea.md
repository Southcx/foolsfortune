# From Dovina (design) to Petra: the open sea's four folds (2026-11-03)

South: "build any outstanding unbuilt features". Built: fragments, capture, a song for the wind, tall tales (OPEN-SEA.md 15.7, GAPS G158 to G161), and the islands' other wants (G286). The rules and the room's hooks are done in Dovina's files (`progress/opensea/`, `world/opensea/seafolds.js` and a few lines in `opensea.js`). Two hooks are in your files. Information, not an order.

1. **The harpoon's pull (G159, `world/opensea/seamounts.js` / `seafight.js`).** Capture needs a tow verb. When a beast is tethered by the harpoon (`c.tether > 0`) and the harpoon is fired again at it, or held, emit:
   `game.events.emit('seabeast.tow', { id, hp, depth, tethered: true, by: 'courier' })`
   with `id` the SEA_BEASTS id (`seaSerpent`, `wolfWhale`, `seaMonk`; others are refused and said), `hp` its hit points left as a share 0..1 and `depth` its stun depth (`stun.depth`, 1..3). `world/opensea/seafolds.js` decides (capture.js `towOk`: hp at most 0.3, depth at least 2), slows the helm (`helm.mods 'tow'`) and emits `seabeast.towing { id }`. On `seabeast.towing` the beast should leave the fight (follow the ship slack on its tether, no attacks) and be taken off its lair for the game day as a sinking does (the `seabeast.down { lair }` handler); on `seabeast.lost { id, why }` it is free again at about half hp and hostile; on `seabeast.captured { id, how }` it is gone for the day. Until then DEBUG's `/tow <beast>` stands in. A hull hit (`sea.hull`) parts the tether, so a beast in tow that is still being hit by something else will be lost: that is the intended risk.
2. **The Crucibelle's notes at the helm (G160).** The song listens for `crucibelle.note { degree 1..5, by: 'courier' }` while the free helm is sailing. The Crucibelle is a mount at sea (the toll, `SEA_MOUNTS`) and its note pads are not at the helm, so there is no way to play three notes on touch or in the mount's arc. Needed: the five note pads (the touch strip's tiles `touch.cru1`..) shown at the helm, emitting that event. Keyboard `/windsong east|west|north|south` works now.
