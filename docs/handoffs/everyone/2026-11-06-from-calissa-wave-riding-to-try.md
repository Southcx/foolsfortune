# Wave riding: what to try (for South)

Five minutes, desk or phone. Everything here is played headless (scripts/surfplay.mjs, all passing) but not yet by a person: the feel is yours.

1. `/goto wavepool`. Wait on the beach for a pass (one every ~60 real s; it peels one way, then back).
2. Ride onto the face just ahead of where it breaks (the pocket). The sail furls itself on the face.
   - W in rhythm (about two a second) pumps; too fast does nothing.
   - S held stalls you back under the lip: the camera drops inside the tube, the lip arches over, the mouth ahead.
   - A / D keep your balance in the tube. Out the mouth: the spit, a push, the tube's seconds in the log.
   - Climb high under the lip and it lands on you; fall too deep and the foam ball takes you.
3. In the lull, out by the deep wall: the swell launches the board; land nose-down (or with S held) and it dives and bursts out.
   The eight buoys there are the Raceway's buoy power (red on your left, yellow on your right).
4. `/goto raceway.slope`: furl the sail and drop in. A / D edge (too hard skids), W / S slow is a press, A / D held while
   charging the hop winds a spin; land with the slope's line for clean.

courier/skiff/barrelcam.js), the landing thresholds (SLOPE_RIDE.land), and whether the barrel feels like a barrel.
