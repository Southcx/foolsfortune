# The mech night: what to try (Calissa, for South on waking)

Everything below is on `claude/calissa-art-cups` (Petra gates it to the published build). Go to THE HAND at the Index, then the hangar.

1. **The hangar.** Walk in from the door and look up: a kiln-cathedral, the gantry a torii beam with a shimenawa over your frame,
   bamboo scaffold, a climbing kiln up the east wall, blue light down the lift shaft. Seal in at the walkway (F): the lanterns flare
   gold on each turn of the screw and the kilns burn brighter while you are in.
2. **Your frame's looks.** `/mechstyle form hero|doll`, `/mechstyle hip crinoline|tassets|kusazuri|pteruges`, `/mechstyle cape
   renko|kohai|kazebukuro|tenne|knight`, `/helm <id>` (38 of them; the Arcana's 22 among them). Seal in with the renko or kohai on: the
   drum roll and the flames come up to full once the Jar is in.
3. **The proving ground.** The yard is paved with range rings, an ishigaki wall round it, cable pylons and a launch pad. The straw
   giant is Pip's biggest try now (burlap on a scaffold, X eyes, a target); its barrier is an AT field: hit it and watch the octagons,
   press your barrier on it (V held, close) and it tears open.
4. **The specials** (E head, Q arms, R legs, Y core; on the phone, PICK): each has its look now (Scan's iris and mark, Barrier's hex
   bubble, Hasten's hoops and after-images, Sunder's clay blade, Gust, Quake, Portal, Telekinesis's tether).
5. **The readout** while sealed (bottom right; on the phone in the bottom band, clear of the arc): the sync dial, the internal battery
   counting down in Nixie tubes, the cable, a damage doll of your frame, the giant's barrier and stagger. Get low on battery or
   berserk to see it change. The gantry's page (the lectern) is dressed as a garage with load and energy gauges.
6. **The camera close to a building**: your frame dithers out so you can see.
7. **Dovina's smash challenges and the colossus** (her note has the how): the lane under a vermilion torii whose lamps count you in,
   kokeshi bowling pins that tip with a STRIKE tag, a painted target, medal confetti; the dune colossus as a sand-shedding haniwa with
   Lachrymite in its back, its burrow, clod and wave marked; hold a giant overhead and the frame's joints glow ember, slam it for dust.
8. **The Pneuka Jar's saddle** on the slip jelly mount (Y in the Dunes): a lacquered kura with tassels.

**Not right yet:** the yard is lit by the clock's noon sun under a dusk sky (asked Petra for a zone light); hip pieces on the tetrapod
and treads hang as a farthingale and may still look odd; the knight cape reads heavy brown more than cloth; PRESS's, UNIT's and
CABLE's tile glyphs are hard to read at 48 px; the colossus is still cylinders from the side, the sand wave reads poorly in a still, effects small from 40 m.
