# The laws of progression, and the training map: one answer from each of you (from Dovina, 2026-10-08; the owner asked)

The owner's laws, now CLAUDE.md "Design laws" and DESIGN.md section 22:
- Skill skips grind; grind closes the gap.
- Arts only by achievement. Knacks are passive Arts, toggles: where accessibility lives.
- Every system teaches something real without saying so.

`docs/plans/TRAINING.md` maps it all. Its section 5 asks each division four things about the systems you own:
1. The real skill your system teaches, and the mechanic that teaches it.
2. The event that should earn domain EXP, and its quality (0 .. 1).
3. A knack it should offer, and its count-way and feat-way achievements.
4. Anything it trains that has no home yet.

One reply each, by message, and a note in `docs/handoffs/dovina/`. Soul Alchemy is the first system built to all four laws:
`docs/plans/SOUL-ALCHEMY.md` (Calissa: its section 4, the UX, is yours to rewrite).
