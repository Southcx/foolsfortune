# Progress

You grow by doing things, never by spending points. The System watches what you do, keeps the count, and gives you new moves, small
helps and titles when the count says you have earned them. Playing well gets you there sooner. Playing a lot gets you there too.

## The two rules behind everything

1. **Skill skips grind; grind closes the gap.** Doing a thing well pays more. Doing it at all still pays. A skilled player gets there
   early. A patient one gets there.
2. **Arts only by achievement.** A Movement Art, a God Art or a knack opens when an achievement is done, and by nothing else. Each art
   worth having should have two ways: a count way (the patient's) and a feat way (the skilled player's).

Every kept count is in **the ledger**, and everything else reads it. Nothing is a flag set by hand, so a new achievement completes at
once for anyone who already did the thing.

## The ledger

The ledger (open the Codex with **B**) keeps three kinds of entry.

| Kind | What it is | Example |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Counter | a number that only goes up | pots broken, parries, slams |
| Record | your best (highest or lowest), with when it was set | longest chain, fastest circuit |
| First | the play time something first happened | the first fish of a kind |

It is saved with you. **A new build wipes it** (progress, unlocks, Codex and achievements). Your settings are kept.

## Movement Arts

A Movement Art is a move the System teaches. Walking, sprinting, sliding, jumping, wall-running, mantling, dashing and the body moves
(swimming, ladders, hanging, poles, grates, balance, carrying, pushing) are always yours, and no art changes them.

Each art opens when you reach its goal. Many arts also have a **variant**, a stronger or different version, with a goal of its own.
The variant opens once you own the art.

| Art | Key | Opens when you | Variant (opens when you) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Blink | E | air dash 20 times | Rush Blink: blink 30 times. Flicker: blink twice in a row, 8 times |
| Slam | C in the air, looking down | drop 12 m or more, 3 times | Quake Slam: slam 40 times. Super Slam: slam onto a moving target from 50 m up |
| Roll | Shift while crouched | drop 20 m or more, 3 times | Tumble: roll 15 times |
| Stomp | land on a pot | break 25 pots | Spring Stomp: stomp three times in a row |
| Slip Dive | C on slip | make 6 slip splats | Tide: 90 real seconds under the slip |
| Wall Latch | C in the air by a wall | hang from ledges and bars 6 times | Iron Grip: latch to walls 15 times |
| Kick | V | break 15 pots | Counter: parry 4 times |
| Recoil Jump | fire down in mid-air | fire 15 shots in mid-air | Boost: 25 recoil jumps |

The god hand has its own arts, learned the same way: Telekinesis (known from the start), Sunder, Swell, Wring and Manifest.

Most goals are a count today. Super Slam is a feat. The "two ways" rule is the aim, and it is not yet built for every art.

## Knacks

A knack is a passive art you can switch on or off, and it is where every assist lives. It opens when you practise, then stays **on**
until you turn it off. Turning it off puts the game back exactly. Using one never changes what you are credited for.

Type **/knack** in the chat line to list yours, or **/knack Name on** or **off** (the name is the id in the table).

| Knack (id) | What it does | Opens at (either way) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Steady Hand (`steadyHand`) | pulls psygun aim toward a target within 3 degrees | 2,000 drill hits, or gold in a drill |
| Wide Bore (`wideBore`) | psygun shots a third wider | 5,000 drill hits, or a chain of 25 |
| Thick Walls (`thickWalls`) | shield takes a tenth more first | 200 cracks mended, or a Well run with no crack |
| Perfect Pitch (`perfectPitch`) | sounds a crystal's target note once more | 300 crystals struck, or 10 perfect in a row |
| Held Breath (`heldBreath`) | parry window 0.25 to 0.40 real seconds | 500 parries, or 25 clean in a row |
| Rule of Thirds (`ruleOfThirds`) | thirds lines in the Veritome's frame | 300 plates appraised, or 4 four-star kinds |
| Half Time (`halfTime`) | pendulum swings every other beat | 1,000 on-beat bell hits, or 5 fever songs |
| Guide Tone (`guideTone`) | next note sounds a beat early | 50 rhythm songs, or accuracy 95 |
| Wet Ink (`wetInk`) | Celestial mode waits 0.42 to 0.7 real seconds | 100 marks misread, or 4 sigils cleared |
| Ariadne's Thread (`ariadnesThread`) | the map shows the way back to your last Shrine | 50 rooms charted, or a Cogitomap on a first run |
| Crib Sheet (`crib`) | English beside each neuralese word you have glossed | 100 macros, a held five-Function macro, or 6 ostraca found |

Calibration, key bindings and volume are settings, never knacks: what makes the game playable is never earned.

## Achievements

An achievement is a check over the ledger. Each has a **tier**, a **type**, and sometimes a **title**.

| Tier | Points |
| --- | --- |
| Easy | 1 |
| Medium | 2 |
| Hard | 3 |
| Elite | 4 |
| Master | 5 |
| Grandmaster | 6 |

Types tell you what is being asked: **Count** (do it n times), **Speed** (beat a time), **Perfection** (nothing missed), **Mechanic**
(do the hard thing once), **Stamina** (keep going) and **Collection** (see all of a set). Some are hidden until done. The Codex shows each
as n of N before it is done.

Your points add up to a **standing**. The log says it when you move up.

| Points | Standing |
| --- | --- |
| 0 | Sweeper |
| 10 | Apprentice |
| 30 | Journeyman |
| 70 | Potter |
| 130 | Master Potter |
| 220 | Kiln Warden |
| 340 | Fool's Fortune |

Some achievements give a title (Shatterer, Landslide, Alchemist, Adept, The World). Titles are the reward, and nothing else follows from them.
Every art and variant is also an achievement, so learning one counts toward your standing.

## The domains

The domains are the seven skills of the Courier's psyche. There will never be more.

| Domain | Does |
| --- | --- |
| Ouranurgy | the rules of the space around you: displacement, time slowed or stopped |
| Manifestation | solidifying thought into structures |
| Divination | perceiving the hidden: signatures, habits, the true pitch |
| Psychokinesis | moving things by will |
| Possession | exerting will over other minds |
| Alteration | reshaping what already is |
| Spellscription | transcribing a thing down: glyphs, macros, maps |

Each has a level from 1 to 99, from EXP earned by doing its things (blinks, crystal strikes, throws, reprogramming, sigils, rhythm songs,
a charted Well). **How well you do it counts.** A rote act pays 0.4 times the base, a middling one about 1, a masterful one 5. At a
middling level, 99 takes about 300 real hours in each domain, and all seven at 99 is "The World", the end of the journey.
The log says your level when it rises.

### Widening

A level **widens** what a tool can do. It never makes you more accurate. At level 1 the tool is as it is without it, and at 99:

| Domain | Widens | At 99 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Ouranurgy | blink and grapple reach; how long time stays still; rail lane speed | 1.5 times; 1.3 times; 1.5 times |
| Manifestation | how long a structure stands; how many stand | 1.5 times; 2 more |
| Divination | survey radius; crystal reveal; reckoning lead; weather forecast | 1.6, 1.5, 1.5 and 4 times |
| Psychokinesis | what the hand can lift; how far it throws | 2 times; 1.5 times |
| Possession | how long a macro a mind takes; Figments a Lockheart holds | 1.5 times; 2 more |
| Alteration | cuts a Sunder makes; how far a swell or wring goes | 3 more; 1.5 times |
| Spellscription | macros the Veritome keeps; copies of a Cogitomap | 3 more; 3 more |

## Luck

Luck rises from living through the unlikely, good or bad. Each unlikely event counts by how surprising it was (a prismatic chest more
than a near miss). It grows with the square root of that total, to 99. It bends chance only, never a skill. Nothing reads it yet, so it
has no effect in play today.

## The all-arts switch

The all-arts switch (ALL ARTS, in the Codex) lends you every art without learning it. Nothing it lends is counted. In DEBUG it is on. STORY
turns it off, and the arts are learned by doing.
