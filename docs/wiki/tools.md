# The tools

You carry seven psychic tools. Each is a different way to fight, move, sense or catch things, and each is drawn with its own key.
This page is one card per tool: what it is, how to use it, and what it becomes on a ship at sea.

## How the belt works

| Rule | Detail |
| --- | --- |
| In hand | one tool at a time. Drawing one puts the other away first |
| Worn | five places on the body: two across the back, two at the hips, one at the neck. The Lockheart starts at the neck |
| Carried | a tool not worn rides in the Pneuka Box (P) and cannot be drawn |
| Fittings | what fits into a tool (a lure, an instrument, the Lockheart's Possibilikeys) is kept in the Pneuka Box |
| First person | every tool works in first person (Z) |
| Lock-on | middle click with the Sondelass or the Soul Brush out toggles a lock on a target |

## The Psygun (X)

**A hand-cannon** that runs on Lachryma. It fires, charges a piercing beam, and throws special shells.

| Input | Does |
| --- | --- |
| Left click | fire a shot (semi-auto, buffered) |
| Hold left click | charge a piercing beam |
| Right button | aim down sights |
| Middle click | fire the chambered shell |
| 1 to 9, 0, minus, or the wheel | choose a chamber |

A psygun is how many **chambers** it has and how deep each is. Your first gun has 6 chambers holding 8 of a shell each (4 of the
heavy ones: Bomb, Well, Anchor). You choose what goes in each chamber in the Pneuka Box. Refill shells at the glowing reliquaries
(3 shells a visit, then a 20 real second wait). Other guns: the Pepperbox (11 chambers, 3 each), the Long Tube (3 chambers, 16 each).

| Shell | Does |
| --- | --- |
| Cleave | a 4.2 m line of light that cuts in two whatever it sweeps. Press its key again to turn it level or upright |
| Push | a cone of force |
| Well | pulls everything into orbit for 3.2 real seconds, then pops. What reaches the core is crushed into baubles |
| Mark | stuns clapperjars for 4.5 real seconds and marks pots for 15: they glow through walls, take double damage and drop Lachryma |
| Bomb | a clay grenade: splash damage, molten slip, a hot pool |
| Bank | bounces up to 5 times, harder each time, bending toward a target |
| Seek | hold to paint up to 6 targets, release to fire a fan of seekers |
| Slip | liquid clay that paints floors and walls wet (dive in with C) |
| Groove | a mirror ball: everything near dances for 9 real seconds |
| Anchor | pins one thing where it is, in mid-air if that is where it was |
| Hatch | a pot cracks open and a friendly clapperjar climbs out; a clapperjar it hits turns friendly |

**At sea:** **Blaster**. It is always mounted. Left click fires; hold the right button to **lock on** up to 8 targets and release to
fire a lance at each. Each lock costs 3 energy.

## The Sondelass (Q)

**A telescoping blade** worn on the back, with three forms. Press **1**, **2** or **3** to change form.

| Form | Key | Does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Cutlass | 1 | a three-stroke combo; a lunge (the Stinger); a guard on V that turns a projectile aside or, well timed, sends it back; blade mode |
| Rod | 2 | angling at the Weir, the oasis in the Dunes |
| Hook | 3 | a grapnel: **Grapple** |

- **Blade mode.** Time slows and the blade cuts along the plane you draw (like Metal Gear Rising's zandatsu). A creature that is not
  stunned resists the cut.
- **Grapple** (the hook). Solid ground pulls you to it; jump cuts the line and keeps your speed. Something loose is yanked to you. A
  held line lets you swing. The line is up to 46 m long.
- **Angling** (the rod). Pick a **lure** (a made one, or any curio) and the feeling a sounding pushes into it (Wonder, Mirth, Desire,
  Grief, Dread). Cast, twitch and sink. **Sound** with middle click to stir the pond. Answer the bite inside its window, then fight:
  keep the tension needle in its band while the fish tires, lean against its pull, and give line when it runs. There are 10 species,
  each with its own depths, tides, bites and fights. The Codex's ANGLING shelf is the bestiary.

**At sea:** **Grapple**. Pulls loot in and yanks boarders off. Reach 16 m, cooldown 1.5 s.

## The Soul Brush (G)

**A club** the size of a calligrapher's brush, soaked in slip. It alters things more than it hurts them.

| Input | Does |
| --- | --- |
| Left click | the club: three heavy blows that bat clapperjars away |
| Hold left click | a slam |
| Tap right button | a flick of slip at range |
| Slide with it out | the core slide trails a stroke of slip you can dive into |
| Hold right button | **Celestial Brush**: time stops, the screen turns to paper, and what you draw is read when the hand rests |

Each drawing writes a technique onto what it was drawn over. Every one costs Lachryma from the brush's Lachrymato Bottle.

| Drawing | Technique | Cost | Does |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| a stroke across | Still | 4 | holds what it crosses in place (a pot in mid-air is a step) |
| a stroke down | Bounce | 3 | makes it bounce |
| a circle | Mend | 5 | a cracked pot is mended with gold, a wreck is rebuilt, a clapperjar is befriended |
| a circle with a fuse | Ember | 4 | the pots in it burst when broken |
| a spiral | Gale | 5 | a gust. In the Dunes it turns the wind itself |
| a bolt | Bolt | 6 | a strike that stuns |
| ^ | Light | 3 | makes it light |
| V | Heavy | 3 | makes it heavy |
| a heart | Solace | 6 | every clapperjar in view dances; the god hand's jar is soothed |

**At sea:** **Absorb Spray**. A cone (50 degrees, 9 m) that eats enemy shots of your own feeling. Costs 4 energy.

## The Veritome (J)

**A book that is a camera.** It gathers what is true and changes nothing.

| Input | Does |
| --- | --- |
| 1 | the **Flash**: a cone of light that dazzles and fills the **stun** meter of everything with eyes in it |
| Right button | raise the lens |
| Left click (lens up) | expose a plate. The memory holds 24 |
| Middle click on a stunned creature | **Reprogram** (below) |

Plates are appraised in a batch in the darkroom (Codex, VERITOME). The **Compendium** keeps your best photograph of each thing. The
**bestiary** learns what each creature was doing. The **Book** receives a card for each Major Arcana whose sitting you caught. The
Book is also the **bank**: it stores things as cards and gives them back into the Pneuka Box, only while the Veritome is drawn.

**Reprogram.** Walk up to a stunned creature and press the middle button. Pick one of your **macros** and type its words in neuralese
before time runs out. You compose macros in the Codex (VERITOME, THE MIND shelf) on a lattice of **Functions**, each one a part of a
creature's own mind (an action, a status, a drive, a relation, a memory wiped). The better it fits, the stronger it is. You learn
Functions by photographing creatures doing them.

**At sea:** **Snapshot**. Opens weak points for a few seconds (its Flash holds one open 3 s). Costs 6 energy, cooldown 6 s.

## The Dreamvane (K)

**A shepherd's crook** with a dreamcatcher, a pick and a tuning fork. Everything made of Lachryma gives off a signature, and the
Dreamvane finds them. While it is worn, the compass rides at the top of the view.

| Input | Does |
| --- | --- |
| Hold right button | **Dowse**: the dreamcatcher turns toward the loudest signature. The wheel attunes it |
| Left click | **the pick**: mines crystal formations in the Dunes |
| Tap right button | **the fork**: thrown. In crystal it doubles the pick; in a creature it shakes Lachryma out; in the ground it rings and draws creatures to look |
| Middle click | **the survey**: charts the ground around you |

**Tuned by ear.** Each pick blow sounds a note: sharp above the crystal's sweet spot, flat below, wavering from the wrong side. A blow
at the right height from the right side opens the crystal at once and pays many times over.

**At sea:** **Radar**. Passive. The reckoning marks half again as early, the shoal's caller is shown, and the Leviathan's breach is marked
a bar ahead.

## The Crucibelle (U)

**A smoking bell** held like a lantern. Its notes are always in key with the music, so none is wrong.

| Input | Does |
| --- | --- |
| 1 to 5 | play the five notes of the music's minor pentatonic. Hold the right button for an octave up |
| Left click | toll the bell |
| Notes on the beat | build **fever** |

The **instrument** fitted in the Pneuka Box is its voice.

| Song | Notes | Cost | Does |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Seeing | 5 3 1 | 8 | raises veiled crystal and lights Lachryma signatures |
| Seeming | 2 4 2 4 | 12 | stands up a smoke Courier that hunting creatures take for you |
| Rally | 1 3 5 | 14 | quickens your allies and your Lachryma |
| Lullaby | 5 4 3 2 1 | 14 | puts nearby foes to sleep |
| Call | 1 1 5 5 | 24 | stands up a smoke spirit that fights beside you |

**Busking.** Press F on a busker's mat (Old Grog's pier at the Weir, Margarite's dock) with the Crucibelle worn to play a song in the rhythm mode for tips: keys 1 to 0 on the notes as they come. A miss leaves a hole in the tune. Walk off the pier and it stops.

**At sea:** **Bomb**. Clears every shot within 10 m (14 m on the beat), scatters a shoal, staggers boarders. 3 charges a crossing.

## The Lockheart (I)

**A small coffin on a chain**, worn at the neck from the start. Worn, it drinks the Lachryma that overflows your pool.

| Input | Does |
| --- | --- |
| Left button (drawn) | **Vacuum** (the hoover): pulls in loose baubles and the Lachryma of a creature laid low |
| Right button (full, with a Possibilikey on its ring) | **the Opening**: your ultimate |

**The Opening.** The game stops, the Possibilikeys go in, and a **wheel** of odds stands in the sky. Whatever it lands on comes out as
hard as the coffin was full, from a spill of Lachryma to the slip nuke. You can put up to 4 Possibilikeys on the ring. Each changes
the wheel:

| Possibilikey | Effect |
| --- | --- |
| Inverted | turns the odds upside down: the likeliest becomes the rarest |
| Even | every outcome as likely as any other |
| Loaded | the rarest outcome 5 times as likely |
| Twin | it spins twice and both happen |
| Wide | whatever comes out reaches twice as far |
| Echo | whatever comes out happens again a moment later |

A brass Possibilikey is spent in the turning. The others can be used again, but each use makes one likelier to snap (about 3 openings on average).

**Catching.** With a **summoning coffin** on the chain, the Opening becomes a catch. Open it on a Figment that is laid low (stunned,
asleep or melted) in front of you within 8 m. The wheel is two sectors, caught and free, and their width follows the odds (the
Figment's class, how long its stun has left, its feeling, your Possibilikeys). Caught, it is bound and waits in your Pneuka Jar for
the Spirit Garden. Missed, the Possibilikeys are spent all the same.

**At sea:** **Vacuum**. Sucks in shots and small fish for 2 Lachryma each. A cone of 35 degrees and 8 m, cooldown 6 s.

## Tools at sea

A ship carries the psygun plus as many worn tools as its hull has slots (sloop 2, frigate 3, destroyer 2, tanker 1, galleon 1). Keys 1 to 3 fire them in the order chosen at the pier. Cooldowns are in real seconds (the game counts them in beats of the music, 1.5 s a bar).
