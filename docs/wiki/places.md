# Places

The game has four layers you can walk: the workshop (two floors), the basement below it, and the Dunes far beneath both. Past the
Dunes lies the Emocean, a sea you sail across. This page says what each place is for and how to get there. Press **M** for the map.

## The workshop

You start here. It is the kiln, the folk and a lot of pots to break.

| Place | What you do there |
| --- | --- |
| The kiln (**F** at its mouth) | choose and fire a look for your clay body, mend cracks. See [Things and money](economy.md) |
| The ground floor | shelves, wheels, slip barrels. Mistress Saggar keeps the kiln |
| The gallery (balcony stairs, or the Lachryma geyser) | sculptures, a porcelain showroom, an upper kiln, lanterns on ropes you can shoot |
| The gong by the door | **F** starts the time trial: break about a dozen glowing jars on both floors. Medals are generous |
| The Throwing Room (a side room) | measure aim and recoil: targets, Strawman, a spray wall and drills. It never pays and never counts |
| The Bisque Shrine | where you rest and are made whole. See "Shrines" below |

The pots are lathed by the game in three clay bodies that break differently: stoneware in slabs, earthenware in shards, porcelain in
slivers and dust. Ember urns explode in chains.

## The basement

Go down through the glowing hole in the ground floor. The geyser beside the landing throws you back up. The basement is where you
learn to move and test what you can do.

| Room | What it is |
| --- | --- |
| The hub | the metrics gym: a height ladder, clearance gates, slope ramps and a long-jump lane, all labelled. The **Index** stands here |
| The Index | a console. **F** at it, then pick any room to be sent there |
| The course | one loop of eight stations round the hub, one skill each, with checkpoints and splits. **R** returns you to the last station |
| The movement lab | one hall, five wings: the hands (crates, throwing, a mortar to parry, recoil), the rigging (ledges, bars, ziplines, beams, grates, poles), the techs (the pool, ladders, slam, slip, blink, stomp), the clockwork mill (cogs, a millstone, belts, lifts, a shuttle, a ferris wheel, steam) and the kiln stack (a 57 m well with moving carts to slam onto) |
| The lap circuits | The Braid (three lines between junctions), The Mill Race (the mill in one loop), The Spindle (a chain of skills, then a long chute) |
| The siege | the god hand's arena. Raids happen here and nowhere else. See [The god hand](god-hand.md) |

Lap circuits score you with splits against your best: 1 second for a slow gate, 3 seconds for a fall, and a medal at the finish.
Everything that moves under your feet (cogs, lifts, belts, swings) carries you, and you keep its speed when you leave it.

### How far the gaps are cut

The basement's gaps are cut to about 85% of what you can do at default tuning:

| Move | Distance |
| --- | ---: |
| Sprint jump | 4.0 m |
| Sprint and double jump | 6.7 m |
| Sprint jump and dash | 9.1 m |
| Slide-hop, dash and double jump | 13.7 m |
| Jump height | 0.92 m |
| Double jump height | 1.66 m |

## The Dunes

The Dunes are a sand sea about a kilometre across, under a low gold sun, with half-buried ruins and a pale spire. An invisible edge
at 480 m from the centre shows itself only where you touch it. Use the Index (or a Shrine) to get there.

At the heart is the oasis and **the Weir**: a pond in terraces, a pier, a well of liquid Lachryma, the Tithe and its treasury (Raku
keeps it), and Old Grog fishing from the pier. Three slip jellies live on the flats. Crystals stand about the sand.

| Place | What you do there |
| --- | --- |
| The Weir's pier | fish, buy lures and casks of crude from Old Grog |
| The treasury | Raku's shop, five chests on plinths, and the Tithe (48 cubes a pull). See [Things and money](economy.md) |
| The crystals | break them with the Dreamvane's pick, tuned by ear, for cubes. See [The tools](tools.md) |
| The Gnomon (the pale spire) | **F** at its foot by day starts the Solar Skiffing trial |
| The Lamp Shrine | at the mouth of the Great Dunemaw |
| The Float Shrine | at the Weir |
| The shore | due east of the oasis, about 500 m (or **E** at the Index) |
| The Great Dunemaw | north-west of the oasis, about 180 m (or **W** at the Index) |

### The shore and the jetty

The sand runs down to the Emocean and there is nothing but crude sea to the horizon. You can wade a step in, no further. The jetty runs
out over it, and your sloop moors at the end. **F** at the jetty's end opens the pier, where you choose an island and cast off. All of
that is on [The Emocean](emocean.md).

### The Great Dunemaw

A dark pool turning in a ring of fallen stones. **F** at it goes down into a Well: three floors of rooms, laid out afresh each game
day, ending in a boss fight with the Great Slip Jelly. The Dreamvane hears it from far off. A pale pool is the way up with your haul.
The full guide is [The Great Dunemaw](great-dunemaw.md).

### Solar Skiffing

Press **Y** to bring out the **skiff**, a small hovering boat with a lug sail. The wind alone drives it.

| Key | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **W** | hoist the sail (it stays up) |
| **S** | lower the sail and brake. Hoisting again quickly is a **pump** |
| **A** / **D** | steer. In the air, spin the whole skiff (land a whole turn for a boost) |
| **Space** | crouch and hop |
| **Shift** | solar flare, for Lachryma |
| **Y** | stow it |

A tailwind is fastest. Sailing into the wind is slow but never stalls. The pennant shows the wind.

The **Solar Skiffing trial** begins at the Gnomon's foot, by day only (it is closed at night).

| Trial number | Value |
| --- | ---: |
| Rings | 24, on a loop laid out each game day |
| Time on the dial | 90 real seconds |
| Bonus for each lit ring passed | 2 real seconds |

A ring is lit where the sun reaches it and dark in a dune's shadow, so dawn and dusk are the hard runs. There is a medal at the last
ring, and each medal pays once. Leaving the Dunes ends the trial.

## Shrines

A Shrine is where you rest (your pool fills and your mind settles), where you are made whole after a shatter, where you can travel to
any Shrine you have found, and where you enter the Spirit Garden. **F** at one finds it. There are none inside a Well.

| Shrine | Where |
| --- | --- |
| The Bisque Shrine | the workshop (you start with it) |
| The Lamp Shrine | at the Great Dunemaw's mouth |
| The Float Shrine | at the Weir |
| The Pearl Shrine | on Margarite's dock |

A Shrine is not a save point. The game keeps everything as it happens.

## The Spirit Garden

Entered at any Shrine. You become the Pneuka Jar hopping round small planetoids, and your cursor is the god hand. It is where you
raise spirits, grow materials and press your soul colour. See [The Spirit Garden](spirit-garden.md).

## Margarite's dock

The far end of a first crossing. It has a lamp tower, the Pearl Shrine, the Purser (who buys crude, maps and materials) and Letty
Marque, who posts bounties. It is where the Emocean leaves you after your first crossing. See [The Emocean](emocean.md).

## Finding your way

- **M** opens the map ("Mind Mapping"), with one tab for each layer: the upper floor, the ground floor, the basement and the Dunes.
- The map shows only ground you have explored.
- The Index sends you to any room in the basement, the Dunes' landmarks and more.
- Names in the log appear as you enter each room.
