# The Spirit Garden

The Spirit Garden is a small galaxy inside your Pneuka Jar. You hop round tiny worlds as the Jar, and the cursor is the god hand: it
reshapes the ground, pours water, places buildings and holds the spirits you caught. Come here to turn good play into steady income
(cubes and materials that grow while you are away), to raise the creatures you bound, and to run Soul Alchemy's press (see
[Soul Alchemy](soul-alchemy.md)).

## Getting in

Enter at any Shrine. You arrive on the Dantian as the Pneuka Jar, never as the Courier. The torii on the Dantian takes you back out.

| Key | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| W A S D | Hop (longer the longer you hold). In the overhead view they move the view instead. |
| Space | Jump higher |
| Q / E | Turn the view |
| Mouse wheel | Zoom (or tilt, in first person) |
| Z | First person, from the Jar's mouth |
| ` (backtick) | Overhead: the god hand's view straight down |
| F | Use what you stand at (the torii, the shed, a bed, a spirit, the press, the tree) |
| 1 to 9, 0 | Choose a hand art (below) |
| Shift + wheel | Stroke size, 1 to 12 m |
| R | Turn the choice of the art: which ground to paint, which feeling the water has |
| Ctrl + Z | Undo (the last 10 strokes) |
| Ctrl + Backspace twice within 3 real seconds | Put the planetoid under the hand back to its rest shape (free) |

The sky takes the tint of your last draught, and the moonflower opens at night by the game hour.

## The planetoids

Each planetoid is a sphere you can hop round in 5 to 15 real seconds. A launch lotus on one flies you to a neighbour.

| Planetoid | Radius | Plots | What it holds |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **The Dantian** (the heart) | 20 m | 6 | The way in, a lake of your own Lachryma, the Pneuka Box's shed |
| **The Herb Terraces** | 12 m | 6 | The beds |
| **The Athanor** | 10 m | 3 | The spirit press and the plate shrine (see [Soul Alchemy](soul-alchemy.md)) |
| **The Pavilions of Echoes** | 14 m | 5 | The mastery slots |
| **The Mulberry Grove** | 16 m | 8 | The spirits and the cocoon tree |
| **The Chimney** | 8 m | 2 | The meditation mat, the Heavenly Kiln, the keepsake pots |

**Myggdrasil's planetoid** (26 m, 6 plots) is given at the second Firing and is not bought.

**Bought planetoids** sit on the ring: 10 slots, 95 m from the Dantian, alternately 12 degrees above and below its equator. You buy a
seed at the shed, carry it up with the god hand and let go in open sky. It takes the nearest free slot and grows lotuses to its two
nearest neighbours. They are 8 to 16 m wide with 4 to 8 plots. Money alone never buys one: each also needs a Firing.

| Bought planetoid | Cost | Needs |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1st | 480 cubes | 3rd Firing |
| 2nd | 960 cubes | 4th Firing |
| 3rd | 1,920 cubes | 5th Firing |
| 4th | 3,840 cubes | 6th Firing |

The four are the Moonflower Moon, the Koi Pond, the Drill Yard and the Bone Bed. A spirit vein glows between planetoids and ends on
each at the highest ground within 35 degrees of the way to the other. Raise a ridge and the vein's end follows it.

## Reshaping the ground

The hand's arts are on the number keys. The planetoid stays a planetoid: the surface moves within a band of its radius and never
through its heart. Reshaping is free.

| Key | Art | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | **Grab** | Pick up the Jar or a spirit and throw it (up to 26 m/s). Tap a spirit to pet it, right button to flick it. Also lifts a placed feature to a free plot. |
| 2 | **Raise** (pull) | Lifts the ground |
| 3 | **Lower** (press) | Pushes the ground down |
| 4 | **Smooth** | Eases bumps |
| 5 | **Flatten** | Levels to the height where the stroke began (terraces) |
| 6 | **Carve** | A narrow groove for water to run in |
| 7 | **Roughen** | Breaks the surface up |
| 8 | **Water** | Left pours, right drinks up, Shift + left sets a spring, Ctrl + left a drain, Shift + right takes the nearest away |
| 9 | **Place** | Put a feature in a plot |
| 0 | **Paint** | Left paints the chosen ground, right clears it |

## Painting the ground

Each ground belongs to one feeling. Painting is free.

| Ground | Feeling | Spreads plants? |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Moss | Wonder | yes |
| Ash | Mirth | no |
| Loam | Desire | yes |
| Slate | Grief | no |
| Silt | Dread | yes |

A bed on its own feeling's ground grows 1.25 times as fast. Plants spread by themselves each game hour, but only onto wet ground that
spreads. Ash and slate, or bare ground, make plants wilt a stage each game hour.

## Water and rain

Water runs downhill, pools in hollows, spills over rims and wears the ground (rivers cut, deltas build). Each patch of water keeps a
feeling. Opposite feelings mixed cancel to plain fair water (Wonder and Grief, Mirth and Dread). Otherwise the larger share wins.

* The Dantian's lake holds the draught you entered with.
* A spring holds the feeling you chose when you set it.
* **Rain** is you. Your draught falls as rain as hard as your mental state is liquid.

| Mental state | Rain strength |
| --- | --- |
| Stoic, Resolved, Balanced | 0 (dry) |
| Fluid | 0.5 |
| Prismatic | 0.9 |
| Brimming | add 0.1 |

So drinking Lachryma in the world waters the garden, and meditating at the Chimney clears it. A bed watered with its own feeling grows
1.25 times as fast (both ground and water: 1.56 times). A spirit that drinks water of its stat's feeling gains 1 point a game hour.

## Placing features

Choose **Place** (9), pick a plot, a feature and a feeling. A feature costs cubes by its size and one material of its feeling's kind.
Prices: small 40 cubes, medium 96, large 240. A Firing opens each feature.

| Feature | Size | Opens at | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Herb Terrace** | medium | 1st Firing | Grows more of a material you plant (it is a bed) |
| **Pond** | medium | 1st Firing | Water that flows downhill; visitors drink here |
| **Lantern** | small | 1st Firing | Light at night; night visitors come |
| **Spore Bed** | small | 1st Firing | Fungus that transforms what you put in it |
| **Pavilion** | large | 2nd Firing | Pays cubes for a fight you beat (it is a mastery slot) |
| **Incense Burner** | small | 2nd Firing | Calms you faster while you rest near it |
| **Booster Stone** | small | 2nd Firing | Boosts the features next to it |
| **Spirit House** | medium | 3rd Firing | Room for two more spirits |
| **Drill Yard** | large | 4th Firing | Spirits train here |

A terrace or pavilion you place gives you a bed or a slot for free. Moving a placed feature costs nothing.

**Formation.** Features next to each other make each other stronger or weaker, by the five feelings' cycles. A neighbour whose feeling
**generates** yours adds 0.1 to your feature's strength; one that **overcomes** yours takes 0.1 away. The ground under it and water
reaching it each count as one neighbour. The strength never drops below 0.5, so a bad layout is weak, never dead. A feature within 3 m
of a spirit vein's end is on the vein, and its strength doubles.

| Feeling | Generates | Overcomes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Wonder | Mirth | Desire |
| Mirth | Desire | Grief |
| Desire | Grief | Dread |
| Grief | Dread | Wonder |
| Dread | Wonder | Mirth |

## Mastery slots (the dividend)

An **encounter** is mastered when every achievement in its group is done. Set a mastered encounter in a slot (on the Pavilions) and
it pays while you are away.

| Fact | Number |
| --- | --- |
| Starting slots | 3 |
| Pay per slot | 5% of what farming it by hand pays an hour (24 cubes per real hour) |
| Fills for | 8 game days (8 real hours), then waits to be collected |
| Extra slots | 1,440, 2,880, 5,760 cubes |
| Spirits at work | up to +25% |

The five encounters are the slip jellies, the crystal formations, the Great Dunemaw, the Lockheart and the catch. One encounter fills
one slot. Collecting is free and restarts the filling.

## Beds

A bed takes one material from your Pneuka Box. After 6 game hours (15 real minutes) it has grown 2 more of that kind, at the same
tier. Harvest it to take them. You start with 2 beds. More cost 480, 960, 1,920 and 3,840 cubes, or come free with a placed terrace.
A spirit tending a bed adds up to +25% to the yield.

## Spirits: catching, raising, releasing

**Catching.** A Figment caught in the world is bound and waits in your Jar. Two ways:

* **Lockheart:** a critically stunned Figment, then the wheel of odds. A miss spends the Possibilikeys.
* **God hand:** hold a stunned Figment over the Jar's mouth through its struggle. It lasts 1 real second plus 1 per class (Guppy 1 s up
  to Leviathan 5 s). It is certain if held through, but the Jar stands still and a stun that ends first frees it.

Bound spirits hop out into the Mulberry Grove. F at one to feed it, train it, take it out as an ally, name it, race it or release it.
Spirits have five stats (0 to 999), one a feeling. A drill raises one stat by less the higher it is, and tires the spirit. A
spirit can also arrive as a **visitor**:

| Visitor | Comes to | In a garden that feels |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Slip jelly | 1 pond | Grief (2 features) |
| Clapperjar | 2 lanterns | Mirth (2 features) |
| Glint | 2 ponds | Wonder (2 features) |
| Lobber | 1 drill yard | Desire (2 features) |

A visitor whose wants are met visits once a game day. After 3 visits (4 for the glint) it settles if a Spirit House has room.

**Releasing.** A spirit is bound until you release it. A released spirit is fired at the Chimney into a **keepsake pot** that stands
in a ring there for good. It holds the spirit's name, kind and colour, and a line of its song. The Chimney shows up to 64 pots, the
newest 6 painted.

## Firings and the Heavenly Kiln

Your Firing is read from the sum of your Soul Alchemy ranks. It opens places and verbs, never a number on you. Cross one at the
Chimney's mat: lightning falls on you, each strike outlined on the ground first. Hop out of the ring or flick the bolt back with the
hand (right button, in its last 0.45 s). More than 3 hits and the kiln closes. Failing costs nothing but the try.

| Firing | Rank sum needed | Strikes | Seconds apart |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1st, Candling | 0 | (had from the start) | |
| 2nd, Sinter | 7 | 20 | 0.97 |
| 3rd, Lustre | 14 | 24 | 0.87 |
| 4th, Salt | 24 | 28 | 0.79 |
| 5th, Reduction | 36 | 32 | 0.71 |
| 6th, Anagama | 50 | 36 | 0.64 |

After the sixth, every 20 more ranks is another Firing, by number. Each bolt lands within 1.2 m of you, its ring is outlined for 0.9
real seconds.

## Races

Carve one groove that closes on itself and runs 40 m or more to make a track (up to 4 on a planetoid). Up to 4 spirits run it, begun
from a spirit's page. Each stretch reads a stat.

| Stretch | Stat |
| --- | --- |
| Flat | Mirth |
| Climb | Desire |
| Water | Dread |
| Last third | Grief (stamina) |
| Inside line | Wonder |

Speed is 4 m/s plus up to 3 m/s from the stat. The winner gains 2 bond. Races pay no cubes. They count in the ledger.

At the Chimney two of your spirits can also spar (45 real seconds at most, nobody hurt). Each gains 6 points in its strongest stat and
25 fatigue.

## The mycelium

The mycelium is a second road to a colour for Soul Alchemy. Everything a fungus eats has a colour (a hue and a saturation). What it
gives back is coloured by what it ate. You choose the colour by choosing the food.

### Spore beds

Place a **Spore Bed**, **inoculate** it with a strain you hold, then **set** what it eats from your Pneuka Box. It works, and the result
waits in it, never spoiling. You may take a thing back unchanged in the first game hour (2.5 real minutes).

| Strain | Feeling | Verb | What it does | Eats | Takes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| The lichen | Wonder | **Graft** | Two curios become one: the curio nearest their mixed colour. Two of one tier and near opposites give a tier up. | 2 curios | 12 game hours |
| Koji | Mirth | **Ferment** | Makes a material more vivid (saturation up 0.25) | a material | 8 |
| The inkcap | Desire | **Print** | Copies anything as one material of its exact colour, one tier lower | a material, curio or fish | 4 |
| The oyster | Grief | **Rot** | Breaks anything into materials of its colour, worth 60% of it | a material, curio or fish | 6 |
| Witches' butter | Dread | **Dissolve** | Makes a material paler (saturation down 0.3) | a material | 6 |

A game hour is 2.5 real minutes, so 4 to 12 game hours is 10 to 30 real minutes. Your first bed comes with the oyster and the inkcap.
The other strains come from Myggdrasil. Spore beds in the generating order next to each other (Wonder, Mirth, Desire, Grief, Dread)
work 25% faster. A bed next to the strain that overcomes it works 25% slower. A spirit set to tend a bed makes it 25% faster.

### Myggdrasil

Myggdrasil is a huge tree (48 m) on its own planetoid. F at its roots to feed it anything with a colour, to pick the crown, or to hang
a card.

* **The tincture** is its sap: the average colour of all it has eaten, each meal weighed by its worth.
* **Girth** grows as meals double (girth = floor of log2 of 1 + worth eaten / 64).
* **Caps** are its ten fruiting bodies. One opens at the start, one more each girth. At each dawn (game hour 5) every open cap fruits
  one material of the tincture's colour, leaned 25% toward the game day's feeling. The crop waits in the crown.
* The days run Wonder, Mirth, Desire, Grief, Dread, then a **fair** day (no lean) and a **prismatic** day (one fruit at full saturation).
* Caps one to ten bear tiers 0, 0, 1, 1, 2, 2, 3, 3, 4, 4. The seventh cap, Mercy, gives you a strain's spores when it opens.

**Branches.** Hang a Major Arcana card from your Book on one of its 22 branches to open it for good. Each adds one thing.

| Branch adds | Cards | Effect |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A fruit | 8 | One more fruit each dawn |
| A sharper tincture | 5 | Fruit leans less toward the day's feeling (each sharper one makes the lean 40% weaker) |
| A strain's seed | 5 | Spores of one strain (Lovers Wonder, Judgement Mirth, World Desire, Death Grief, Hanged Man Dread) |
| A sporeling | 4 | Sporelings come more often |

**Sporelings** are small fungal spirits that stand round the roots and hop now and then, up to 6 at once, in the tincture's colour.
They do not yet settle as visitors; that is not built.

### The Grimoire of Echoes

The Codex has a page for the mycelium. It shows only what you have met: the strains you hold and what they eat, every graft you have
made (its two curios and the result), Myggdrasil's girth, open caps and tincture colour, the branches hung (the rest as ???), and the
keepsake pots.
