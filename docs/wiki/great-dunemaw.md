# The Great Dunemaw

The Great Dunemaw is the Well in the Dunes: a dungeon you run. You go down three floors of rooms full of slip jellies, pick up
finds, and come back up the same way with a haul and a payout. At the bottom is the great cavern, where the Great Slip Jelly waits.
It is the hardest fight in the game so far, and the one you learn by losing.

A Well is not a place that stays the same. Each game day lays it out again, and each run draws some of it down.

## Going down

| What | How |
| --- | --- |
| Find the mouth | Out on the sand north-west of the oasis (about 180 m). A dark pool turns in a ring of fallen stones. The Dreamvane hears it from far off. |
| Go in | **F** at the mouth. The view closes on the pool, the floor is built, you arrive. |
| Rest before | The Lamp Shrine stands 3 m beside the mouth. There are no Shrines inside. |
| Come back | **F** at a pale pool (the way up). You walk out at the mouth with your haul and your pay. |

A **run** is one trip down and back. You can only be in one run at a time.

## The floors

Three floors, and then the great cavern under the third.

| Floor | Rooms with jellies | Jellies | Clutches of eggs | Pots | Artifacts |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | 2 | 1 each | 1 | 12 | 2 |
| 2 | 3 | 1 each | 3 | 16 | 3 |
| 3 | 4 | 1 each | 3 | 20 | 4 |
| Great cavern | the bowl | the Great Slip Jelly | 8 | none | none |

Each floor is a grid of 5 by 5 cells, 18 m a cell (a square 90 m across). Rooms are built from a dozen designed rooms, turned to fit their
doorways. Doorways are round arches 4 m wide. The first room on a floor has no jelly.

The floors are laid out from the game day's seed. Everyone gets the same Well on the same game day (a game day is one real hour). A
Well from an earlier game day is gone. Larger floors (13, 19 and 25 cells a side) are in the plan and are not built yet.

- **The way up** is a pale pool on each floor. F at it takes you out to the mouth.
- **The way down** is a dark pool on each floor. F at it takes you one floor deeper.
- The third floor's way down drops you into the great cavern. The cavern has no way down.

## Clutches and brood

A **clutch** is a nest of 3 to 6 eggs, guarded by the jellies near it. While a guard lives and you are within 20 m, one **brood** (a
small jelly) hatches every 20 real seconds, up to 3 out at once. Three blows break a clutch. A broken egg leaves slip roe 30% of the
time (a bed in your Spirit Garden grows it). A clutch you break before the big fight is brood the Great Slip Jelly cannot call.

## Pots and artifacts

- **Pots** can be broken. A quarter of them hold a find (a few cubes, a curio or a material).
- **Artifacts** glint in the walls; the Dreamvane hears them. F takes one. Each is worth 4 minutes of play, and 25% more for each floor
  down.
- **The warped artifact**: one a floor, worth three times as much. Taking it moves the floor: the way down shifts, a sandfall opens, and
  2 brood wake. Read the room first.

## The haul and the run's pay

When every jelly on a floor is down, the floor gives up a **material** (one of seven kinds). It is a tier higher for the floor with a FOE
beaten, and one time in four a tier higher again. That is the **haul**. It only comes home if you leave alive.

What you are paid is set by your deepest floor and the FOEs you beat. At a full Well:

| Run | Pay (cubes) |
| --- | --- |
| Down to floor 1 | 20 |
| Down to floor 2 | 45 |
| Down to floor 3 | 76 |
| Floor 3 and the Great Slip Jelly burst | 139 |
| Floor 3 and the Great Slip Jelly reprogrammed | 108 |

Artifacts and finds add their worth on top. A **Cogitomap** (a ticket to a seeded run of that Well) comes with the haul if you charted
80% of the floors you walked. It sells for 30% of what that run paid, scaled by how much you charted.

## Leaving: the three ways out

| How | Pay | Haul and Cogitomap |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Walk up the pale pool | all of it | kept |
| Blow the **Wake Whistle** | 75% | kept |
| Shatter | none | lost |

Travelling away from a run by other means also loses it. A shatter wakes you at the mouth with nothing.

### The Wake Whistle

A small clay whistle. Old Grog sells it on the Weir's pier (3 in stock, 64 cubes). You carry one at a time. Use it from the Pneuka Box in a
Well: you hold your breath for 1.5 real seconds, then it breaks and you wake at the mouth. Any blow that hits your shield or your clay
breaks the breath, and the whistle stays in your box. Outside a Well it does nothing.

## A Well fills and runs dry

Each Well has a **fill** from 0 to 1, and pay is multiplied by it.

| Number | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Each run draws | 0.12 of the fill |
| The lowest the pay can fall to | 0.15 of full |
| Refill | 0.03 an hour (real), faster in grief and dread weather |

A Well drawn down to nothing is **dry**: it still pays 15%. A Cogitomap's worth also follows the Well's fill now, so farming a Well
makes its maps cheap and letting it rest restores them.

## The great cavern: the Great Slip Jelly

The fight is a scripted **timeline** in the style of a raid boss, learned by wiping. There is one difficulty. The same casts come in the
same order each pull. The log names each one as it begins ("The Great Slip Jelly readies Lidfall."). The jelly is the Well's **FOE**,
a big creature that keeps the last floor, and the folk call it the Pithos.

### Starting the fight

You arrive on the ledge, 6 m up, behind the **Lip Stone**. The camera sweeps the bowl first, so you see the boss before you face it.
Walk down into the bowl. The fight begins (the **pull**) when you are on the floor within 20 m of the jelly.

### What you hit

| Part | Weak point? | What hits it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| The crown (the broken urn on its head) | no | Impact cracks it (1.5 a blow); a slam 3; its own ram into a pillar 6. Every other type only chips (0.25) and rings off with the resist mark. |
| The body, crowned | no | a quarter of the damage |
| The core (under the crown) | yes | double damage when the jelly is bare |
| The body, bare | no | half damage |

The crown has 3 stages of 6 crack points. It breaks when it rams a pillar: stand in front of a pillar and sidestep. A pillar cracks on
the first ram, falls on the second, and leaves rubble on the third. When the crown bursts, the jelly reels for 4 real seconds and every
blow lands 3 times over.

### The phases

| Phase | Health | What happens |
| --- | --- | --- |
| The crown | 100% to 65% | Ram, slam, rings, trail. Break the crown. |
| Unstopped | at 65%, about 30 real seconds | Untouchable. It sinks and the Blowout hits. The clutches still whole hatch in pairs. |
| Bare | 65% to 30% | The core is the target. The floor slides toward it. It sinks every 12 real seconds for 3. |
| The Sherds | 30% to 5% | It splits into four calves. Kill all four within 30 real seconds or they mend and heal it 10%. A second Blowout at 15%. |
| The Overflow | under 5% | The whole floor turns to slip and every clutch left hatches. |

Health is 900 plain blows (a slip jelly bursts at 8). An expert's clear is set at about 8:00. The **hard enrage** is at 9:30: the
Dunemaw swallows the bowl and the attempt ends. The health number is a model until real fights measure it.

### The casts

| Cast (log name) | Answer |
| --- | --- |
| Lidfall (the big lunge) | **Parry** (V) or roll. It costs 60% of your pool and leaves you Sodden: your next hit taken is doubled. |
| Shoulder Charge (the ram) | Stand before a pillar and step aside. |
| Throwing Rings | Out of the first ring, then back in. |
| Slip Trail | Lead the drops to the rim. Each leaves a puddle for 60 real seconds. |
| Eye Cup (a gaze) | Look away. Looked at through the Veritome, the gaze turns back and stuns it. |
| Blowout (raidwide) | You cannot avoid it. Guard (V held) at the flash for half. It takes 40% of your pool. |
| Centring, Wedge, Decant | Stand on islands (rubble); step off the rings; get behind the jelly. |
| Broodwake | Kill the brood before they reach it. Each heals it 2%. |
| Sherds | Kill four calves in 30 real seconds. |
| The Dunemaw Swallows | The enrage. No answer. |

### Wipes

A shatter in the fight, or the enrage, is a **wipe**. It costs the attempt, not the run. The cavern is laid again, the jelly whole, and
you are made whole at the Lip Stone. Clutches you broke stay broken. The Lip Stone is not a Shrine. The Wake Whistle still takes you
out. (The Lip Stone is drawn as a plain stone for now.)

### The end

| Ending | Run's pay | Afterwards |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Burst | 2 floors' worth extra | The way up forms in the bowl, at the middle. |
| Reprogrammed (below 20% health, while it reels or is stunned) | 1 floor's worth extra | The way up forms in the bowl. The nursery becomes yours. |

The boss drops cosmetics to anyone whose fight met an achievement: clearing it (the Jelly-crown glaze), never being hit by a Lidfall (a
curio, the Crown of the Dunemaw), every clutch broken before the pull (a slip jelly to ride, not built yet), finishing with more than a
minute left (the title Jellybane), and turning its gaze back with the Veritome (the Eye Cup glaze). Nothing in the cavern is for sale.

- The ride-a-jelly drop gives the achievement and the log line only.
