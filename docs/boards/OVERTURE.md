# THE OVERTURE: the trailer's look (Calissa's, to Wanda's "Fortune Favours the Fool", E minor, 150 bpm: a bar 1.6 s)

**The one idea: the trailer is a firing.** Cold dark clay (the mask, unlit) to the logo fired, the chest glaze's climb (celadon,
crazing, raku, kintsugi gold); every section a step hotter, so the colour script is the structure.

- **Colour script:** FUSE near black; RIFF and VERSE warm clay rising; CLIMB the Mind's labradorite (the tools drawn); CHORUS the five
  damage types, one a bar; SOLO gold and violet (the Lockheart); BREAK sepia stills; CHORUS 2 full saturation; ASCEND cooling to evening;
  THE STRIKE kintsugi gold on black.
- **Screen direction:** left to right to the CLIMB (one journey); the chorus comes at the camera; ASCEND turns vertical.
- **No words on screen** but the logo. Cuts land on downbeats; hits on accents.
- Prior art: the anime opening (a cut a bar, the run at camera, the line-up, Bebop's stop-time freezes), Xenogears' and Chrono Cross's
  openings, the music video's cut on the accent.

## As built (R45)

`cine/overture.js` (the director, clocked to the music's own bar), `cine/overture.board.js` (**the board, as data**: when, where, what the
Courier does), the `overture` sequence in `cine/sequences.js` (the cameras, editable in CINEMA). Once a session on the title; any key
skips; `/overture` plays it anywhere.
- The world is run for it (real moves, real tools, real statuses); nothing in it is the player's (the ledger restored, the log silent).
- The solo is the Lockheart's opening stepped on the music, its wheel spun to nothing; the break is four stills (`ui/stills.js`);
  ASCEND to THE STRIKE is the title's scene, the logo (`vfx/logofire.js`) glazed by the chest's climb and flooded gold on the strike.
- **Open:** CHORUS 2 bar 6 (the Vessoul's forms on one silhouette: the god hand, the Jar, the Courier, the sloop) can come back now that
  the sloop exists.
