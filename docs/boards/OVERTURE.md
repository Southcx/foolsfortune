# THE OVERTURE: the storyboard (Calissa's, to Wanda's cue sheet)

The owner's ask: an extended opening cinematic, an in-game trailer that shows what the game is. Wanda's music is done ("Fortune Favours
the Fool", `src/music/overture.js`, E minor, 150 bpm: a bar 1.6 s, a beat 0.4 s); these are the pictures. Times are from the first note.
A **cut** lands on a downbeat unless it says otherwise; a **hit** is a frame that lands on an accent.

## The one idea

**The trailer is a firing.** It opens on cold, dark clay (the mask, unlit) and ends with the logo fired: one kiln from the first frame to
the last, the same climb the chest glaze makes (`vfx/chestglaze.js`: celadon, crazing, raku, kintsugi gold). Every section is a step
hotter, so the colour script *is* the structure, and THE STRIKE pays off what the FUSE promised.

- **Colour script** (`docs/ART.md`, the first layer: feeling is lightness and saturation, not hue): FUSE near black; RIFF and VERSE the
  island's warm clay, rising; CLIMB the labradorite of the Mind (the tools); CHORUS the five damage-type colours, one a bar; SOLO gold and
  violet (Lachryma, the Lockheart); BREAK drained to the photograph's sepia; CHORUS 2 everything at full saturation; ASCEND and HOLD cooling to
  the evening sky; THE STRIKE kintsugi gold on black.
- **Screen direction**: the Courier travels left to right all the way to the CLIMB, so the island reads as one journey; the chorus breaks
  that on purpose (everything comes at the camera); ASCEND turns it to the vertical (the crane up).
- **No words on screen** but the logo (CLAUDE.md: marks, not text). The tool names, the places, the features are told by the shot.

Prior art, as the board borrows it: the anime opening's grammar (a cut a bar in the verse, the run toward camera, the group line-up on the
chorus, freeze frames on stop time: Cowboy Bebop's "Tank!" for the stop-time hits, Persona 5's and Tales of Vesperia's openings for the
character-and-place montage and the logo slam), Xenogears' and Chrono Cross's openings for a world shown before it is explained, and the
music video's habit of cutting on the accent, not the bar, when the band hits.

## What it needs to be built (if the owner wants it in the engine)

- **One sequence** (`src/cine/`): `overture`, its segments the sections above, each shot a key at its time; `len` stretching already fits a
  section to its music. The music's clock drives it (Wanda's cue times), so a cut never drifts.
- **Places**: the shots need the Courier and the camera put in each room for a bar (a teleport the sequence asks for, then the zone draws
  only that room). Petra's: `course` and the zones.
- **New looks**: the photograph freeze (a darkroom grade over a held frame), the tool ring for the CLIMB, the logo as a mesh to glaze. Mine.
- **Puppetry**: the Courier's moves on cue (wallrun, slide, dash, mantle, the draws); the jellies' statuses on cue. Driven like the stress
  test drives them, but scripted.
- **Cost**: it plays once a session on the title, so it can afford a little; it must still hold the frame (`npm run perf`).

Canon now: the firing as the through-line, the colour script, the board above. Open: the owner's yes to building it in the engine, and
any bar Wanda wants to move.
