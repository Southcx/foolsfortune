South, 2026-10-31:
- "I need to know what max/endgame looks like before we can truly set any kind of curve."
- "Well dungeon exploration to me is kind of like our catch all for game genre exploration, game mechanic preservation ... why don't we
  design a sidescrolling platformer Well?"

STORY is a test bench today, not the curve. The curve is set backwards from the top, as the god powers' ramp was (GODHAND.md 11).
This plan says what the top is, what it is for, and how the Wells carry every genre.

## 2. What the top is for

Prior art for "after max":
- **OSRS:** the max cape is a trophy, and the game after it is bossing.
- **FFXIV:** max level opens Savage raids and more jobs.
- **Monster Hunter:** G rank, then tempered monsters.
- **Dark Cloud 2:** the endgame dungeon.
- **Shiren the Wanderer:** the 99-floor dungeon.
- **Diablo III:** Greater Rifts, endless and ranked.

The one lesson: **the top must open a new game, never end the old one.** Ours opens three:

1. **Every Well, deeper and stranger** (section 3). A Well at the top runs past the story's floors, endless and seeded by the game day,
   so everyone dives the same Well that day and the best depth is a record (Diablo III's Greater Rifts, Spelunky's daily). The full
   kit is what lets you go deeper; nothing new is unlocked for it.
2. **Making things for others.** Creative mode is a builder's tool, and we already have the pattern: the track editor's share codes
   and the garden layout's share code. The natural top is **a Well floor you author and share** (Mario Maker's course worlds): a made
   floor goes into the daily Well's pool.
3. **Playing together for real.** The co-op host's errand (COOP.md: guests are ghosts today). The top is where a shared Well run
   matters: four Couriers, one board.

## 3. The Wells as every genre

Already ruled (DESIGN.md 19, 2026-10-05): "Wells come in radically different genres; one Courier, one purse, one ledger, the same seven
tools in every one." Parked in SLICE.md: "a JRPG board game" beside the Dunemaw's action combat. South's two asks complete it: a
**board** that a run moves across, and **genre floors** on it.

### 3.1 The Well's board (plain name)

A Well run is a board of spaces (Mario Party's, Dokapon's, Culdcept's). You move across it on **your own dice**: the stones are your
dice (DICE.md), so the chance you roll is your build's, and Luck's re-roll is already yours. Each space is a floor. The FOE waits at the
board's end. The haul is carried along the board and lost on a fall, as it is now.

Why it fits the game:
- **It is the title.** Fool's Fortune: the Fool walks the Major Arcana, and the dice already live on the vessel.
- **It reuses what we have.** The dice, the spreads (a board's layout can be a tarot spread: a space is a position, the Celtic Cross a
  ten-space board) and the duel's board code.
- **It is the right shape for mechanic preservation.** A space can hold any genre for 1 to 3 real minutes, so a genre costs one floor
  module, not a game.

### 3.2 A genre floor (plain name)

One floor module, one genre, with a fixed contract:

| the contract | what it says |
|---|---|
| the avatar | the Courier, or your Pneuka Jar (small, hopping, Galaxy's gravity: the garden's body), or the god hand |
| the frame | a modifier on the camera and the play space, never on the core movement: a sidescroller locks the camera to the side and the Courier to a plane; the run, jump, dash and wallrun are the same as everywhere (the gold standard) |
| the verbs | which of the worn tools and arts are lent, by the floor's own rule |
| the win and the loss | what clears it, what fails it, and what a fail costs (a crack, some haul) |
| the haul | what it pays: a material, cubes, a card's sitting, a seed |
| the prior art | named in its header, the genre's lesson in one line |

**Candidates, each from a verb we already have:**

| genre floor | avatar | the kit it stands on | prior art |
|---|---|---|---|
| sidescroller | the Courier or your Pneuka Jar | the core movement locked to a plane; the crossing's side view's camera | Klonoa, Trine, Celeste, NieR: Automata's side sections |
| twin-stick arena | the Courier | the psygun, the rail's shot field | Smash TV, NieR: Automata's hacking |
| bullet hell | your Pneuka Jar | the rail's patterns and the parry | Touhou, Undertale's soul box |
| rhythm | the Courier | the rhythm mode, the Crucibelle | Rhythm Heaven, Crypt of the NecroDancer |
| block puzzle | the god hand | the island's matter, Sculpt | Boulder Dash, Baba Is You |
| stealth | the Courier | the prowl, the crawl, a creature's senses | Metal Gear, Mark of the Ninja |
| racing | the Solar Skiff | the board's rules | Excitebike, F-Zero |
| fishing | the Courier | angling | Sega Bass Fishing |
| card game | the Courier | Querent, the house deck | Culdcept, Slay the Spire |
| tower defence | the god hand | the siege, wards and totems | Dungeon Keeper, Plants vs. Zombies |
| a minigame space | anyone | WarioWare's five-second rule, one verb each | WarioWare, Mario Party |

**The deep prior art for the whole idea:** NieR: Automata, which changes genre mid-scene and keeps the same character and stakes;
Undertale, where each fight's genre is the foe's; Astro's Playroom, a hub of genres on one controller; Nintendo Land.

**The first one to build** is the sidescroller, as South asked. It needs no new verb, only a camera and a plane, and both already
exist. A Well space that plays it for two real minutes proves the contract.

## 4. How the curve gets set from here

1. **Make the top playable now: built as the lend panel's THE SUMMIT row** (South, 2026-10-31: "build the 'top' as an extension of
   the 'lent' system"). Where every other lend answers yes at a gate, the Summit answers the cap at a level read: every domain at 99
   (`game.psyche.level`, `widen`), every attribute at rank 10 (`alchemy.rank`; a firing still writes the kept rank), the palm die a d20.
   The ledger is never written, so switching it off puts every number back where play left it. It is off on a new DEBUG save, so the
   curve is still felt; the other caps of section 1 (the garden's Firing, every seed, every achievement) are the panel's other rows.
   Still to fold in: the garden's seeds grown to large, which is the ring's runtime (Petra's).
2. **The summit's distance:** ruled, kept as written (section 1).
3. **Work back.** Each track's ramp follows the god powers' `t^1.6` (most of the gain in the last third, as OSRS's) unless it has
   reason not to. STORY's first 40 hours then become a curve with a known end.
4. **Build the board and the first genre floor** (Petra's runtime; my rules: the board, the spaces' table, each floor's contract).
