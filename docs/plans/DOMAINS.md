## 1. The idea: seven questions, not seven stats

Every interaction in the game, whether a blow, a cast, a step, a photograph, a dish or a deal, can be asked seven questions. Each
domain owns one, and its level is how far you can bend the answer:

| domain | its question | what it bends |
|---|---|---|
| **Ouranurgy** | **when, and where?** | where an effect arrives and when: delay, the timing of a release, reach, a passage through space |
| **Manifestation** | **in what form?** | Lachryma's state of matter: liquid (the pool), solid (Lachrymite, made things), vapour (its signature) |
| **Divination** | **what is known?** | which hidden facts you see: a telegraph, a crack to come, the true odds, a creature's make-up |
| **Psychokinesis** | **with how much force?** | momentum: push, launch, weight carried, a blow's shatter, a thrown thing's energy |
| **Possession** | **whose?** | the boundary of the self: which body, which mind, who takes a blow, who uses a verb |
| **Alteration** | **of which kind?** | conversion: a blow's type, an affliction's, a feeling's, a material's; one substrate into another |
| **Spellscription** | **under which rule?** | written conditions: triggers, vows, sigils, seals, copies; the rules themselves made a thing |

Why questions:
- **They never overlap.** Two domains can touch the same blow without fighting over it. Ouranurgy says when it lands, Psychokinesis how
  hard, Manifestation in what state, Alteration as which type, Possession on whom, Spellscription by which rule, and Divination whether
  you saw it coming.
- **They are teachable.** Each is a word a player already has.
- **They un-merge the audit's overlaps:**
  - Manifestation and Alteration: form against kind.
  - Possession and Spellscription: the seat against the script.
  - Slab and Fold: force against place.
- **Each question is real.** Each is a way of thinking about a physical-psychic world, so every domain teaches something true without
  saying so (DESIGN.md 22): thermodynamics' states of matter, momentum, the line between self and other, the conditional.

## 2. The ladder: a widening, then a relation, then depth, then a rule-break

From the prior art: growth should be **a rule per tier, not a number per tier** (Braid), with a budget whose pressure you can see (Hollow
Knight's notches) and a reserve that skill refills (the sand tanks of Sands of Time).

| levels | what it gives | the law it keeps |
|---|---|---|
| 1 to 29 | **widening**: the question's numbers grow (reach, count, duration, share) | widening is never accuracy or tempo, the attributes' (glossary) |
| 30 to 59 | **a relation**: a new way the question can be answered, a verb or a rule that did not exist | each opens by its level *and* has a feat way (arts by achievement) |
| 60 to 98 | **depth**: the relation reaches further and joins other domains' relations | a combination is two questions answered at once |
| 99 | **the rule-break**: one base rule bent for you, its source the domain at its top | the summit (ENDGAME.md); Creative mode is the god hand's |

The god powers and a duel's powers stay as they are: each is already a domain's. They are read through the domain's question in the
Codex, so a power reads as "Ouranurgy: where".

## 3. The seven, one by one

Each domain gets:
- the substrate it touches;
- its ladder;
- what feeds it (experience: asking its question well pays more, per the design law);
- the attribute it must never step on;
- its tools, the ones that ask its question most.

### 3.1 Ouranurgy: when, and where?

**Owns:** the clock as it touches an effect (delay, the release beat, when the pool begins to refill), and the space between (reach,
apertures).

| level | gives | prior art |
|---|---|---|
| 1-29 | reach (the hook, a portal's span); the regen's delay starts earlier | (built: `reach`, `still`) |
| 30 | **Postpone:** a blow taken can be put off. Its crack arrives 4 real s later, drawn as a gold-dashed crack, and a parry before it lands halves it. One at a time. | FFXIV's Doom, a delayed debt you can answer |
| 45 | **Apertures:** a pair of openings that keeps momentum through (the god hand's Portal, on foot) | Portal |
| 60 | **The fuse:** afflictions built on a creature can be held latent and released on one beat of your choosing, all at once | Chrono Trigger's combined techs; Into the Breach's ordered turn |
| 80 | a postponed blow can be sent back to where it came from, if parried | |
| 99 | **Rewind the vessel:** your pool and cracks put back as they were 5 real s ago, once a game hour. The place and the body's position stay. | Sands of Time; Fire Emblem's Divine Pulse |

- **Fed by:** things arriving on time: a postponed blow parried, a fuse released into a break, a portal passed at speed.
- **Never:** your own tempo (Dexterity's).
- **Tools:** the Sondelass's hook, the Crucibelle's beat, the psygun's shells that delay.

### 3.2 Manifestation: in what form?

**Owns:** Lachryma's states of matter. The pool is liquid; Lachrymite (cubes, shells, made things) is solid; a signature is its vapour.

| level | gives | prior art |
|---|---|---|
| 1-29 | a made thing's hold and hp (a creation, a ward, a wall, a called spirit); the Bottles' capacity | (built: `span`, `count`) |
| 30 | **Plating:** pool set solid on one region of the vessel, taking that region's next crack instead. The price is held off the pool's max while it stands (the held stretch), and melting it back loses a share. | Hollow Knight's notches; latent heat (a change of state costs) |
| 45 | **A made thing's state decides what breaks it:** solid shatters to Impact, liquid spreads under it and ignores it, vapour takes only the psychic types | Divinity: Original Sin 2's surfaces; Noita's matter |
| 60 | **Catch the weather:** a feeling's falling Lachryma caught as liquid in a Bottle, its feeling kept for a draught | Breath of the Wild's chemistry: the world's state is yours to use |
| 80 | a made thing can be made of a feeling: a wall of Grief's rain, a ward of Fury's sparks | |
| 99 | **A second vessel:** a clay double made from the pool, with its own cracks. It stands where you made it, takes blows as its own, and the pool it held comes home when it shatters. | |

- **Fed by:** things made that last and do their work: a plate that took a crack, a wall that held, a spirit that fought its time out.
- **Never:** the shield's size (Willpower's). Plating is a region's armour, paid from the max, never a larger shield.
- **Tools:** the Soul Brush (paint is liquid laid down), the psygun's shells (solid), the god hand (matter).

### 3.3 Divination: what is known?

**Owns:** information: what is hidden, and only *new kinds* of it, never the same thing sooner (Perception's).

| level | gives | prior art |
|---|---|---|
| 1-29 | the Figment telegraph ladder (built: 2, 8, 20, 35); a tag's depth; the forecast's hours | (built) |
| 30 | **The crack to come:** you see which region a blow will crack before it lands, and a body's shatter line (how near it is to breaking) | Slay the Spire's intents; Into the Breach's perfect information |
| 45 | **A body's make-up:** a creature's constitution and the type that bites it, read from facts you hold (photographs, the bestiary), before you ever land a blow | Outer Wilds: knowledge is the progression |
| 60 | **True odds:** world chance shown as numbers (a chest's tier, the Lockheart's wheel, a duel's flee, a crystal's shard) | Darkest Dungeon's scouting |
| 80 | **Call it:** commit to a prediction (a creature's next move, a forecast). Right, and it pays and builds a short window where your read is exact. Wrong costs nothing but the call. | Return of the Obra Dinn: the answer is committed, then checked |
| 99 | **See the next roll:** your own dice's next throw is shown before you commit to it, once a game hour | |

- **Fed by:** predictions that come true, facts appraised, a weakness found before it was struck.
- **Never:** earlier warning of what is already shown (Perception's).
- **Tools:** the Veritome, the Dreamvane (the sky read), the duel's Scan.

### 3.4 Psychokinesis: with how much force?

**Owns:** momentum: push, launch, mass carried, a blow's shatter (SUBSTRATE F5b), a thrown thing's energy. Never damage itself: skill
decides damage.

| level | gives | prior art |
|---|---|---|
| 1-29 | knockback and launch; the weight you can lift and throw (the dead `weight` key, wired) | (built: `throw`) |
| 30 | **Shatter:** your heaviest blows (charges, smashes, Guard Crush) carry shatter, a share that grows with level | Smash's KO power |
| 45 | **The catch:** a projectile parried can be held, then thrown back where you aim, with its energy and yours | Half-Life 2's gravity gun |
| 60 | **Relay:** a thrown body that strikes another passes its momentum on, a chain of launches | Katamari; Control's Launch |
| 80 | **Heft:** a carried mass held off the pool's max (visible), its throw as hard as its weight | Control; Katamari |
| 99 | **Banked motion:** your own speed stored as you move (a sprint, a slide, a fall) and released as one blow's force and shatter | Superhot's motion as the clock, inverted |

- **Fed by:** force delivered well: a launch's distance, a catch returned to its thrower, a relay's length.
- **Never:** damage, accuracy, tempo.
- **Tools:** the psygun (its push, which earns it nothing today), the kick, thrown things, the god hand's Slab.

### 3.5 Possession: whose?

**Owns:** the boundary of the self: which body acts, which mind is open, who takes a blow, whose verb is used.

| level | gives | prior art |
|---|---|---|
| 1-29 | reprogramming's slots; a decoy's pull; how many minds you can link | (built: `macro`, `song`) |
| 30 | **Receptivity:** a mind's openness shown on its body (its EmO band and its afflictions), and a possession only takes when it is open | Shin Megami Tensei's negotiation; Pokemon's capture odds |
| 45 | **The link:** a creature you hold bound shares your cracks. Each blow on either is split between you, and a blow that strikes both lands twice. | Dishonored 2's Domino |
| 60 | **Borrow a verb:** a creature's move lent to you for a while, as your sub's verb on R | Kirby; Prey's typhon powers |
| 80 | **Ride:** leave your vessel standing (open to blows) and drive a creature's body. The slip jelly mount, made any body. | Ghost Trick; Dishonored's Possession |
| 99 | **No border:** a bound spirit's pool and yours are one well | |

- **Fed by:** minds won while open, links held through a fight, a borrowed verb used well.
- **Never:** a status's duration (Focus's). Possession moves *whom* a status is on, never how long.
- **Tools:** the Veritome's reprogramming (the seat; Spellscription writes the script), the Lockheart's drain, the Crucibelle's song.

### 3.6 Alteration: of which kind?

**Owns:** conversion, the exchange rate between kinds:
- a blow's type into another;
- an affliction into another;
- a feeling into another;
- a material into another;
- and one substrate into another.

| level | gives | prior art |
|---|---|---|
| 1-29 | a draught's strength and hold; paint's reach and build | (built: `swell`, `paint`) |
| 30 | **Re-type:** one tool's blows set to a damage type of your choosing for a while (the type chart, woken by F3, made yours to play) | Pokemon's Soak and Conversion |
| 45 | **Reactions:** where two feelings' paints meet, the agate table reacts. Opposites cancel to a calm patch, and agates set a mixed weather on the ground. | Divinity: Original Sin 2's surfaces; Magicka |
| 60 | **Walk the cycle:** an affliction on a creature turned into the one its type trumps (doubt into charm, charm into confusion) | |
| 80 | **The exchange:** pool into mending, or a crack taken on purpose into pool. Instant, lossy (about 3 to 2) and capped a game hour, beside Resilience's slow, free mending. | Fullmetal Alchemist's equivalent exchange |
| 99 | **Re-make a body:** a creature's constitution changed for a while. A slip jelly made an Illusion body is broken by plain force. | |

- **Fed by:** conversions that pay: a re-typed blow on a weakness, a reaction set off, a status walked into a break.
- **Never:** mending's rate (Resilience's). The exchange is a different door: instant, lossy, capped.
- **Tools:** the Soul Brush (paint and its feelings), the Lockheart (Delirium), the press (Soul Alchemy's materials).

### 3.7 Spellscription: under which rule?

**Owns:** written conditions: triggers, vows, sigils, seals, copies. The rules themselves made into things you carry and place.

| level | gives | prior art |
|---|---|---|
| 1-29 | a sigil's count; an inscription's length; copies (a Cogitomap) | (built: `slots`; `copy` wired) |
| 30 | **The chain:** what you cast is read in order, and a chain you cannot afford keeps its trigger and drops the rest (never a dry click) | Noita's wands |
| 45 | **A standing rule:** one "when X, then Y" written on your sub tool's lent verb, fired by the event it names (when you parry, toll; when the pool drops below a quarter, drink a Bottle) | FF12's Gambits; Magic: the Gathering's triggers |
| 60 | **A vow:** a restriction written on yourself (no dash, no shield) that makes your costs cheaper while it holds, its source your seal | Hunter x Hunter's vows; a real oath's binding |
| 80 | **Rewrite a tag:** add a tag to a thing (make a wall `hookable`, a crate `hurtable`), never remove one | Baba Is You; the Well's Script stage |
| 99 | **A place's law:** an inscription that changes one base rule inside its circle while it stands (shots free, no regen delay, gravity halved), visible as a sealed ring | |

- **Fed by:** rules that fire and pay: a trigger answered, a vow kept through a fight, a tag that opened a way.
- **Never:** the moveset's own timing.
- **Tools:** the Soul Brush's Celestial mode, the Veritome's macro lattice (the script), cradles and seals (HANKO.md).
