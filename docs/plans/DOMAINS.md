# The seven Domains: each one question every interaction asks

South, 2026-11-01: "Do you think now is a good time to start evaluating the Domains and building them out more thoroughly WRT their
identities and impact?" And of the first answer (each domain widening one dial of the substrate): "the dials you listed are good
starters, but I think we can go much deeper than that! Think outside the box." And: "if working on them in parallel is more beneficial,
do it that way."

A plan; ruled in part (section 6). Research: `research/DOMAINS-AUDIT.md` (what is built), `research/DOMAINS-PRIOR-ART.md` (the prior art, every mechanic with its
source). It builds on SUBSTRATE.md: the pool, the vessel, the mind, the clock.

## 1. The idea: seven questions, not seven stats

Every interaction in the game, whether a blow, a cast, a step, a photograph, a dish or a deal, can be asked seven questions. Each
domain owns one, and its level is how far you can bend the answer:

| domain | its question | what it bends |
|---|---|---|
| **Ouranurgy** | **when, and where?** | where an effect arrives and when: delay, the timing of a release, reach, a passage through space |
| **Manifestation** | **in what form?** | Lachryma's state of matter: liquid (the pool), solid (Lachrymite, made things), vapour (its signature) |
| **Divination** | **what is known?** | which hidden facts you see: a telegraph, a crack to come, the true odds, a creature's make-up |
| **Psychokinesis** | **with how much force?** | momentum: push, launch, weight carried, a blow's shatter, a thrown thing's energy |
| **Possession** | **whose?** | the boundary of the self: which body, which mind, who takes a blow, who uses a verb |
| **Alteration** | **of which kind?** | conversion: a blow's type, an affliction's, a feeling's, a material's; one substrate into another |
| **Spellscription** | **under which rule?** | written conditions: triggers, vows, sigils, seals, copies; the rules themselves made a thing |

Why questions:
- **They never overlap.** Two domains can touch the same blow without fighting over it. Ouranurgy says when it lands, Psychokinesis how
  hard, Manifestation in what state, Alteration as which type, Possession on whom, Spellscription by which rule, and Divination whether
  you saw it coming.
- **They are teachable.** Each is a word a player already has.
- **They un-merge the audit's overlaps:**
  - Manifestation and Alteration: form against kind.
  - Possession and Spellscription: the seat against the script.
  - Slab and Fold: force against place.
- **Each question is real.** Each is a way of thinking about a physical-psychic world, so every domain teaches something true without
  saying so (DESIGN.md 22): thermodynamics' states of matter, momentum, the line between self and other, the conditional.

## 2. The ladder: a widening, then a relation, then depth, then a rule-break

From the prior art: growth should be **a rule per tier, not a number per tier** (Braid), with a budget whose pressure you can see (Hollow
Knight's notches) and a reserve that skill refills (the sand tanks of Sands of Time).

| levels | what it gives | the law it keeps |
|---|---|---|
| 1 to 29 | **widening**: the question's numbers grow (reach, count, duration, share) | widening is never accuracy or tempo, the attributes' (glossary) |
| 30 to 59 | **a relation**: a new way the question can be answered, a verb or a rule that did not exist | each opens by its level *and* has a feat way (arts by achievement) |
| 60 to 98 | **depth**: the relation reaches further and joins other domains' relations | a combination is two questions answered at once |
| 99 | **the rule-break**: one base rule bent for you, its source the domain at its top | the summit (ENDGAME.md); Creative mode is the god hand's |

The god powers and a duel's powers stay as they are: each is already a domain's. They are read through the domain's question in the
Codex, so a power reads as "Ouranurgy: where".

## 3. The seven, one by one

Each domain gets:
- the substrate it touches;
- its ladder;
- what feeds it (experience: asking its question well pays more, per the design law);
- the attribute it must never step on;
- its tools, the ones that ask its question most.

### 3.1 Ouranurgy: when, and where?

**Owns:** the clock as it touches an effect (delay, the release beat, when the pool begins to refill), and the space between (reach,
apertures).

| level | gives | prior art |
|---|---|---|
| 1-29 | reach (the hook, a portal's span); the regen's delay starts earlier | (built: `reach`, `still`) |
| 30 | **Postpone:** a blow taken can be put off. Its crack arrives 4 real s later, drawn as a gold-dashed crack, and a parry before it lands halves it. One at a time. | FFXIV's Doom, a delayed debt you can answer |
| 45 | **Apertures:** a pair of openings that keeps momentum through (the god hand's Portal, on foot) | Portal |
| 60 | **The fuse:** afflictions built on a creature can be held latent and released on one beat of your choosing, all at once | Chrono Trigger's combined techs; Into the Breach's ordered turn |
| 80 | a postponed blow can be sent back to where it came from, if parried | |
| 99 | **Ouranurgy's rewind** (always so called: the Raceway's rewind is practice's): the area put back as it was up to 6 real s ago, whole: every body's position, every creature's state, shots in flight, your pool and cracks. You keep only what you know. Once a game hour, at its price: the clock, a game hour's held stretch, and dissonance (section 7). | Braid; Sands of Time; Life is Strange (the memory kept); Fire Emblem's Divine Pulse |

- **Fed by:** things arriving on time: a postponed blow parried, a fuse released into a break, a portal passed at speed.
- **Never:** your own tempo (Dexterity's).
- **Tools:** the Sondelass's hook, the Crucibelle's beat, the psygun's shells that delay.

### 3.2 Manifestation: in what form?

**Owns:** Lachryma's states of matter. The pool is liquid; Lachrymite (cubes, shells, made things) is solid; a signature is its vapour.

| level | gives | prior art |
|---|---|---|
| 1-29 | a made thing's hold and hp (a creation, a ward, a wall, a called spirit); the Bottles' capacity | (built: `span`, `count`) |
| 30 | **Plating:** pool set solid on one region of the vessel, taking that region's next crack instead. The price is held off the pool's max while it stands (the held stretch), and melting it back loses a share. | Hollow Knight's notches; latent heat (a change of state costs) |
| 45 | **A made thing's state decides what breaks it:** solid shatters to Impact, liquid spreads under it and ignores it, vapour takes only the psychic types | Divinity: Original Sin 2's surfaces; Noita's matter |
| 60 | **Catch the weather:** a feeling's falling Lachryma caught as liquid in a Bottle, its feeling kept for a draught | Breath of the Wild's chemistry: the world's state is yours to use |
| 80 | a made thing can be made of a feeling: a wall of Grief's rain, a ward of Fury's sparks | |
| 99 | **A second vessel:** a clay double made from the pool, with its own cracks. It stands where you made it, takes blows as its own, and the pool it held comes home when it shatters. | |

- **Fed by:** things made that last and do their work: a plate that took a crack, a wall that held, a spirit that fought its time out.
- **Never:** the shield's size (Willpower's). Plating is a region's armour, paid from the max, never a larger shield.
- **Tools:** the Soul Brush (paint is liquid laid down), the psygun's shells (solid), the god hand (matter).

### 3.3 Divination: what is known?

**Owns:** information: what is hidden, and only *new kinds* of it, never the same thing sooner (Perception's).

| level | gives | prior art |
|---|---|---|
| 1-29 | the Figment telegraph ladder (built: 2, 8, 20, 35); a tag's depth; the forecast's hours | (built) |
| 30 | **The crack to come:** you see which region a blow will crack before it lands, and a body's shatter line (how near it is to breaking) | Slay the Spire's intents; Into the Breach's perfect information |
| 45 | **A body's make-up:** a creature's constitution and the type that bites it, read from facts you hold (photographs, the bestiary), before you ever land a blow | Outer Wilds: knowledge is the progression |
| 60 | **True odds:** world chance shown as numbers (a chest's tier, the Lockheart's wheel, a duel's flee, a crystal's shard) | Darkest Dungeon's scouting |
| 80 | **Call it:** commit to a prediction (a creature's next move, a forecast). Right, and it pays and builds a short window where your read is exact. Wrong costs nothing but the call. | Return of the Obra Dinn: the answer is committed, then checked |
| 99 | **See the next roll:** your own dice's next throw is shown before you commit to it, once a game hour | |

- **Fed by:** predictions that come true, facts appraised, a weakness found before it was struck.
- **Never:** earlier warning of what is already shown (Perception's).
- **Tools:** the Veritome, the Dreamvane (the sky read), the duel's Scan.

### 3.4 Psychokinesis: with how much force?

**Owns:** momentum: push, launch, mass carried, a blow's shatter (SUBSTRATE F5b), a thrown thing's energy. Never damage itself: skill
decides damage.

| level | gives | prior art |
|---|---|---|
| 1-29 | knockback and launch; the weight you can lift and throw (the dead `weight` key, wired) | (built: `throw`) |
| 30 | **Shatter:** your heaviest blows (charges, smashes, Guard Crush) carry shatter, a share that grows with level | Smash's KO power |
| 45 | **The catch:** a projectile parried can be held, then thrown back where you aim, with its energy and yours | Half-Life 2's gravity gun |
| 60 | **Relay:** a thrown body that strikes another passes its momentum on, a chain of launches | Katamari; Control's Launch |
| 80 | **Heft:** a carried mass held off the pool's max (visible), its throw as hard as its weight | Control; Katamari |
| 99 | **Banked motion:** your own speed stored as you move (a sprint, a slide, a fall) and released as one blow's force and shatter | Superhot's motion as the clock, inverted |

- **Fed by:** force delivered well: a launch's distance, a catch returned to its thrower, a relay's length.
- **Never:** damage, accuracy, tempo.
- **Tools:** the psygun (its push, which earns it nothing today), the kick, thrown things, the god hand's Slab.

### 3.5 Possession: whose?

**Owns:** the boundary of the self: which body acts, which mind is open, who takes a blow, whose verb is used.

| level | gives | prior art |
|---|---|---|
| 1-29 | reprogramming's slots; a decoy's pull; how many minds you can link | (built: `macro`, `song`) |
| 30 | **Receptivity:** a mind's openness shown on its body (its EmO band and its afflictions), and a possession only takes when it is open | Shin Megami Tensei's negotiation; Pokemon's capture odds |
| 45 | **The link:** a creature you hold bound shares your cracks. Each blow on either is split between you, and a blow that strikes both lands twice. | Dishonored 2's Domino |
| 60 | **Borrow a verb:** a creature's move lent to you for a while, as your sub's verb on R | Kirby; Prey's typhon powers |
| 80 | **Ride:** leave your vessel standing (open to blows) and drive a creature's body. The slip jelly mount, made any body. | Ghost Trick; Dishonored's Possession |
| 99 | **No border:** a bound spirit's pool and yours are one well | |

- **Fed by:** minds won while open, links held through a fight, a borrowed verb used well.
- **Never:** a status's duration (Focus's). Possession moves *whom* a status is on, never how long.
- **Tools:** the Veritome's reprogramming (the seat; Spellscription writes the script), the Lockheart's drain, the Crucibelle's song.

### 3.6 Alteration: of which kind?

**Owns:** conversion, the exchange rate between kinds:
- a blow's type into another;
- an affliction into another;
- a feeling into another;
- a material into another;
- and one substrate into another.

| level | gives | prior art |
|---|---|---|
| 1-29 | a draught's strength and hold; paint's reach and build | (built: `swell`, `paint`) |
| 30 | **Re-type:** one tool's blows set to a damage type of your choosing for a while (the type chart, woken by F3, made yours to play) | Pokemon's Soak and Conversion |
| 45 | **Reactions:** where two feelings' paints meet, the agate table reacts. Opposites cancel to a calm patch, and agates set a mixed weather on the ground. | Divinity: Original Sin 2's surfaces; Magicka |
| 60 | **Walk the cycle:** an affliction on a creature turned into the one its type trumps (doubt into charm, charm into confusion) | |
| 80 | **The exchange:** pool into mending, or a crack taken on purpose into pool. Instant, lossy (about 3 to 2) and capped a game hour, beside Resilience's slow, free mending. | Fullmetal Alchemist's equivalent exchange |
| 99 | **Re-make a body:** a creature's constitution changed for a while. A slip jelly made an Illusion body is broken by plain force. | |

- **Fed by:** conversions that pay: a re-typed blow on a weakness, a reaction set off, a status walked into a break.
- **Never:** mending's rate (Resilience's). The exchange is a different door: instant, lossy, capped.
- **Tools:** the Soul Brush (paint and its feelings), the Lockheart (Delirium), the press (Soul Alchemy's materials).

### 3.7 Spellscription: under which rule?

**Owns:** written conditions: triggers, vows, sigils, seals, copies. The rules themselves made into things you carry and place.

| level | gives | prior art |
|---|---|---|
| 1-29 | a sigil's count; an inscription's length; copies (a Cogitomap) | (built: `slots`; `copy` wired) |
| 30 | **The chain:** what you cast is read in order, and a chain you cannot afford keeps its trigger and drops the rest (never a dry click) | Noita's wands |
| 45 | **A standing rule:** one "when X, then Y" written on your sub tool's lent verb, fired by the event it names (when you parry, toll; when the pool drops below a quarter, drink a Bottle) | FF12's Gambits; Magic: the Gathering's triggers |
| 60 | **A vow:** a restriction written on yourself (no dash, no shield) that makes your costs cheaper while it holds, its source your seal | Hunter x Hunter's vows; a real oath's binding |
| 80 | **Rewrite a tag:** add a tag to a thing (make a wall `hookable`, a crate `hurtable`), or take one off a thing that is not a creature. A creature's tag is taken off only with Alteration as well (the pairing, section 9), and the creature goes aberrant (section 7.6). | Baba Is You; the Well's Script stage |
| 99 | **A place's law:** an inscription that changes one base rule inside its circle while it stands (shots free, no regen delay, gravity halved), visible as a sealed ring | |

- **Fed by:** rules that fire and pay: a trigger answered, a vow kept through a fight, a tag that opened a way.
- **Never:** the moveset's own timing.
- **Tools:** the Soul Brush's Celestial mode, the Veritome's macro lattice (the script), cradles and seals (HANKO.md).

## 7. The prices

South's ruling 4 makes the price the design. Every power is allowed; what keeps it in its place is what it costs. A price is always
seen before it is paid (CLARITY.md), and every one is a modifier with a source (DESIGN.md 22: base change bad, modifiers good). There
are seven, from mild to severe, and a power may stack two:

| price | what you pay | shown by | it suits |
|---|---|---|---|
| **the clock** | a cooldown, or once a game hour or game day | the power's tile rim | anything strong and short |
| **the held stretch** | pool max held while the power stands, back on release (SUBSTRATE F7) | the Lachrimeter's held stretch | anything that lasts: a plate, a bubble, a heft |
| **the toll** | a crack on a region, of your choice or the power's (the Caster's toll) | the vessel's cracks | a single great act |
| **overreach** | a cast with the pool short: see 7.1 | the bulb cracking, the screen's edge | the gamble |
| **the stake** | something put up against an outcome: cubes, a crack, a day's Luck re-roll | the stake drawn at the act | a prediction, a vow, a contract |
| **dissonance** | the island notices: the Dissonance Level climbs, and rejection answers with stronger Figments (sentinels when built) | the Sight's Dissonance arc | anything that warps the place (time, law, kind) |
| **aberrance** | the warped creature goes aberrant: see 7.6 | the aberrant's look | rewriting a creature's own rules |

### 7.1 Overreach: high risk, high reward

**Overflow** is too much Lachryma (the pool pushed past full). **Overreach** is the mirror: too little.

- **What it allows:** any power cast with the pool short of its cost, instead of a dry click.
- **What you pay:** the shortfall is drawn from the vessel instead, at the shield's own rate (35 pool to one full blow's crack:
  `vessel.js` SHIELD), so the vessel's one rule prices it.
- **What it gives:** the power at **double** strength. This is the reward, and the reason to do it on purpose.
- **What it risks:** for 6 real s after, every blow on you cracks double (Hollow Knight's overcharm: more power, more hurt). A blow with
  shatter in that window can end the fight.
- **Shown:** the Lachrimeter's bulb crazing, the vessel's cracks glowing in the film, the screen's edge gone raw. One log line:
  "You overreach."
- **The law it keeps:** nothing is refused for want of pool. A dry click remains only on a basic shot, which draws on ambient Lachryma
  (SUBSTRATE F2).
- **Feeds:** the domain of the power overreached, at x1.5 if you live through the window. A high-risk play pays its own domain
  (design law: doing it well pays more).
- **Prior art:** Hollow Knight's overcharming; Dark Souls' Pyromancy at 1 hp; Bloodborne's rally (the risk window is where the reward
  is won back); Kakegurui's whole premise.

### 7.2 The clock

A power's cooldown runs on the game clock where the world's clock bends it (a game hour's cooldown runs faster at a Shrine's rest) and
on the real clock in a fight. The rule: the stronger the power, the longer the clock. At 99, the clock is once a game hour.

### 7.3 The held stretch

Already the substrate's (F7): pool max held off while the power stands, paid back on release, and a share lost if broken. It suits
anything with a duration, and makes duration a choice: hold the plate and fight on a smaller pool, or drop it.

### 7.4 The stake

What Divination and Spellscription bet with. A stake is put up openly at the act (cubes, a crack, Luck's re-roll for the game day) and
returned with a winning, or lost. It is the casino's own grammar brought onto the field, and Raku's odds are its table.

### 7.5 Dissonance

The god hand's price (GODHAND.md 10) made general: a power that warps the island (Ouranurgy's rewind, a law written on a place, a creature's
kind rewritten) raises the Dissonance Level as a miracle does. So the island's answer, rejection, is how the world pushes back on a
player who bends it often. One of Ouranurgy's rewinds is a step; three in a game hour are an island alarmed.

### 7.6 The aberrance line (South's ruling 4)

A creature is aberrant when its rules have been broken. Today the world breaks them (a Figment gone wrong, `game.duelFoes`). After
this ruling, **you can too**:

- **The line:** a creature with any of its own rules rewritten by you goes aberrant at once:
  - a tag taken off (`hurtable` off: the untouchable Figment);
  - its constitution remade past its span (Alteration 99 held past a game hour);
  - a law of Spellscription's written onto it.
- **What follows:** it is dealt with as an aberrant. It takes no blow in the world, and its pull opens a puzzle duel (PUZZLE-DUELS.md,
  ABERRANTS.md). The power did what it said; the island's answer is that the creature is no longer of the island.
- **The gamble:** a made aberrant is a duel on your terms, and an aberrant's drops and learned skill (ABERRANTS.md 8, 9) are real.
  So making one is a door as well as a price.
- **The price that keeps it fair:**
  - a made aberrant drops at half (the island did not make it, so it holds less of the island);
  - a made aberrant raises the Dissonance Level a whole step;
  - a made aberrant never rolls shiny;
  - and the economy reads it as its own faucet (`scripts/economy.mjs`, a row capped a game day), so a farm of them is measured
    before it is built.
- **Prior art:** Dishonored's chaos (power used freely, the world worse for it); Pokemon's shiny and alpha variants; Breath of the
  Wild's chemistry (the system says yes to what you try).

## 8. What else they could do

A bank, not a commitment: the rungs of section 3 are the ladder, and these are the powers it could also hold, each with its price
from section 7. A rung is swapped for one of these only when the swap is measured to read better in play. Prior art beside each.

### 8.1 Ouranurgy (when, and where?)

- **The echo:** your last 5 real s of movement replayed by a clay ghost of you, which presses plates, takes a blow and fires what you
  fired. Clock and held stretch. (Braid's Shadow; Ratchet & Clank's Clank.)
- **Stasis:** a bubble where game time runs at a fifth for everything but you, shots inside slowed to beads. Held stretch, dissonance.
  (Braid's ring; Superhot.)
- **Stored force:** a thing frozen in place takes every blow you land on it as momentum, and flies with all of it when released.
  Clock. (Breath of the Wild's Stasis.) It joins Psychokinesis (section 9).
- **The anchor:** set a mark and recall yourself to it within 8 real s, along your own path (Ouranurgy's rewind's first step). Clock.
  (Overwatch's Tracer.)
- **Hurry:** a thing's own clock run fast: a seed grown, a blot ripened early (and its blotling fought at your hour), a creation's
  crumbling done, a crystal regrown, a weather spell ended. Held stretch, dissonance. (Animal Crossing's time travel, priced.)
- **Rewind a thing:** one object put back alone (a smashed pot whole, a fallen bridge risen, a creature's last windup undone), while
  everything else runs on. Clock. (Braid's green objects; Prince of Persia.)
- **A shortcut's fold:** a door carried to another place you have stood in, for a game hour. Dissonance. (Antichamber; Portal.)

### 8.2 Manifestation (in what form?)

- **Footholds:** a step of Lachrymite set in the air where you look, for the core movement to land on (a modifier in the world, never
  a change to the jump). Held stretch. (Mirror's Edge's runner vision, made solid.)
- **Freeze the weather:** a feeling's falling Lachryma frozen where it falls, its drops hanging as platforms and cover. Held stretch.
  (Celeste's crystals; Super Mario Galaxy's Ice Flower.)
- **Set paint solid:** a painted road stood up into a wall, or a wall laid down into paint. Clock.
- **Condensed overflow:** overflow on foot set into shells for the psygun's chambers, as the palm die sets it into cubes. Free, by
  the overflow's own cap.
- **Veil the signature:** your signature turned to vapour, so a mind that senses Lachryma (a Lantern Wisp, a Figment that drinks)
  loses you. It is stealth's Manifestation (STEALTH.md). Held stretch.
- **Sublimate:** a Figment's glaze shell turned to vapour, skipping the crackle break; the drain gets less, the fight ends sooner.
  Toll.
- **A body of glass:** the base material changed for a fight (clay cracks, glass crazes and refracts Illusion back). Toll.
  (REGISTERS.md's base materials, made a stance.)

### 8.3 Divination (what is known?)

- **The watch:** a Figment's watch shown before it turns: a countdown on its tag to when its head comes up (stealth's `vigilAt` is
  seeded, so the future is known). (Metal Gear Solid's Soliton radar, in time instead of space.)
- **Future ghosts:** every creature's next 1.5 real s drawn as a ghost of its body. Held stretch. (Into the Breach; Hotline Miami's
  planning, shown.)
- **Read a mind:** a creature's mind's top three wants and their weights, read from its reasoner (`creatures/ai/`): why it does
  what it does. (Shadow of Mordor's Nemesis cards; The Sims' wants.)
- **A Cogitomap's preview:** a Well's floors seen before you go down, its haul and FOE named. Stake (cubes). (Darkest Dungeon's scouting.)
- **The folk's tell:** whether a line of the folk's is true, shown on their body (Nebula Bluff's tell, out of the casino).
  (L.A. Noire; Disco Elysium's Drama.)
- **The odds of you:** your own chance of shattering in the next 10 real s, as a number, from the cracks, the shield and what winds up
  round you. (XCOM's hit chance, turned on yourself.)

### 8.4 Psychokinesis (with how much force?)

- **Throw a creature:** a stunned body lifted and thrown as a thing, its energy a thrown hit's (`world/impacts.js`), striking its kin.
  Toll for the big ones. (Control's Launch; Half-Life 2.)
- **The island's matter on foot:** Slab and Scoop lent to the Courier, the ground torn up and flung. Held stretch, dissonance.
  (Control; the god hand's powers, borrowed.)
- **A turning field:** shots inside a ring round you slowed and curved off. Held stretch. (Max Payne's bullet time, made a place;
  Control's Shield.)
- **The wall splat:** a launch into a wall breaks the body against it, shatter doubled. Free: the skill is the angle.
  (Tekken's wall splat; Smash's meteor.)
- **The fall's bank:** a landing's force kept and let out as a ground ring, scaled by the height fallen. Free. (Banked motion's
  first step; Zelda's ground pound.)
- **Orbit:** debris lifted round you as armour that is thrown off by blows. Held stretch. (Control's Shield; Katamari.)

### 8.5 Possession (whose?)

- **Possess a thing:** the Courier's soul passed into an object along a line of things in reach, each with its own verb (a crate
  falls, a lamp swings, a gate opens). Traversal by things, not by the body. Clock. (Ghost Trick.)
- **Swap:** your place and a creature's traded in an instant. Clock. (Mario Odyssey's capture, inverted; Gravity Rush.)
- **The scapegoat:** your next blow taken lands on a bound creature instead. Stake (the bond). (Final Fantasy's Cover, reversed.)
- **A borrowed sight:** see through a creature's senses, its vision cone and what it heard. Held stretch. (Ghost Trick; Dishonored's
  Dark Vision; Splinter Cell's camera hop.)
- **The puppet:** a Figment's scatter held for 10 real s as a body you drive, one blow its last. Clock.
- **Your mood as the weather** (an alternative 99): the Courier's mental state set as the place's weather, the island's mood yours.
  Dissonance, heavily. (Weathering With You.)

### 8.6 Alteration (of which kind?)

Feelings are downstream of the damage types and afflictions (South, ruling of SUBSTRATE 3). So Alteration changes the upstream
things, and the feeling and the behaviour follow:

- **Walk the affliction:** the 60 rung; a creature charmed into confusion behaves as the confused do, and its feeling shifts with it.
- **Pain into a draught:** a crack taken turned into a draught of the blow's type's affliction, so the next blows you land build it
  faster. Free: the crack is the price.
- **Refine in the field:** a slick of crude refined on the spot into a feeling's Lachryma (crude "refined, takes a feeling again":
  ECON.crude), drinkable. Capped a game day, since crude is cargo and money (ECONOMY.md).
- **Transmute matter:** sand into glass (a bridge over the Dunes' fulgurite way), clay into stone, ice into water. Held stretch.
  (Fullmetal Alchemist; Noita.)
- **Trade natures:** two creatures' constitutions swapped (with Possession: section 9).
- **Make an aberrant:** with Spellscription (section 7.6).

### 8.7 Spellscription (under which rule?)

- **A contract with the folk:** a deal written and sealed with your seal (deliver this by dusk, be paid double; fail, owe the stake).
  Stake. (The Witcher's contracts; Kakegurui's IOUs.)
- **A seal on a creature:** a rule stamped on a body: when it does X, Y befalls it (when it casts, it is stunned; when it flees, it is
  marked). Clock. (Okami's brush techniques; Naruto's seals.)
- **A cradle's macro:** the macro lattice written into a cradle, so a gate, a lift or a ship runs your script (HANKO.md).
- **Spoken neuralese:** a word of neuralese typed on the chat line as a verb (`game.chat.add`): the 127 roots you have glossed by
  ostraca become spells you say. The player learns a real constructed language, Toki Pona's way, and the language is the lesson
  (DESIGN.md 22). (Okami's brush; Magicka's elements; Hey You, Pikachu!)
- **A copy of a rule:** a foe skill seen copied onto a scroll and cast once (a Cogitomap's copy, of a rule). Stake. (Final Fantasy's
  Blue Magic; Kirby.)
- **A wager with the System:** a bet written on your own play (this Well floor without a crack) for its odds in cubes (the casino's
  Luck, outside it). Stake. (Kakegurui; the Sims' wants as bets.)

## 11. How a domain grows: shu, ha, ri

South, 2026-11-01: "Continue to think hard about the full progression/ ability unlocks/ training methodologies of each of the domains."

The ladder of section 2 is the oldest pedagogy of the martial arts and the tea room: **shu-ha-ri** (守破離: keep the form, break the
form, leave the form; Aikido's and the Urasenke tea school's). It gives every band of levels a feeling, not only a number:

| band | levels | the time it takes (one domain, middling / masterful) | the feeling | what it gives |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **shu** (keep) | 1 to 29 | 15 h / 3 h to 30 | the question learned by asking it | widening: the question's numbers grow |
| **ha** (break) | 30 to 59 | 86 h / 17 h to 60 | the form broken: a new way to answer | the relations, one at 30 and one at 45 |
| **ri** (leave) | 60 to 98 | 300 h / 60 h to 99 | the form left behind: two questions in one | depth (60, 80), and the pairings at 60 in both |
| **the summit** | 99 | | the question answered for good | the rule-break, a title |

The times are the curve's (`expAt`, domains.js: 97,404 EXP to 30, 552,013 to 60, 1,935,567 to 99) at PACE's six acts a real minute, and
they're one domain's alone. All seven are trained by different play, so the seven 30s come over about 100 hours of varied middling play,
and over 21 of masterful play. The design law holds: **skill skips grind** (the feat way opens a rung years early, section 12) and
**grind closes the gap** (every act pays something, and the level opens it anyway).

What the bands are NOT:
- A band never gates the core movement, a tool or a mode. Every domain's question can be asked from the first minute; the band says how
  far it bends.
- A level is never lost. No decay, no respec: OSRS's promise, and the level is the proof.
- A band never shows a bar to grind. The Codex shows the level and the next rung, with its two ways (STATUS shelf).

## 12. How a rung opens: two ways, then the lesson

Every rung above the widening is an art, and **arts open only by achievement** (DESIGN.md 22). So each rung is an achievement over the
ledger with its two ways (Monster Hunter's and OSRS's level-or-quest gates):

- **The count way:** the domain's level reaches the rung's (`exp.<domain>` in the ledger, so it's retroactive like everything else).
- **The feat way:** the rung's own act done well, before you have it, n times. Each feat is the rung's idea done the hard way without its
  help: Postpone's feat is ten parries in the last fifth of a window (time held to its edge), Plating's a fight where the shield took
  six blows and never broke (the pool held as armour by hand). It's how a god-gamer gets a rung at level 12 (OSRS's quest skips, Sekiro's
  Esoteric Texts found early).
- **Its achievement tier** is the rung's band: 15 Medium, 30 and 45 Hard, 60 Elite, 80 Master, 99 Grandmaster (section 16 moved the first relations to 15). The 99's awards a title (the
  one pop-up: the domain's own, Espada's to name; plain for now: "Ouranurgist" and so on).

**The lesson** (shu-ha-ri's first beat: the teacher shows once). The moment a rung opens, the log says it in the System's voice, one line
of what it does and one of how ("Postpone: a blow that gets past your shield lands 4 seconds late. Parry before it lands to halve it.").
The first five uses each pay the domain's masterful act ("the kata"): Hades' first-time bonuses, FFXIV's job quest at every rung,
never a tutorial (DESIGN.md 22: the mechanic is the lesson).

**Wearing it.** A passive and a reaction are always on once open (a rung is the domain's own nature, not a charm). An **action** is worn
on the art key in the action slot the class arts use (CLASS-ARTS.md; FF Tactics' one action set): one action at a time, a class's or a
domain's, so choosing a domain's action is a real build choice (Hollow Knight's notches). A rung's price (section 7) is always shown
before it's paid.
