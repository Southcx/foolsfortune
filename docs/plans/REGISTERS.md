> 'Cyber/digital' and what things get touched by 'Psychological' aesthetics, nomenclature, and motifs... Fool's Fortune is much, much
> bigger than just Anagami Island." And on the Courier: "Because the Courier is like a robot... base materials/textures as a means of

| register | what it is | its motifs | the test |

| (dev) | process words, never seen in play | none | not a motif: out of scope |

**The rule.** A thing's *name* (in code, in the UI's label) comes from the register of its widest reach. A universal mechanic gets a
Psyche or System name, and each island may *dress* it in its own register: the ostracon is Anagami's dress for a neuralese word found,
and Margarite's would be something else. The vessel's words must be **material-neutral**, so that the same word is true of clay and of
glass (crack, shatter, made whole, vessel: yes; "bisque", "slip": no).

**The owner's ruling, as a system.** The Courier's **base material** (working name) is the vessel's material: clay at the start
(Kaolin Anagami's format), and others earned where other islands adapted the technology (a Glass Courier). It is customization, kept
wherever you go, and it shows where you have been and what you have done. That makes it a new sink and a new unlock track (mine to spec,
once named), and Calissa's to dress (a material = a base shader and a crack pattern).

## 3. Where clay bleeds: the findings

Twelve entries put Anagami's clay where a universal or carried thing should speak its own register, and one is a name collision.
Section 4 is the list to rule on.

## 4. To rule on (one line each; the default is Dovina's recommendation)

1. **vessel** says "the Courier's clay body". → "the Courier's body, of its **base material** (clay first: Kaolin Anagami's format)".
   The word *vessel* stays: it is true of glass. The **base material** is named in the glossary (Espada's word to rule).
2. **glaze / the kiln** for the Courier: "a colour fired onto a region at the kiln". → Keep *glaze* as the vessel's coat, since kilns fire
   enamel onto glass and metal too, so the word holds for clay and glass. Each island gets its own station that fires one: Anagami's
   kiln; others to name. "At the kiln" becomes "at a finishing station (on Anagami, the kiln)".
3. **crack / MEND at the kiln.** Same as 2: the mend happens at any island's station, and the crack pattern follows the base material
   (clay cracks, glass crazes, nacre chips). That is Calissa's to draw.
4. **the island's matter: "clay, sand, Lachryma, crude".** → "the ZoI's layered ground: **the island's own materials** (on Anagami,
   clay and sand), Lachryma and crude". Code: `clay` and `sand` stay as Anagami's two layers for now. A new island brings its own pair
   (the field's layer names become data). Mine.
5. **a creation: "voxel clay".** → "voxel matter of the island's ground material". Its look follows the island it is raised on. Mine
   and Calissa's.
6. **the god power Fire** (a creation "fired"). → **Set** (psyche: a thought set; "set in stone"). On Anagami it looks like firing.
   Mine.
7. **the god power Rift collides with "a portal / a rift"** (the caster shader: a portal is a linked pair, a rift is one alone). My
   Rift is a linked pair. → Rename the power **Portal** and dress it with Calissa's `Portal` shader. Mine.
8. **a domain's look: "a domain's kiln glaze"** (Calissa's `DOMAIN_LOOK`, and my `DOMAIN_GLAZE`). The domains are psyche, and they show
   in the Codex (System). → Keep the colours: they were chosen to stay off the feelings' hues, and that reason holds. Drop "kiln glaze"
   as their *identity*: a domain's look becomes its colour and its sign, and Anagami's tiles are one dressing of it. Calissa's call on
   the look; mine on `DOMAIN_GLAZE` (it goes when `DOMAIN_LOOK` lands).
9. **the folk: "the clay people ... earthenware < stoneware < porcelain < the Court"**, and the homonym row **tier: the folk's**. → "the
   folk" is any island's people who speak. Anagami's are the **clay folk**, and their tiers are Anagami's. Margarite's folk have their
   own, Espada's to write. Espada's.
10. **The STATUS shelf's bisque and kintsugi gold.** It is the Codex: System. → The radar in the Codex's own colours. "The World" keeps
    gold (the tarot card, and it already reads as the top), but the label is "gold", not "kintsugi". Mine (done when ruled).
11. **the Heavenly Kiln** (the Spirit Garden's tribulation). The Spirit Garden is inside your Pneuka Jar, so it travels with you: it is
    the vessel's, not Anagami's. → Its dressing follows your **base material**: a clay Courier's garden fires in a kiln, a glass one's
    in a furnace. Or rename it to something psyche, such as **the Heavenly Crucible**. Owner and Espada.
12. **an ostracon / the Wake Whistle (a clay whistle) / Strawman (stitched by Pip)**: Anagami's dress on universal mechanics (a word
    found, a way out of a Well, a training dummy). → Keep them on Anagami. The *mechanic* names in code should be neutral where they are
    shared: `ostraca.js` → a neuralese find, dressed as an ostracon on Anagami. The Petra and Espada rows are for when a second island
    is built, not now.
13. **sink: "(the glazes, the Spirit Garden)"**. Base materials join the sinks once ruled. Mine.
