# The Wheel: a complete map of feeling (the owner, 2026-10-05)

Kept by Dovina. The owner's ask: think forward to a complete emotional wheel, after **Plutchik's wheel of emotions** (as presented by
6 Seconds, 2025: <https://www.6seconds.org/2025/02/06/plutchik-wheel-emotions/>), apply it to the existing systems, and, with the
divisions, determine what has to expand. A proposal for their input; nothing is built yet.

## Plutchik, in brief

- **Eight primary emotions in four opposed pairs:** joy against sadness (connect, withdraw), trust against disgust (embrace, reject),
  fear against anger (get small, get big), surprise against anticipation (jump back, examine closely).
- **Intensity** rises toward the centre of the wheel. Three rings per petal: serenity, joy, ecstasy; acceptance, trust, admiration;
  apprehension, fear, terror; distraction, surprise, amazement; pensiveness, sadness, grief; boredom, disgust, loathing; annoyance,
  anger, rage; interest, anticipation, vigilance.
- **Dyads:** two adjacent petals blend into a feeling of their own: joy and trust, love; trust and fear, submission; fear and surprise,
  awe; surprise and sadness, disapproval; sadness and disgust, remorse; disgust and anger, contempt; anger and anticipation,
  aggressiveness; anticipation and joy, optimism.
- What 6 Seconds stresses: **several feelings at once**, every feeling has a message, and left unchecked a feeling intensifies.

## Our five, on the wheel

The five aspects we have are five of Plutchik's eight petals, and two of his opposed pairs are already ours:

| Ours (shown order) | Plutchik's petal | Its rings (mild, basic, intense) | Its opposite |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Wonder** | surprise | distraction, surprise, amazement | anticipation = **Desire** (ours) |
| **Mirth** | joy | serenity, joy, ecstasy | sadness = **Grief** (ours) |
| **Desire** | anticipation | interest, anticipation, vigilance | surprise = **Wonder** (ours) |
| **Grief** | sadness | pensiveness, sadness, grief | joy = **Mirth** (ours) |
| **Dread** | fear | apprehension, fear, terror | anger: **missing** |

(Grief is literally Plutchik's word for intense sadness, and Dread is the fear petal: the canon was already on the wheel.)

**Missing: three petals.** Trust (acceptance, trust, admiration), Disgust (boredom, disgust, loathing), Anger (annoyance, anger, rage).
With them the wheel closes: Dread meets its opposite (Anger), and Trust and Disgust are a pair of their own.

## Two kinds of thing, kept apart (the determination's backbone)

- **Feelings** (states: what a mind feels now). These are the wheel: the aspects of Lachryma, the weather, the crude, the fish, the
  creatures' moods, the songs. **These expand to the wheel.**
- **Forces and characters** (what a blow is; who a mind is). The five damage types and their statuses (the trump cycle, the
  Law-Chaos line), the five traits of temperament (OCEAN), the seven domains, the seven attributes. **These stay as they are**: they
  are not feelings, and each is a closed, balanced set already.

## What the wheel gives the systems (the proposal)

1. **Intensity rings, everywhere a strength already is.** The weather's strength, a creature's mood, a cask's grade of purity: mild,
   basic, intense, named by Plutchik's rings (a drizzle of pensiveness, a downpour of grief). The **mental state** (Stoic to Prismatic)
   is intensity already: Plutchik's centre is our Prismatic (every feeling at its strongest, all colours at once). Cheap: names and
   thresholds on numbers we have.
2. **Opposites, as a mechanic.** Opposed feelings cancel: pressing a material of the opposite aspect pulls the soul colour back across
   the wheel; a lure of the opposite aspect repels a fish; mirth weather softens a grief-built status. Two pairs work today
   (Wonder/Desire, Mirth/Grief); the third and fourth need the missing petals.
3. **Several feelings at once.** A creature (and an island) can feel two at once: its strongest and its second. That is the
   **dyad**: a mind in mirth and desire is optimistic, in dread and wonder awed. Dyads are where the mental ecology gets rich: a pack
   in *contempt* (disgust and anger) behaves unlike one in *awe*. Rare blended weathers are dyads too (an aurora and a pall together: awe).
4. **Soul Alchemy's wheel is Plutchik's wheel.** Plutchik drew his as a colour wheel; ours already is one. The seven attributes sit
   on it, and the eight petals can sit under them, so pressing walks the soul through feelings as well as hues (Calissa: do the
   petal colours and our aspect colours agree?).
5. **The three missing petals, added as aspects (staged).** Trust, Disgust, Anger become Lachryma aspects: their own fish, crude
   grades, weathers, songs. Each maps to the damage type nearest its place (a closed set of five types fed by eight feelings: some types
   fed by two). This is the expensive step: every five-of-a-kind that is a feeling grows to eight.

## The owner's principle: cycles of expansion and contraction (2026-10-05)

"Emotional mixes help simplify things: we don't want mental overload juggling too many emotions." So the wheel grows the world, never
the load on the player: **expand** (the eight feelings, the rings, the agates exist in the world) and then **contract** (what a player
reads at any one moment stays small). The rules that contract it:
- **A place carries few feelings.** An island or a Well has a climate of its own few (Anagami the five, Margarite its faith and mirth,
  Entropolis's underground its fury and gall); the new feelings arrive with new places, a few at a time.
- **A mind shows one feeling, or one agate.** Never three: the strongest feeling and, if close, the second, folded into one named
  agate (awe, love, contempt, remorse) that reads as a single thing.
- **Rings are adjectives**, not new names (Espada): intensity changes how a feeling looks, sounds and pays, never how many there are.
- **Each expansion is followed by a contraction pass**: after the new feelings land, prune what does not earn its place, merge what
  reads alike, and keep the shown order short.
