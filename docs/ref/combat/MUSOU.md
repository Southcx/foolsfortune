# Musou melee reference: snappy, flowing, elegant sword combat

Scope: Omega Force / Koei Tecmo Warriors lineage (Dynasty Warriors, Samurai Warriors, Hyrule Warriors, Warriors Orochi as the Warriors-engine reference). One Piece: Pirate Warriors 3/4 and Dynasty Warriors: Gundam are covered where sources exist.

Source quality: "PRIMARY" = publisher manual or official Koei page. "WIKI" = Koei/Zelda/Wikipedia pages. "PRESS" = interview or review. "SNIPPET" = only a search-result summary was read; the page itself was blocked (HTTP 402/403) or not fetched. "UNSOURCED" = common knowledge, no citation found.

Blocked during research: koei.fandom.com PW4 page and Lu Meng movesets (402); strategywiki PW3/PW4 controls (403); twinfinite PW4 dodge guide (403); zeldadungeon Hyrule Warriors combat article (403). Frame data: none found for any title. No frame numbers are given below.

## 1. Combo grammar (strings, charge branches, branch types, air, dash)

- Basic attack strings are chainable: "The most basic move, which can be chained together for consecutive attacks." Charge attack: "A more powerful attack" that "opens varied follow-ups when chained from Normal Attacks." (WO4 manual, PRIMARY) https://www.koeitecmoeurope.com/manual/wo4/2500.html
- Hyper attacks (Samurai Warriors characters): chaining a Hyper into a Normal makes the Hyper stronger; "this attack can be blocked by officers." (WO4 manual, PRIMARY) https://www.koeitecmoeurope.com/manual/wo4/2500.html
- Switch combo: switching character "immediately after executing a Charge Attack or a Hyper Attack" keeps the chain going and, after a Charge, creates damaging tornadoes. (WO4 manual, PRIMARY) https://www.koeitecmoeurope.com/manual/wo4/2500.html
- Dynasty Warriors 9 branch types: Flow Attack (Square) = combo strings "that change depending on an enemy's state"; Trigger Attack (R1 + button) = powerful state-changers: R1+Square stuns, R1+Triangle launches into the air, R1+Cross knocks down; Reactive (Triangle) = prompted/situational attacks (Counter, Separating, Dash, Smash of a mid-air enemy into the ground, Guard Break, Assault on an unaware enemy, Finish); Special R1+Circle = "Powerful attacks unique to each officer." (DW9 official system page, PRIMARY) https://www.koeitecmoamerica.com/dw9/system_action.html
- DW9 sample flow: "Guard Break -> Combo -> Aerial Launch -> Final Blows -> Ground Slam", with "aim Flow Attacks at the enemy to perform desired multiple action sequences" after landing a Trigger. (DW9 official, PRIMARY) https://www.koeitecmoamerica.com/dw9/system_action.html
- DW9 state combo (press coverage): Trigger opens a combo that may be followed by a Flow string, which can combo into another Trigger; the Square string changes depending on whether a Trigger preceded it. (PRESS, SNIPPET) https://twinfinite.net/ps4/dynasty-warriors-9-changes/3/ and https://www.playstationlifestyle.net/2017/09/06/dynasty-warriors-9-state-combo-system-explained-detail/
- Crowd clear vs single-target: Flow strings let the player pick a combo for crowd control or single-target damage (fan wiki summary). (WIKI/SNIPPET) https://koei.fandom.com/wiki/Lu_Meng/Movesets (page blocked; search summary only)
- Gundam (Musha / Unicorn Gundam wiki): charge combinations from C2 onward can be canceled into a Boost Attack; Unicorn Gundam's Boost Attack is a shield rush; the C1 branch was NOT found for the Gundam titles. (WIKI, SNIPPET) https://breezewiki.discard.no/koei/wiki/Musha_Gundam and https://koei.fandom.com/wiki/Unicorn_Gundam
- Gundam series combo: "four combo chains," moveset determined by the mecha, not the pilot; Boost Charge = forward rush after specific combo commands, follow-ups often aerial. (SNIPPET, source unclear) https://mechabay.com/dynasty-warriors-gundam
- Weapons: from DW3 on, higher-level weapons "extend their attack chain." (WIKI) https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dynasty_Warriors
- One Piece PW4 air combat: called the biggest addition by reviewers; a ground string can be carried into the air via a mid-combo jump; an air-juggling launcher is triggered by pressing X during a string (Koei fan wiki summary). Character air proficiency varies (long strings for Marco, single air attacks for Whitebeard). (SNIPPET) https://koei.fandom.com/wiki/One_Piece:_Pirate_Warriors_4 (402, not fetched); review context https://gamingtrend.com/reviews/straw-hat-pirates-set-sail-one-piece-pirate-warriors-4-review
- PW4 special attacks: R1 + X / O / Square / Triangle on PS4 (SNIPPET) https://twinfinite.net/guides/one-piece-pirate-warriors-4-special-attacks/ (not fetched)
- PW3: R2 mapped to "Kizuna Rush" in the controls table; face-button combo mapping not visible in the snippet. (SNIPPET) https://strategywiki.org/wiki/One_Piece:_Pirate_Warriors_3/Controls (403)
- Hyrule Warriors combat: "brute force combo-strings" is the critic's term for the moment-to-moment combat, and Omega Force handled core design with Team Ninja helping on one-on-one combat. (WIKI, quoting Simon Parkin's review) https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hyrule_Warriors
- Hyrule Warriors and the context system: "context-based combat system," character abilities change with the weapon (WIKI) https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hyrule_Warriors
- UNSOURCED: The C1..C6 letter notation for the Dynasty Warriors chain (Square x N then Triangle). The Gundam and DW9 sources above use other names.

## 2. Cancel rules and buffering

- Power-type characters cancel certain moves by blocking; speed-type cancel by jumping: "Certain moves can be canceled by using a block." / "Certain moves can be canceled by jumping." (WO4 manual, PRIMARY) https://www.koeitecmoeurope.com/manual/wo4/2500.html
- Speed-type can jump again while airborne and are invincible during that move. (WO4 manual, PRIMARY) https://www.koeitecmoeurope.com/manual/wo4/2500.html
- Musou: "You are invincible while in the middle of a Musou Attack." (WO4 manual, PRIMARY) https://www.koeitecmoeurope.com/manual/wo4/2500.html
- Hyrule Warriors / Warriors dodge: dodging is used to avoid attacks and cancel attack animations (Zelda Wiki says Warriors-series dodges cancel attacks; the Perfect Dodge detail is tagged [verification needed] on the page). (WIKI) https://zeldawiki.wiki/wiki/Dodging and https://zeldawiki.wiki/wiki/Evading
- A Warriors-series wiki tip: dodging during an attack cancels it and resets stance (from an Age of Imprisonment-era wiki entry). (WIKI, SNIPPET) https://zeldawiki.wiki/wiki/Dodge
- Age of Calamity: a TV Tropes entry says nearly every character can dodge-cancel out of an attack, including canned animations. (SNIPPET, low-authority) https://www.tvtropes.org/pmwiki/pmwiki.php/GameBreaker/HyruleWarriorsAgeOfCalamity
- Age of Calamity Hestu fan guide: he can dodge at any point in an attack animation and resume walking almost at once. (SNIPPET, fan guide) https://twinfinite.net/guides/hyrule-warriors-age-of-calamity-flurry-rush-how/ (not fetched)
- Dodge-cancel caveat: do not hold the dodge button when trying to dodge-cancel (SegmentNext). (SNIPPET) https://segmentnext.com/guides/hyrule-warriors/
- PW4 dash: reviewer summary says the dash lets the player cancel out of a combo to start anew; the claim that almost any animation can be canceled with a dash came from a low-quality site and is discounted. (SNIPPET, mixed quality) https://gamingtrend.com/feature/reviews/straw-hat-pirates-set-sail-one-piece-pirate-warriors-4-review/
- Input buffer windows: NOT FOUND in any source. Frame-level cancel windows: NOT FOUND.
- UNSOURCED: Warriors-style "press the next input during the recovery to continue the string" buffering is common knowledge, not documented here.

## 3. Animation chaining, advance per hit, tracking, attack speed

- Flow strings change with enemy state (DW9, see 1). Chaining across types is the documented design: Trigger -> Flow -> Trigger (DW9, PRIMARY) https://www.koeitecmoamerica.com/dw9/system_action.html
- Attack-chain length is a weapon property (DW3 onward) (WIKI) https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dynasty_Warriors
- Switch combos keep a chain alive across characters (WO4 manual, PRIMARY) https://www.koeitecmoeurope.com/manual/wo4/2500.html
- Per-hit root motion, tracking/turning toward targets between hits, attack-speed scaling: NOT FOUND in any source read. Treat as unsourced.
- Kinetic chain and phase-fusion principles (general action, not Musou; Japanese): the "preparation / main action / follow-through" phases should fuse so energy does not appear from nowhere; the energy starts at the ground and travels through joints; deliberately breaking the chain reads as a character trait (a master throws the punch first and lets the body catch up; a brawler delays it). (CEDEC 2026 session report, GameBusiness.jp, PRESS, Japanese) https://www.gamebusiness.jp/article/2026/08/31/27830.html
- Same article: combo construction by sketching the weapon tip's trajectory first, then fitting matching actions and linking them with weight shifts; each action split into parts with a theme, avoiding duplicated directions or techniques. (PRESS, Japanese) https://www.gamebusiness.jp/article/2026/08/31/27830.html
- Same article, weapon weight: a greatsword is drawn to the body axis, lifted with momentum, then the downswing uses gravity and the body follows into a low stance; a sword sweep carried far behind the body reads as showmanship within realism. (PRESS, Japanese) https://www.gamebusiness.jp/article/2026/08/31/27830.html

## 4. Impact presentation (hitstop, launch, camera, trails, crowds)

- Enemy launches: DW9 Trigger R1+Triangle launches into the air; Aerial Launch is a step in a sequence; a mid-air enemy can be slammed into the ground by the Reactive Smash. (DW9, PRIMARY) https://www.koeitecmoamerica.com/dw9/system_action.html
- Airborne enemies take extra damage from technique-type characters (WO4 manual, PRIMARY) https://www.koeitecmoeurope.com/manual/wo4/2500.html
- Knockback: power-type characters are not knocked back by standard or ranged attacks (WO4 manual, PRIMARY) https://www.koeitecmoeurope.com/manual/wo4/2500.html
- Hit-count scaling: speed-type characters keep the hit count rising longer and get bonus damage at 300 and 1,000 thresholds (WO4 manual, PRIMARY) https://www.koeitecmoeurope.com/manual/wo4/2500.html
- Camera: third-person, "with the camera set behind the player as they engage the enemy forces." (WIKI) https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dynasty_Warriors
- Camera complaint in PW4 reviews: the camera struggles to keep up when the player zips around (PRESS, SNIPPET) https://gamingtrend.com/reviews/straw-hat-pirates-set-sail-one-piece-pirate-warriors-4-review
- Crowd scale and enemy AI: Omega Force aimed at a 10,000-soldier on-screen count for Origins; enemy groups act on their own in response to battle (producer Tomohiko Sho, TGS 2024 press summary). (PRESS, secondary) https://automaton-media.com/en/news/dynasty-warriors-origins-devs-aimed-to-put-as-many-enemies-as-the-ps5-could-handle-on-screen/
- Origins design: "go kill the player" instruction for enemy design; combat tuned to balance offense and defense (PRESS, secondary) https://automaton-media.com/en/news/dynasty-warriors-origins-developers-were-instructed-to-go-kill-the-player-when-designing-enemies/
- Hitstop length on crowds vs officers: NOT FOUND. Ragdolling of crowd soldiers: NOT FOUND. Trails and screen effects in Musou: NOT FOUND. All UNSOURCED.
- UNSOURCED (common knowledge): Musou games typically use very short hitstop on soldiers and longer on officers; soldiers are cheap, staggered, and cleared in bulk, while officers guard and block. The WO4 manual's "officers can block" line (Hyper attacks) supports the officer/soldier split only in part.

## What to take (for a sword moveset)

1. Give each string step a named branch: a charge (heavy) follow-up branching from steps 2 and 3 only, so the branch grammar is a table, not a special case. (WO4 manual: charge "opens varied follow-ups when chained from Normal Attacks.")
2. Make the branch set depend on enemy state, with the same Square-type string changing after a launcher or stun. (DW9 Flow Attack.)
3. Keep a launcher (Triangle-type) and a crowd/area clear as separate kinds, and make the launcher the only one that leaves an enemy airborne for a follow-up. (DW9 Trigger: R1+Triangle launch, R1+Square stun, R1+Cross knockdown.)
4. Allow a jump cancel out of selected recovery frames and a dodge cancel out of any recovery, with a dodge that is not blocked by holding it (the Warriors dodge rule as the wiki reports it; verify timing in-house).
5. Let a Musou-style special grant invincibility for its duration only, and no other move (WO4 manual).
6. Use a switch or swap to continue a string across two characters or tools, with a window only right after a charge or special (WO4 Switch Combo).
7. Scale hit-count bonuses at fixed thresholds (300 and 1,000 in WO4) so the count reads as a goal, not a hidden multiplier.
8. Sketch the blade's tip arc first and fit each step to it with a weight shift (CEDEC 2026 combo construction; PRESS, Japanese).
9. Stage the impact flash at the pose of the strike while the energy is delivered a step earlier, and have the blade's sweep carry past the body for showmanship within realism (CEDEC 2026; PRESS, Japanese).
10. Measure hitstop and launch on the desk per enemy weight class; no hitstop numbers are sourced here (UNSOURCED: the crowd/officer split is common Musou practice, not verified).
