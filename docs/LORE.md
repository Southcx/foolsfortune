# Fool's Fortune: the series bible

Who lives in this world, where things are, what things are called and why, and how it all sounds. Espada (Lore) keeps it; read it
before writing words a player will see. The owner builds the lore functionally: the frame first, details when a feature needs them.
Blanks stay blank.

**How firm:** unmarked is **canon** (the owner has ruled). *Proposed* waits on the owner. *Open* is a blank kept on purpose. Words
already in the build that this page does not name are provisional. Strings that live in code are not repeated here: the code is the
record (section 12 says where).

---

## 1. The frame

- **The Emocean**: the collective unconscious, a sea of raw thought and feeling, "a sea of salt made from tears". It is the
  atmosphere of pure Lachryma round every island, and dangerous to cross. **The world is a snowglobe**: an island holds the Emocean
  out by its owner's Will.
- **Islands of Ego**: where an identity is strong enough, an island precipitates out of the Emocean, coloured by what made it. Weaker
  egos gather round stronger ones.
- **Kaolin Anagami *is* the first island.** *Kaolin* is porcelain clay; an *anagama* is a tunnel kiln. **Anagami Island** is a 5 x 5
  grid of chunks: his plateau in the centre, **the Dunes** round the rocky outcrop.
- **The clay folk are fragments of Kaolin**, and the shares are uneven: the finer the artistry, the bigger the share, and the finer
  the shape. They know what they are, and they are a pseudo-hivemind: losing clapperjars costs the Prince nothing; break his favourite
  cups and he is annoyed. Unisex by construction; each identifies as it likes: write round pronouns unless a folk has chosen
  (Saggar "she", Raku "he"; Grog and Pip unset).
- **The Prince of Clay**, Kaolin's main avatar and truest picture: playful, experimental, aloof, detached, witty, haughty, a bit of a
  brat, wholly devoted to making. "He", femboy-coded: Lloyd de Saloum (*Dainana Ouji*) meets Tet (*No Game No Life*). A headpiece
  that is crown, top hat and kiln chimney at once, drip glaze under its rim. He lives in a chateau on the thumb of the hand tower.
- **The Pneuka Jar is his Magnum Opus**, made from divine inspiration from **the System**: a vessel that can house **Players**,
  people outside the game. (Spelled **Pneuka**, never Pneuma.)
- **The Vessoul** (*vessel* + *soul*): the player in the world, a soulspark from beyond in a vessel. One being in several forms, one
  design language: **the Pneuka Jar** (its true form), **the Courier** (the jar in humanoid form), **the god hand** (the same will
  reaching out while the body rests as the jar), **the ships** (sloop, frigate, tanker, destroyer, galleon). The Solar Skiff is a limb,
  not a vehicle. Player text says "you" and "your Pneuka Jar", never "the jar", "the vessel" or "the player".
- **Couriers**: every player is one. They help, clean up cogitohazards, map the Emocean, find islands, and carry goods across it.
  They resist excess Lachryma best. An NPC can become one, at great risk: no one lasts in the open Emocean without being a
  **Contractor** with a **Tulpa**.
- **Excess Lachryma** drives anyone else mad by degrees, then transfigures them into a Figment: mercury poisoning meets Mr Hyde.
- **Cogitohazard**: the umbrella for raw Lachryma's dangers and malicious Figments. **Figments** are hewn from an island's own psyche;
  **Egregores** spawn from the Emocean, authored by no one. Neither is good or bad by nature.
- **The System**: the game's code made a voice; all of us making the game. Flat, helpful, rare; it never jokes.
- **Lachryma is everything**: magic and feeling alike. **Lachrymite** is its solid form (the mineralogist's *-ite*, "of the stone":
  the stone of tears): a cube is a coin of it, a crystal a formation of it. Inert and safe, so it is money. The game divides into STORY
  and DEBUG.

### Wells and the town that was
- **A Well is a pocket of distortion**: rumination gone round until the Lachryma pools. Any feeling in excess, joy as much as grief:
  give Wells of elation as often as Wells of grief. A Well drifts, as rumination does. The Weir's Well is a place; a Well is a
  distortion. A **Cogitomap** (*cogito*, "I think": a map of a thought) charts a Well as it was on one game day.
- **The town that was** stood in the Dunes: jars and jugs and round-bellied pots, all talking at once. It dug for crude deeper than
  the Weir's Well to reach the rich seam; the shaft gave way and the sand came in (Old Grog's "before the sand came"). The Well got into
  its folk: some are still out there as slip jellies, mourning their kin where they burst. Grog stayed by the water. The showcase for
  Lachryma's downside; Couriers walk where the town could not.
- **"Touch nothing but the lamp"** is the rule of every Well: a memory changes each time it is handled. Grog's form: "Take what you
  came for, and nothing that is looking at you."

### Lachryma as crude
Set the condition (a feeling-made, mind-radioactive substance that behaves like petroleum) and let the consequences fall:
- **Crude is fossil feeling**: the deeper, the older, until it is a feeling nobody remembers having.
- **Drawing a Well down is working a feeling through.** A Well that runs dry is a mind that has healed; that is why Wells drift and
  Cogitomaps go stale. The moral axis of the economy: harvesting helps; a greedy driller wants the Well to keep filling. Intent, not
  alignment, decides which a Courier is.
- **Grades** by aspect, as crude grades sweet or sour; each island wants its own. The unit is the **cask**.
- **Liquid is unstable** (a bauble sours from cream to black); Lachrymite is inert. **Couriers are refineries** and the Pneuka Jar a
  containment vessel: only a Courier turns liquid to solid, so the currency is a refined product.
- **Ships by trade**: sloop (errands, letters), frigate (escort), galleon (refined cubes), destroyer (hunts Egregores), tanker (crude,
  double-hulled because it must be).
- **A spill is a cogitohazard**: Figments bloom where it lands. **The town that was** is a boomtown gone bust.
- **The resource curse**: the richest islands are the unhappiest minds. Stable minds are safer and poorer, chaotic ones riskier and
  richer. Cartels that hoard Wells: *open*, until a story needs them.

## 2. The King, the Queen, and their son

Two more Islands of Ego at the ends of the Law–Chaos line; Kaolin, who lives to make, between them. The arcana's three factions: Law,
Creation, Chaos. Concept art: `docs/ref/concept_king_magnus_margarite.png`, `docs/ref/concept_queen_entra_polearis.png`.

**The King: Magnus Ibrahim Manus. Pure iron Law.**
- *Magnus*, great; *Ibrahim*, "father of a multitude"; *Manus*, the hand (and in Roman law the head of a house's power): *magnum* and
  *manus*, the great hand, a rhyme with the hand tower and the god hand. M. I. M. points at Mímir, who keeps Odin's well of wisdom.
- **Margarite** (Greek *margarites*, a pearl): a sentinel lighthouse on the back of a cosmic whale. Nacre is Law's method: coat the
  grit that hurts, layer on even layer, until it is smooth. The lighthouse moves, because the whale swims. Its lantern is an hourglass
  with a flame inside; its gallery is black and white checks.
- **Avatar**: an old admiral, white-bearded, captain's cap, gold-trimmed coat, pipe, a fishing rod hung with a lantern lure, a gold
  pocket watch. His Figments (names *open*): a capped sailor, a white-hooded rifleman, a lamplighter, a dark golem with a lamp, the whale.
- **Wants order.** He hunts aberrants hard. The safe, stable, poor end of the economy.

**The Queen: Entra Polearis. Peak chaos, growth, abundance.**
- An exact anagram of *Astral Pioneer*. *Entra*: entropy, "enter". *Polearis* wears Polaris, the fixed star: a fine joke for chaos.
- **Entropolis** (*entropy* + *polis*, her name folded in): a blacklight metroplex. Tilted towers, magenta lightning down the streets
  like cracks, neon-green pools, a dark moon in a green blindfold. The green "head" at its foot is not one: it is toxic green Lachryma
  seeping from the sewers. **Overground**: flashy hedonism, every light on. **Underground**: twisted decay, where the party drains.
- **Rave culture, good and bad**: belonging, music, strangers who look after each other; and chasing the next high, the comedown, the
  burnt-out sinking below. The high is Lachryma itself, raw feeling taken for the rush, so no drug is ever named. The look (art
  direction only): New York dipped in LSD, then hit by a tsunami of DMT.
- **Avatar**: red hair, a witch's hat haloed by a dreamcatcher, glowing green eyes, smoke with red eyes, marionette strings to a small
  green puppet. Her Figments (names *open*): a horned one-eyed beast, a hooded visored silhouette, a red-patterned doll, a mushroom
  creature of eyes, a faceless runner with a club, a hooded pyramid with red drips.
- **Wants more.** The core feeling of chaos is **desire**. She is every feeling at once, pegged high, swinging without warning; the
  game shows it and never names it.

**The family.** The Prince is their son. The map is a circle: Entropolis on the rim, growing like wildfire; Magnus orbiting with her,
cleaning up; **Kaolin the centre, an oasis**. Anagami repeats it in small: an oasis in the Dunes, Old Grog fishing at its edge.
- **The Prince, explained**: the aloofness and the jokes are the shield a child grows between a mother who overflows and a father who
  burns the overflow. Porcelain is clay fired hotter than any other until it is fine, luminous and brittle: his mother's clay and his
  father's fire. The boy between a flood and a furnace built the one thing that carries raw feeling without breaking or burning.
- Never a villain parent and a victim child: both love him in their extreme ways, and the lesson is how he holds himself together, and
  when he lets the Courier help.

**The lighthouse and the toxic symbiosis.** Margarite's light keeps the **Leviathan-class Egregores** back, and it burns crude. So
Chaos digs it up cheap and Law buys it dear; the hauler lives in between. Letty's bounties are mostly the Queen's strays.
- **Magnus does it out of love**, calcified: duty, rules, a lamp lit on schedule. Nacre is that, calcium laid in thin loving layers until
  it looks like a wall. A lighthouse is a light left on for someone at sea; his hourglass measures her feelings by the grain. He never
  says her name: it is the one thing he cannot say without the shell cracking.
- **The Queen lets things go**; that someone gathers them up is, to her, what the world is for.
- **Toxic both ways**: a calmer Queen would put out his light; a King who stopped buying would leave her drowning. Neither can afford
  the other to get well, and their son lives in the middle. Growing up is noticing which one you are being, and choosing the oasis.
- **Aqua regia, the King's water**: the one acid that dissolves gold, and gold is what kintsugi mends with. It is Margarite's refined
  lamp fuel: what holds the Leviathans back also dissolves what was mended.
- **The middle**: the Pneuka Jar is the only safe vessel for crude and the only refinery. The Prince sells to neither side: every
  Courier both his parents need came out of his kiln.

## 3. Feeling

- **The five feelings, shown in order** (most positive to most negative): **Wonder, Mirth, Desire, Grief, Dread**, wherever they are
  listed. Their place on the Law–Chaos line is a separate thing. *Desire* was "hunger"; hunger is now desire in excess, a distortion.
- **The wheel** (after Plutchik): Lachryma is every feeling; the five are what Anagami's waters carry. The three Anagami lacks are the
  three a boy shields himself from with a joke: **Faith** (trust), **Gall** (disgust: bile and bitterness), **Fury** (anger). They pool
  in its Wells and fall freely elsewhere: faith over Margarite, gall and fury in Entropolis's underground. Expansion is a geography.
- **Agates** (agateware: two clays wedged, never blended): compound Lachryma where two moods overlap, rarer and worth more. Love (mirth
  and faith: the King's calcified kind), awe (dread and wonder), contempt (gall and fury: Letty's propaganda), remorse (grief and gall).
  In the weather: delight (mirth, wonder), hope (mirth, desire), guilt (mirth, dread), disappointment (wonder, grief), awe (wonder,
  dread), longing (desire, grief), worry (desire, dread; never "anxiety"), despair (grief, dread). Opposites cancel.
- **Rings** are adjectives, never new names (mild, basic, intense). Mirth's top is **elation**. The centre is **Prismatic**.
- **Emotional weather**: the sea of feeling settling onto a mind each game day. The mood is the island's ego's; **fair** (calm) is the
  glaze holding, which on Anagami is not proof that nothing is wrong. Wonder is **the aurora**; mirth **the fox's wedding** (a
  sunshower); desire **the wanting wind** (*want*: desire and lack at once); grief **the long rain**; dread **the pall**; faith **the
  halo**; gall **the miasma**; fury **the hail** (no lightning: nothing flickers). A Well has its own mood; the open Emocean's is *open*.
- **Grain** (always capitalised): a mind's temperament. A clay body has a grain, set before firing, worked with or against. Player
  words, both poles: **curious / wary**, **orderly / erratic**, **bold / shy**, **gentle / hostile**, **skittish / steady**; the OCEAN
  ids stay in code. Grain is climate, mood is weather: an island sets its Figments' mean Grain; an Egregore has the widest spread; the
  slip jellies carry the boomtown's (bold, erratic). Lines: "Grain: curious, bold, steady. Weak to: Illusion, Impact." · "You turn its
  Grain: skittish to steady." Kaolin's own Grain: *open*.
- **The five phases** *(proposed)*: wonder wood, mirth fire, desire earth, **grief metal, dread water** (the tradition's own pairing:
  grief to the lungs, fear to the kidneys). The generating cycle then runs in the shown order, Wonder → Mirth → Desire → Grief → Dread →
  Wonder, and that last step is awe. Overcoming: wonder checks desire, desire checks dread, dread checks mirth, mirth checks grief,
  grief checks wonder. Metal for grief is kintsugi's gold; water for dread is the deep the Leviathans rise from.

## 4. How a Courier drinks

- **The stones** set in the Courier (the kiln's STONES region) are where Lachryma enters: a pot breathes through its glaze, a Courier
  drinks through its stones. The set decides how much, how fast, which feeling, and where overflow goes. **The Maker's Stones** are the
  Prince's balanced choice; every other stone is a trade, its virtue from what old lapidaries believed: amethyst (*a-methystos*, "not
  drunk": slow and clear; in Entropolis a charm the good half of rave culture sells), citrine, moonstone, onyx, emerald, sapphire, ruby,
  diamond, opal. Blurbs are in `courier/vessel/glazes.js`. Look and function one choice: *open* (a glamour is the owner's call).
- **The words**: **the draught** is the feeling last drunk (a drink, and a draught under a door). A stone is **heady** when what you
  drink goes to your head. Too much and you are **brimming** (a vessel full past its brim; pushed toward Prismatic): "You are
  brimming." · "You settle." Never "drunk".
- **The Lachrymato Bottles** (the owner's name): a *lachrymatory* is an old tear bottle mourners filled. Worn on the upper back: a
  reserve the Soul Brush paints from and mops into. Glass: a broken shield can crack one.
- **Blots and blotlings** *(proposed; code says stain)*: spilled crude soaks in as a blot (a stain, and an inkblot a mind reads shapes
  into) and grows each game day; full-grown, it gives up a **blotling**. Mopping one is working a feeling through before it grows teeth.
  "You mop up a blot of grief crude." · "A blotling crawls out of a blot of dread crude."
- **Shrines**: a roadside shrine (a *hokora*), the size of a small kiln. Resting there is a refiring in small: whole again, pool full,
  mind settled. "You rest at the Shrine.", never "Game saved." None in Wells. Named *(proposed)* for what sits in the niche: **the
  Bisque Shrine** (by Saggar's kiln: the first firing), **the Lamp Shrine** (the Dunemaw's lip), **the Float Shrine** (Grog's pier:
  a float stays up whatever pulls), **the Pearl Shrine** (Margarite's dock: a wound made beautiful).
- **The Wake Whistle** *(proposed)*: a clay ocarina, a pot you play. A Well is going round and round; the way out is to *wake* (and a
  wake is the line a ship leaves).

## 5. Places

- **The workshop and the kiln**: Saggar's; the kiln is "her", and "everything in this workshop came out of her belly". "The workshop"
  is, in the end, Kaolin: clapperjars hold what Kaolin weeps.
- **The basement hub**: the movement lab, THE COURSE, THE SPINDLE, THE BRAID, THE MILL RACE, THE SIEGE. Testing grounds.
- **The Throwing Room**: off the Workshop's east wall, where aim and recoil are measured. A potter throws on the wheel; the Courier
  throws shots.
- **The Dunes and the Weir**: the oasis, the pier, the Weir's Well; the still centre.
- **The Gnomon**: the 150 m pale spire with the beam of light. Greek "the one who knows", a sundial's shadow-stick: the Dunes are its
  dial, the town told the game hour by its shadow, and it keeps the hours for nobody now. The Solar Skiffing trial races its shadow.
  The beam's meaning: *open*.
- **The Great Dunemaw**: a maw is a mouth that swallows. The pit where the town's dig fell in, and a Well because the island keeps going
  over it: *what if they had not dug so deep*. Kaolin swallows his stress behind a joke, and it pools here. Its mouth stands apart from
  the oasis, in a ring of fallen stones (a Well pools away from the still centre). Below: the forgotten pots (folk who did not turn,
  empty now), what the boom bought, and **the buried head**: the town's statue of the Prince, fallen face first, the three horns the
  points of his chimney-crown. Whether it is only a statue: *open*. **The nursery**: the first jellies were townsfolk; the brood were
  born of the slip: the town's grief, having children.
- **The Tithe**: Raku's console. Where the chests come from: *open*.
- **The title, THE FOOL'S PRECIPICE**: not a place; a metaphor for where the story has got to, changing as it goes.
- **The Spirit Garden**: a pocket dimension inside your Pneuka Jar, entered at a Shrine (the Pneuka Box is its shed). Its **moonflower**
  opens only at night, by the game hour (its root, datura, stays out of player text). The planetoids (`src/npc/realmnames.js`):
  **the Dantian** (the elixir field, where a cultivator keeps what they refine: a lake of your own Lachryma), **the Herb Terraces**,
  **the Athanor** (the alchemist's furnace at one slow heat, *al-tannur*, the oven; "the kiln" is Saggar's), **the Pavilions of Echoes**,
  **the Mulberry Grove** (the cocoon tree is a mulberry, as silk's is), **the Chimney** (where the Heavenly Kiln draws; the Prince's
  chimney-crown). Bought later: the Moonflower Moon, the Koi Pond, the Drill Yard, the Bone Bed (a layer of old bones: the fossils).
- **The Inner Realm**: your garden by its name, offered in neuralese words the Veritome teaches, drawn as runes: HEMA-LUNO (home,
  rest), KITH-HEMA, LUNO-DEO, STIL-DEO, EZA-LON, ROMI-LON, SIVA-LUNO (an oasis), HUSA-HEMA, AMI-HEMA, MOR-LUNO (where grief rests).
- **The Firings** *(names proposed)*: the soul refired, no ceiling. **Candling** (the last water driven out: never mud again),
  **Sinter** (grains fuse into one body), **Lustre** (the third firing), **Salt** (thrown in at the hottest: it stings, and it glazes),
  **Reduction** (starved of air, the colours change from inside), **Anagama** (the days-long wood firing; the island's own name). After
  that, numbers only: no potter has fired past an anagama. The tribulation is **the Heavenly Kiln**. The spirits' kinds: *open* until
  Dovina sets them.

  Wears a mask. Seven psychic tools on the belt. Achievements show on the body as kintsugi.

- **The names**: a *saggar* shields a pot in the kiln; *grog* is fired clay crushed back into new clay; *raku* is a firing; a *pip* is

- **Strawman**: Pip stitched it, the one thing in a workshop of clay that cannot shatter, made by the apprentice afraid of breaking
  things. A scarecrow, a training dummy, and the argument set up to be knocked down: it always stands back up, and the real argument is
  still out there. The heart charm is Pip's. The log uses its name with no article: "Strawman rocks back up."

**Seger, the Witness Cone** (commissions; stoneware; not yet in the world). Hermann Seger invented the pyrometric cone; a witness cone
slumps when the firing reaches its heat: it certifies the work, it does not do it. Tall, three-sided, pale, a number pressed in its side;
upright when unimpressed, slumped when the work was right. Exact, a little smug, kind underneath. Teaches persistence. Lines for
`talks.js`: "Ah. The Courier. {p:0.4}I'm Seger. I stand in the fire and say when it's done." · "Witnesses don't do the work.
{p:0.3}{small}We only make sure it was done properly.{/}" · "Seven in a row. {p:0.4}A cone that has seen seven firings and still stands
is either very good or very lucky." · "Broken. {p:0.4}Never mind. {slow}Every cone starts at zero.{/}" · "I've witnessed a thousand
firings. {p:0.4}Not his. {p:0.3}{small}They say the cones in his kiln melt flat from sheer admiration.{/}"

**Letty Marque, Contractor** (bounties; nacre, of Margarite; "she"). A *letter of marque* licensed lawful prize-taking: Letty (letter),
a *mark* (the target), a *marquee* (star billing, which she would like).
- **A zealot on a mission**, gung-ho about clearing the Emocean of aberrants. She takes the King's letter more literally than the King:
  he keeps the light lit to hold the deep things back; she goes out to finish them. Pirate-coded, a feathered tricorn.
- **Nacre**: shell-pale, a rainbow film, harder than clay; nacre coats the wrong thing until it is sealed away, which is her creed.
- **Poll**, her Tulpa: a paper parrot folded from every notice she closed. A parrot's name, a head count, a tax per head.
- **The voice: a war hawk itching for escalation**: long rolling sentences, each clause a step up the ladder; cheerful, never in doubt,
  never clipped. Kid-safe words: clear out, put right, take the fight to them, finish.
- **She points at Entropolis**, and **has never been there**. Her picture is propaganda from secondhand stories: it keeps the bad of
  both halves and drops the good, and she has never heard that anyone there is happy. Asked, "Well, no. Not yet." The Courier can go
  and see; what they find against what she says is her story's lesson.
- **The undertone**: the canon says no Figment is evil by nature, and she is sure they are; a bounty may be somebody's neighbour. Never
  a villain; the lesson that certainty and being right are different things. What a stray was, and whether she learns it: *open*.

## 9. Tone and voice

- **Light overtones, dark undertones.** Safe for a child, layered for when they are older. No blood, no swearing; the dark is implied.
- **The stories teach growing up**: hardship, resilience, persistence. Nobody is lectured; the lesson is what the character does next.
- **Density over length**: a line carries two or three meanings. **Names are their own destiny**: research every root.
- **Set the initial conditions and let the consequences follow.**
- **The System and the log are clean and robotic**: subject, verb, object, number. "Your Pneuka Jar breaks." No exclamation, no
  metaphor. The log is a combat log: "you" for the Courier, third person for the rest, one sentence an event (FFXI). `Name : words`.
  The System: "Notice. ..." / "Warning. ...".
- **UI text is ASD-STE100**: short sentences, one instruction each, active, present, one word one meaning, no idioms. The folk, the
  Arcana and item flavour keep their style, trimmed.
- **Habits**: British spelling. Plain, wry, understated ("It does up nothing. It has been on a great many coats."). Short sentences
  with one turn in them. The folk show feeling with their bodies, never stage directions. The Courier's own hand (the Arcana) is first
  person, dry. Word pools: the potter's trade, the sea, the tarot, board games, the number five. Compound concrete names (clapperjar,
  Sondelass, Veritome, Dreamvane, Crucibelle, Lockheart). The Prince's voice is the key to the island's; every pot is a smaller piece.
- **The siblings' voices** are voice cards in `src/coop/personas.js`: five built to differ on every axis (length, punctuation,
  register, what comes first, how a line ends), with the house voice's tells banned for all five.
- **No clinical words** in player text: show mania, worry, despair; never name them.

  word **beside a picture of what it does** (a bilingual, the Rosetta Stone's trick at the size of a shard), and finding it adds that
