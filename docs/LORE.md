# Fool's Fortune: the series bible

Who lives in this world, where things are, what has happened, what things are called, and how it all sounds. Espada (Lore) keeps
this page. Other divisions read it before writing words a player will see, and ask Espada (through the owner) for anything it does
not answer yet.

The owner builds the lore **functionally and directionally**: the frame first, the details when a feature needs them. This page
follows suit. It records what has been ruled, points the rest in a direction, and leaves blanks blank rather than fill them.

How firm each thing is:

- **Ruled**: the owner has said so. Build on it.
- **Owner's notes**: from the owner's world web and trope compendium (linked at the foot). They give the direction, and may change.
- **In game**: words already in the build (the folk's lines, the log, the Veritome, examine lines). They were written early, from
  partial information, so Petra could build features. **They are provisional, not canon**, and get rewritten to fit the frame.
- **Design**: the maker's notes in the repo (README, `docs/OST.md`, module headers).
- **Open**: waiting on the owner (section 11).

## 1. The frame (ruled)

**The Emocean.** The collective unconscious, a sea of raw thought and emotion: "a sea of salt made from tears". It is a dangerous
place to cross. *(Ruled: name and role. Owner's notes: the details.)*

**Islands of Ego.** Where an identity is strong enough, an island precipitates out of the Emocean, coloured by the emotions and
events that made it. Weaker egos gather around stronger ones and form societies. *(Ruled / Owner's notes)*

**Kaolin Anagami *is* the island we start on.** It is his island of ego, the first place, before the Courier goes out into the
greater Emocean. (*Kaolin* is porcelain clay; an *anagama* is a tunnel kiln.) *(Ruled)*

**The clay folk are aspects of his psyche, and the shares are uneven.** Every pot person is a fragment of Kaolin Anagami. The
finer its artistry, the bigger its share of him. *(Ruled)*

**The Prince of Clay** is the most powerful of them, the main avatar of Kaolin Anagami and the truest picture of him: his
personality, mannerisms, proclivities and philosophy. He is **playful, experimental, aloof, detached, witty, haughty, something
of a brat, and wholly devoted to the act of creation.** In the concept art he stands tall and symmetrical, eyes lowered, with a
faint smile, under a headpiece that reads as crown, top hat and kiln chimney at once, with molten drip glaze just under its rim.
He lives in a chateau on the thumb of the hand tower. *(Ruled: who he is and what he is like. Owner's notes: the look and the
chateau.)*

**The Pneuka Jar is his Magnum Opus.** Divine inspiration from **the System** came to him, and from it he made the Pneuka Jar: a
vessel able to house **Players**, people like us outside the game world. *(Ruled)* (The spelling is **Pneuka**. *Pneuma* was an
early mix-up.)

**The Courier is the Pneuka Jar in humanoid form.** Couriers go out and enact their will on the world, or worlds:
- helping with tasks,
- cleaning up **cogitohazards**,
- mapping the Emocean,
- finding new Islands of Ego,
- carrying goods and services across the Emocean, which is itself dangerous. *(Ruled)*

**Couriers are less affected by excess Lachryma**, by default. Too much Lachryma does harm, and Couriers resist it best. *(Ruled)*

**From Round 38** (passed on by Petra in `docs/HANDOFFS.md`, from the owner) *(Ruled)*:
- **Lachryma is everything**: the stuff of magic and of emotion alike.
- **Cubes are Lachryma made solid.**
- The long-term sink is an **Internal Shrine Garden**, a pocket dimension inside the vessel.
- The game divides into **STORY** and **DEBUG**.

### How the frame reads the game we have
- **"The workshop" is Kaolin's island**, and in the end Kaolin himself. So the in-game line that clapperjars hold "what the workshop
  weeps" turns out to be literal: they hold what Kaolin weeps.
- **The four answers about where Lachryma comes from collapse into one.** It is emotional and cognitive output: the world's
  tears (the Emocean is a sea of them), the tide bringing it up "from somewhere deep" (the Emocean under the island), what the mind
  can spare (the Psygun), what the workshop weeps (Kaolin).
- **Saggar's hint was true.** "Even you, I shouldn't wonder": the Courier did come out of the kiln. She is the Prince's opus.
- **The god hand** fits the owner's notes. A player can leave the vessel as a cursor, most likely a disembodied hand, and the
  Prince's own hand tower echoes it: two makers' hands. Becoming the Pneuka Jar while the hand works is the player stepping out of
  the humanoid form into the jar it really is.

## 2. The concept art

### The island (Kaolin Anagami's island of ego)
A floating island in an empty grey field, in the game's clay ladder: oxblood, brick, terracotta, cream. From left to right:
- **A torii gate** at the lower left: the arrival. The compendium's first stop is "arrival beach and torii gate". Further left, a
  plank jetty runs out into the void with a small craft at its end.
- **Bottle-shaped towers**, tall spouted vessels and a terraced cluster of jars and pots, the size of buildings: the kiln
  workshops and the pot people's quarters.
- **Slip veins**: cream paths branching across the whole island like a circulatory system, gathering into cream pools. Slip
  drips off the island's underside in long strands. The owner's notes say the slip is Kaolin's essence, it powers the
  constructs, and the slip of a broken pot flows home.
- **The hand tower**: a giant clay hand rising from the island, fingers up. A window in its palm holds a pale cube, the core,
  pouring slip down into a splash. A small building with a spire sits on the thumb: the Prince's chateau.
- **A whirlpool** in a lower basin, cream and brown: perhaps where a Well opens (the compendium's sixth stop is the first Well,
  entered through the palm).
- **A domed observatory** on a striped column at the right, its telescope raised: the compendium's observatory (Divination, the
  map, the first mote hunt).
- **A small figure on a plinth** at the island's centre, ringed by paths.
- **Gold swallow shapes** wheeling round the island, and terracotta clouds.

### The folk (the Prince's fragments, by size)
A line-up in silhouette. Read from the rule that artistry is share, it is a ladder:
- **The Prince of Clay**, at the lower centre: the chimney-crown with its drip, eyes lowered, a high ruff, puffed sleeves,
  ball-jointed arms open in a showman's gesture, a bell of a skirt, long legs. The tallest and the most finished.
- **Six tall, ornate pot people** around him, each a full figure with spiral slip-trail decoration:
  - a tall veiled figure with long streaming ribbons, top left;
  - a slender one with long hanging sleeves of swirl-work;
  - a witch-hatted one, its wide brim pierced by a finial, in a bell-shaped robe, with claw hands;
  - a stout one with two round coils of headdress;
  - a vast round-bodied knight with a barred visor and spiral pauldrons;
  - a great armoured golem with a grille of teeth.
- **Small ones**: a little round one with a pom on its head among the tall figures, and along the foot a row of household
  vessels on legs, a heron-necked bottle, a jug, a teapot whose lid has popped up, a kettle, a tiny jar. All have glowing eyes.
  These are the clapperjars' kin, the smallest shares.

The current folk (Saggar, Pip, Grog, Raku) are nowhere in it by name. Which of these figures they are, if any, is open.

## 3. Directions from the owner's notes

None of this is ruled yet, but writing should lean this way. *(Owner's notes)*

- **The spine**: mind, ego and attention are the most valuable things there are, and the whole world argues it through games. Play
  is a condition of sentience. The people of the world found that play is the most efficient interface to reality, so games are
  cosmic law (hence the checkerboards, pawns, dice and cards).
- **Fiction and mechanics make one claim**: Lachryma answers to mental acuity, in the world and in the player.
- **Intent, not alignment**: nothing is good or evil in itself. Light/Dark and Law/Chaos are never good/evil.
- **Solid and liquid** is the master motif: Law and Chaos, Stoic and Prismatic, cubes and liquid Lachryma, fired clay and wet slip.
- **East and West** are two ways of applying the same power. The East lets outside forces in (cultivation, hosting a spirit, living
  with gods). The West imposes its will (binding by contract, curses, technology). Both have every domain; what differs is the
  manner. Possession is the model: hosting a spirit versus riding another mind.
- **Beings**: Players (*Vessouls*: soulsparks from beyond, reaching in through what looks like a browser, the only ones who turn
  liquid Lachryma solid); **Figments** (tsukumogami-like thought-constructs with crystallised cores); **Egregores** (feral
  thought-forms authored by no one and fed by everyone, the antagonistic force); **Tulpas** (authored with care); Contractors.
- **The divinity is dual**: Pirates and Witches are central. Whether they are gods, cultures or ways of living is open.
- **Wells**: distortions where Lachryma is harvested. Collective Wells are shared emotion, Individual Mindscapes one mind.
- **Lachryma** is Latin for tears. It looks black, oily, ultraviolet and iridescent, and pops against terracotta and paper.
  Perhaps it smells of salt and hot metal.
- **The Fool's Journey**: the title. Chasing what others call reckless, taking the one-in-a-million shot. The fortune is the
  pursuit itself. The player's arc runs from 0 The Fool to XXI The World.

## 4. Tone and voice

Habits the text already has, kept until the owner says otherwise.

- **British spelling**: colour, favourite, grey.
- **Plain, wry, understated.** Funny by being flat about strange things: "It does up nothing. It has been on a great many coats."
- **Short sentences with one turn in them**: "A lamp made of what the sea had finished with."
- **The log is a combat log**: second person for the Courier's deeds, third person for everyone else's, one sentence an event,
  FFXI-style. `Name : words` for speech.
- **The System speaks flatly, helpfully, in an isekai manner**: "Notice. ..." / "Warning. ...". It never jokes.
- **The Courier's own hand** (the Arcana pages) is first person, reflective, dry.
- **The folk show feeling with their bodies**, never with stage directions in the words.
- **Word pools**: the potter's trade (slip, bisque, glaze, saggar, raku, tenmoku, kintsugi), the sea and the tide, the tarot,
  board games, the number five.
- **Things have compound, concrete names**: clapperjar, slip jelly, Sondelass, Veritome, Dreamvane, Crucibelle, Lockheart.
- **The Prince's voice**, when it is written, is the key to the island's: witty, haughty, playful, a little bratty, devoted to the
  making. Every pot person is a smaller piece of that voice.

## 6. People

### Kaolin Anagami and the Prince of Clay
See section 1. Two faces, one ego: the island is Kaolin Anagami, and the Prince of Clay is his main avatar on it. *(Ruled)* Whether
the folk call the Prince "Kaolin" is open.

### The Courier
- The Pneuka Jar in humanoid form, the Prince's magnum opus, inhabited by a Player. *(Ruled)*
- **She / her.** *(In game, everywhere)*
- Wears a mask (the vessel has a MASK region, "the face it shows"). *(Design)*
- Carries the psychic tools on her belt: seven, four made (the Psygun, the Sondelass, the Soul Brush, the Veritome; the Dreamvane,
  the Crucibelle, the Lockheart to come). In the owner's notes each tool is a genre and a domain. *(In game / Owner's notes)*
- Her achievements show on her body as kintsugi, gold in the seams. *(Design)*

## 8. Things

- **Lachryma**: everything (ruled). Forms in game: baubles (cream, oxidising to black), liquid (black, oil-film), cubes (solid, the
  currency). The owner's notes: liquid is volatile mana, solid is currency and crafting, refined is Soul Alchemy's fuel.
- **The System**: the source of the divine inspiration behind the Pneuka Jar *(Ruled)*. In game it announces skills, titles,
  analyses and warnings.
- **Slip**: liquid clay; on the island, Kaolin's essence *(Owner's notes)*. The Soul Brush writes with it.
- **Kintsugi**: breakage mended in gold, a scar made beautiful.
- **Chests, curios, lures, tools, standings**: as in the game (`src/treasure.js`, `src/angling/lures.js`, `src/pneuka/items.js`,
  `src/achievements.js`). The curios are almost all of the sea: a fit for things fished out of the Emocean.

## 11. Open questions for the owner

### Answered by the frame
Of the first edition's thirteen questions, **six are answered** (what the world is, who the Courier is, who the god hand is, East
and West, the folk as clapperjars, the tarot and the five), **three in part** (what Lachryma is: answered, but whose it is and what the
Tithe pays into are not; what the System is: the giver of inspiration, but still a mystery; Saggar's old master: very likely the
Prince, to confirm), and **four not at all** (the dunes and the lost town, folk pronouns, the Spire, Pip's name).

### Open now, the ones that block writing first
1. **Where are the dunes?** The desert, the Weir and its well, the tides and the Spire: are they on Kaolin's island (below the
   workshop, in a Well, inside the hand?), the first piece of the Emocean, or another island? Grog's lost town and the sea that
   went away hang on this.
2. **Do the folk know what they are?** Does Saggar know she is a piece of Kaolin? Does any folk know the Prince is him?
3. **One Courier or many?** The Pneuka Jar is *the* magnum opus; are all Couriers made by the Prince, copies of the first, or made
   elsewhere later? Is "Courier" a kind (what the jar becomes) or a trade (a guild, a job)?
4. **The Prince and Kaolin, in speech.** Is the avatar called the Prince of Clay, the Clay Prince, Kaolin, or all three by
   different folk?
5. **Which part of Kaolin is each folk?** Kept open: a rule for it (one feeling each? one habit each?) would let me rewrite the
   four and write the rest of the line-up.
6. **What is a cogitohazard, to a writer?** The owner's Egregores and Figments, something else, or the umbrella word for both? Is
   the slip jelly one?
7. **What does excess Lachryma do** to those who are not Couriers? (The kiln making clapperjars when it is "too full" and the chests
   going prismatic would be the island's symptoms.)
8. **What is the System?** A god, the game itself, one of the dual divinities, or none of these? Does it speak to anyone but Couriers?
9. **Folk pronouns**: is the Prince "he", and do his fragments share it, or each choose?
10. **Does breaking a pot hurt Kaolin?** The game breaks a great many. (The owner's own open question about masterpieces.)
11. **The title's precipice**: is the hill on Kaolin's island, and is stepping off it the Courier going out into the Emocean?
12. **Pip's name**, and **Saggar's old master** (the Prince?).
